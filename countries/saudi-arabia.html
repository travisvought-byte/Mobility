Disability resources in Saudi Arabia

In danger or in crisis right now?

In Saudi Arabia, call 997 (Ambulance (Saudi Red Crescent). For the police, call 999; for civil defence, call 998).

Crisis and someone to talk to

Psychological Consultation Center call line

Ministry of Health

Connects callers anywhere in the Kingdom with mental health professionals. It is not open around the clock.

Next step: Call 920033360, 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. (Saturdays 1:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m.). In an emergency, call 997. The Ministry's Qareboon app offers mental health counselling by text.

Disability payments and benefits

Monthly financial aid, assistive devices and daily care for persons with disabilities

Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development

Disabled people registered with the Ministry whose disability has been assessed can apply for a monthly financial aid payment, for assistive devices, and for daily social care. You can also ask for your disability assessment to be updated, appeal if your aid is stopped, and university students with disabilities can apply for a grant.

Next step: Apply through the Ministry's e-services (eservices.hrsd.gov.sa). The Ministry's page is in Arabic.

Equipment, home changes and getting around

Traffic facilities card and transport fare discount card

Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development

The traffic facilities card lets a disabled person enter public facility car parks and use reserved disabled spaces across the Kingdom. The fare discount card gives Saudi disabled people and their companions 50% off government transport.

Next step: Apply on the national portal my.gov.sa: service 12346 for the traffic facilities card and service 12345 for the fare discount card. The Ministry's page is in Arabic.
