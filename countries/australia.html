Disability resources in Australia

In danger or in crisis right now?

In Australia, call 000 (Police, fire and ambulance).

Lifeline crisis support: 13 11 14.

Calling without your voice? Deaf, hard of hearing or speech disability: use the National Relay Service. Call 133 677 (TTY or voice) or 1300 555 727 (Speak and Listen) and ask for the number you need.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Lifeline crisis support

Lifeline Australia

Free, confidential crisis support any hour for anyone in Australia who feels overwhelmed, is in emotional distress or is thinking about suicide.

Next step: Call, text 0477 13 11 14, or chat online. Through TIS on 131 450 you can ask for Lifeline in your language.

Where to start

Disability Gateway

Australian Government Department of Social Services

A free, Australia-wide information line for people with disability, families and carers. A real person helps you find services for income, equipment, housing, transport, work, health and rights, and can transfer you to counselling or advocacy. Anyone can call, whether or not they are on the NDIS.

Next step: Call Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm (AEST/AEDT). For another language, call TIS on 131 450 and ask for the Disability Gateway.

Disability payments and benefits

National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS)

National Disability Insurance Agency

The national scheme that funds supports for people with permanent and significant disability. The NDIS can help you apply, or connect you with a local partner who will.

Next step: Call the participant line Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm local time, or use the webchat. First Nations Connect: 1800 411 640.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Australian Human Rights Commission: disability discrimination complaints

Australian Human Rights Commission

Looks into and helps resolve complaints about disability discrimination under the Disability Discrimination Act. Making a complaint is free, and someone such as an advocate can complain for you.

Next step: Call the National Information Service if you are not sure you can complain, or lodge a complaint online. Through the National Relay Service, ask to be connected to the Commission. For an interpreter, call TIS on 131 450.
