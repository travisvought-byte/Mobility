Crisis and someone to talk to

Hotline 116 006

Listed by the 112 emergency service

A nationwide hotline for victims of violence against women and domestic violence. It is free, anonymous and open 24 hours a day, and answers in Georgian, English, Russian, Azerbaijani, Turkish, Armenian, Arabic and Persian. It is not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 116 006 at any hour. In an emergency, call 112.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Public Defender of Georgia

Public Defender (Ombudsman) of Georgia

The Public Defender examines complaints from people who consider themselves victims of discrimination, and has a Department of Protection of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities. It also monitors the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities.

Next step: Call the 24-hour hotline 1481, email info@ombudsman.ge, or send the complaint form to Irakli Paghava Street N6, Avlabari, Tbilisi 0144 or a regional office. The office is open Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00.
