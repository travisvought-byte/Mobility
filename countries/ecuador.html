Crisis and someone to talk to

Mental health line 171, option 6

Ministerio de Salud Pública (MSP)

The health ministry’s mental health line, answered by specialist staff every day including holidays, from 7:00 to 20:00. It is not open around the clock; at other times, call the emergency number.

Next step: Call 171 and choose option 6, between 7:00 and 20:00. In Spanish.

Where to start

Disability certificate (calificación de discapacidad)

Ministerio de Salud Pública (MSP)

The free medical, psychological and social assessment that certifies a disability of 30% or more, for Ecuadorians and foreigners living in Ecuador. It opens the door to disability benefits and aids, and your ID card can then show your disability at no cost.

Next step: After a clinical assessment, the health professional sets your appointment at an MSP health centre. Bring the signed application, your ID or passport and reports from authorised professionals. Deaf people can book by video with sign-language help: call 171, option 4, 8:00 to 14:00. In Spanish.

Disability payments and benefits

Joaquín Gallegos Lara benefit (Bono Joaquín Gallegos Lara)

Government of Ecuador (social development ministry)

A monthly payment of 240 dollars, made to the person who cares for someone with a severe disability (intellectual or psychosocial 65% or more, physical 75% or more), a catastrophic or rare illness, or a child with HIV, who lives in critical economic hardship or extreme poverty.

Next step: Take the beneficiary’s ID, a recent utility bill, a sketch of how to find the home and the disability certificate to your nearest district office, weekdays 8:00 to 16:30. It is free. In Spanish.

Equipment, home changes and getting around

Wheelchairs and other aids (ayudas técnicas)

Ministerio de Salud Pública (MSP)

Free wheelchairs, canes, prostheses, orthoses and other aids for people with a certified disability who need them, after a prescription from an MSP health professional. A home visit is possible if you cannot travel.

Next step: Call 171, option 4, and say it is for an aid prescription. Go to your appointment, then collect the aid from the provincial office. In Spanish.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

National Council for Disability Equality (CONADIS)

Consejo Nacional para la Igualdad de Discapacidades

Free advice and guidance when the rights of a disabled person are threatened or violated, for disabled people, their families and anyone in Ecuador.

Next step: Use the online form, email informacion@consejodiscapacidades.gob.ec, or visit the Quito office or a provincial office, weekdays 8:00 to 16:30. In Spanish.
