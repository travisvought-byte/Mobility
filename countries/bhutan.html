Disability resources in Bhutan

Where to start

PEMA mental health services

PEMA

PEMA is Bhutan's national agency for mental health and child protection. It supports anyone with mental health concerns, runs recovery support groups, skills development and job placement, and is planning interim protection shelters.

Next step: Contact PEMA through its website.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Disabled People's Organization of Bhutan (DPOB)

Disabled People's Organization of Bhutan (formerly Disabled Persons' Association of Bhutan)

Bhutan's national organization run by persons with disabilities; it registers members and runs advocacy, health, education, skills, community and Deaf programmes.

Next step: Register as a member or contact the office in Thimphu by phone or email.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Helpline 1098 for women and children

PEMA

Bhutan's national helpline for women and children, open 24 hours a day, seven days a week, and free. It is run by PEMA, the state agency for mental health and child protection. It is not a general adult mental health line.

Next step: Call 1098 at any hour. In an emergency, call 112 for an ambulance or 113 for the police.
