Crisis and someone to talk to

Child Helpline 111

Listed by UNICEF Kyrgyzstan

A helpline where children, teenagers and parents can get psychological support and counselling, including advice on staying safe online; UNICEF also points parents and caregivers facing difficulties to it. Its hours and cost are not stated on UNICEF’s pages, which date from 2017 and 2019. It is a children’s line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 111. In an emergency, call 103 (ambulance) or 102 (police).

Disability payments and benefits

Lower income tax for persons with disabilities

Ministry of Labor, Social Protection and Migration

Since 2024, income tax for persons with disabilities in groups I and II is 5% instead of 10%, and the benefit was extended to people with hearing impairments in group III. Employers who hire persons with disabilities also get lower Social Fund contributions. A Council on the Affairs of Persons with Disabilities has been in place since 2020.

Next step: Ask your employer or the tax service to apply the lower rate, with your disability group certificate.
