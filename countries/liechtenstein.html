Disability payments and benefits

Invalidenversicherung (IV, disability insurance)

Liechtensteinische AHV-IV-FAK-Anstalten

Disability insurance for everyone who lives or works in Liechtenstein. It pays for help getting into or back into work (career advice, job placement, retraining, with a daily allowance), aids such as wheelchairs, hearing aids, prostheses and vehicles, a pension (quarter, half or full, from 40% disability) when work integration cannot succeed, and a helplessness allowance for people who constantly need help from others.

Next step: Register with the AHV-IV-FAK-Anstalten, Gerberweg 2, Vaduz, before any integration measure starts. Forms are at ahv.li, at the Anstalten and at municipal offices.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Liechtensteiner Behinderten-Verband (LBV) and its Büro für die Gleichstellung von Menschen mit Behinderungen (Office for the Equality of People with Disabilities)

Liechtensteiner Behinderten-Verband

The national disability association hosts the equality office that is the contact point for official matters such as grants and complaints; the association also helps with legal and insurance matters, building advice, wheelchair transport and financial assistance.

Next step: Call or email the LBV office in Schaan (Wiesengass 17), Monday to Friday 8:00-11:30 and 13:30-16:00.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Die Dargebotene Hand 143

Die Dargebotene Hand

The emotional support line that Liechtenstein's Office of Social Services lists for people in Liechtenstein. The Canton of Zurich describes it as open around the clock, completely anonymous, and also reachable by chat and email. Young people can call 147, the helpline for children and adolescents.

Next step: Call 143 at any hour. In an emergency, call 112 or 144 for an ambulance.
