Disability payments and benefits

Invalidity Benefit

Social Security Board

A monthly pension from the Social Security Board for insured people under 60 who are medically certified as permanently unable to do any work because of illness, after 26 weeks of sickness. It runs from $47 to $288 a week. People who have paid at least 26 contributions but do not qualify for the pension can get a one-time Invalidity Grant.

Next step: Fill in form INV1 and send it with a medical certificate from a registered Belize doctor, your Social Security card, passport or birth certificate and bank details, within 13 weeks of the 26th week of illness. A Social Security medical board will assess you.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

National Commission for Persons with Disabilities

Ministry of Human Development, Family Support and Gender Affairs

Launched in September 2026 under the Disabilities Act, 2024, which protects the rights of persons with disabilities in education, employment, health care and accessibility and against discrimination. The commission brings together government, civil society, labour and the private sector to put the Act into practice, chaired by the Chief Executive Officer of the Ministry of Human Development, Family Support and Gender Affairs.

Next step: Contact the Ministry of Human Development, Family Support and Gender Affairs about the commission and your rights under the Disabilities Act.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Ministry of Health and Wellness hotline 0-800-MOH-CARE

Ministry of Health and Wellness

The Ministry of Health and Wellness hotline. In its suicide prevention message the ministry told anyone who needs help to call it, and it was still the ministry's public hotline in May 2026. It is a general health hotline, not a dedicated crisis line, and the ministry does not state its hours.

Next step: Call 0-800-664-2273 (0-800-MOH-CARE). For an appointment, the mental health clinics at public hospitals also take patients. In an emergency, call the emergency number at the top of this page.
