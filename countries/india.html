Crisis and someone to talk to

Tele-MANAS mental health helpline

Ministry of Health and Family Welfare

Free mental health support by phone any hour, from the national tele-mental health programme, in many Indian languages.

Next step: Dial 14416, or 1-800-891-4416.

Where to start

Disability certificate and UDID card

Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities

One national disability certificate and Unique Disability ID card, issued under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016. Many schemes ask for it, including free or low-cost aids and devices under ADIP.

Next step: Apply free on the Swavlamban card portal: register, choose a government hospital in your district, and attend the medical assessment there. You can track your application on the same site.

Equipment, home changes and getting around

ADIP scheme: aids and assistive devices

Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities

Government help to buy or be fitted with aids and assistive devices, given through ALIMCO, the National Institutes, Composite Regional Centres, District Disability Rehabilitation Centres and other implementing agencies. A UDID card is required.

Next step: Apply through one of those agencies or register on the ADIP portal. For replacement or repair of a device from ALIMCO, call its toll-free line.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Chief Commissioner for Persons with Disabilities

Office of the Chief Commissioner for Persons with Disabilities (Divyangjan)

Takes complaints when a person with a disability is denied rights under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016, for example by a government body or employer. Each state also has a State Commissioner.

Next step: Send a written complaint by email or post, or bring it in person. Say who you are, what happened, where, and which organization it was.
