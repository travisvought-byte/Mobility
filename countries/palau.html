Crisis and someone to talk to

Division of Behavioral Health

Ministry of Health and Human Services

The Ministry of Health and Human Services' behavioral health division, which covers mental health. It also answers on 488-4573 and 488-1907. Its hours are not stated, so it may answer only in working hours.

Next step: Call 488-4572. In an emergency, call the emergency number at the top of this page or the hospital emergency room on 488-2558.

Disability payments and benefits

Palau Severely Disabled Assistance Fund

Ministry of Health and Human Services

A monthly payment for people with severe disabilities. From June 1, 2024 it is $250 a month for people who are homebound and $200 a month for wheelchair users and blind people who meet the criteria; 374 people, including 101 children, received it in August 2024. The ministry also issues a disability card that gives free consultations, treatment and medicine. Its Disability Unit is the government's disability focal point.

Next step: Ask the Ministry of Health and Human Services' Bureau of Public Health on 488-2552.
