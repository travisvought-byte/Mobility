Where to start

Disability identification card

Higher Council for the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (HCD)

The HCD issues the disability ID card. After it reworked the system, applications go through an online portal on the Sanad app and the HCD website, the Jordan News Agency reported in August 2026, while the council cleared a backlog; the urgent cases it named involved health insurance cover, university admission and domestic worker permit renewals. The council said it was setting up a hotline for questions but gave no number.

Next step: Apply online through the Sanad app or the HCD website.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

المركز الوطني لحقوق الإنسان (National Centre for Human Rights, NCHR)

National Centre for Human Rights, Jordan

Jordan's national human rights institution; it receives human rights complaints from all residents of Jordan.

Next step: Submit a complaint on the NCHR online complaint platform, or call or email the Centre.

المجلس الأعلى لحقوق الأشخاص ذوي الإعاقة (Higher Council for the Rights of Persons with Disabilities, HCD)

Higher Council for the Rights of Persons with Disabilities

Statutory council under Law No. 20 of 2017 that promotes the rights of persons with disabilities in Jordan, monitors whether government complies with the law, and gives accessibility and inclusion advice.

Next step: See the Council's website for its work and reports.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Family Protection Department

Listed by UNHCR

The department that responds to violence in the family and to children at risk. UNHCR lists it with a WhatsApp number and says all the services it lists are free; it does not give hours for the WhatsApp line. It is a violence service reached through the emergency number, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call the unified emergency number and ask for Family Protection, or send a WhatsApp message to +962 790 193 193. Free.
