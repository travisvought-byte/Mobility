Disability payments and benefits

Novčana naknada za pomoć i negu drugog lica (Attendance allowance)

Republički fond za penzijsko i invalidsko osiguranje (PIO Fund)

A monthly cash allowance for insured people and pensioners who need another person's help with basic daily needs, such as blind people, people who cannot move around, eat or dress without help, and people on dialysis; it does not depend on income.

Next step: Use the application form from the PIO Fund website's forms section; the Fund's medical body assesses the need for care.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Commissioner for Protection of Equality

An independent body that examines discrimination complaints, including discrimination because of disability or health condition. It can propose mediation, issue recommendations and warnings, and go to court with your consent. The procedure is free.

Next step: Send a written, signed complaint saying who was discriminated against, how, by whom, and what evidence there is, by email to poverenik@ravnopravnost.gov.rs. Anonymous complaints are not accepted. A human rights organisation can file for you with your consent.

Crisis and someone to talk to

National Suicide Prevention SOS Line

A free line for people thinking about suicide or in crisis, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Next step: Call 0800 309 309, at any hour. It is free. UNDP also lists Centre SRCE on 0800 300 303, every day 14:00 to 23:00. In an emergency, call 194 for an ambulance or 192 for the police.
