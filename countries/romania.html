ROMANIA · DISABILITY RESOURCES

Disability resources in Romania

In danger or in crisis right now?

In Romania, call 112 (The single emergency number for police, fire, ambulance, SMURD and gendarmerie, free from fixed and mobile phones. Calls are also taken in the main international languages).

TelVerde Antisuicid: 0800 801 200.

Calling without your voice? Deaf or speech-impaired: if you have registered in advance with the national 112 system, call 112 from your registered mobile and then report the emergency by free text message to 113.

Crisis and someone to talk to

TelVerde Antisuicid

Alianța Română de Prevenție a Suicidului (ARPS)

A free counselling line for adults at risk of suicide, run by a non-profit. It is not open around the clock; at other times, call the emergency number.

Next step: Call 0800 801 200, free, on Friday, Saturday and Sunday from 19:00 to 07:00.

Where to start

Disability certificate (certificat de încadrare în grad de handicap)

ANPD and the DGASPC of your county or Bucharest sector

The assessment that sets your degree of disability and opens the payments and facilities below. The assessment can be done at home if you cannot travel.

Next step: Send your file to the DGASPC where you live, in person, by registered post or by email, or through your town hall: the application form, ID, a social inquiry from the town hall, proof of income, a current report from your specialist and, the first time, a letter from your family doctor. You are assessed within 60 days and the certificate is posted to you. In Romanian.

Disability payments and benefits

Monthly allowance and complementary personal budget

ANPD (Autoritatea Națională pentru Protecția Drepturilor Persoanelor cu Dizabilități)

Adults with a severe (grav) degree get a monthly allowance of 419 lei plus a complementary personal budget of 180 lei; a marked (accentuat) degree brings 317 lei plus 132 lei; a medium degree brings a personal budget of 72 lei. Income does not affect them. Severe and marked degrees also bring free urban public transport and free intercity trips each year, and parking is free in spaces reserved for disabled people.

Next step: These rights come with the disability certificate above. In Romanian.
