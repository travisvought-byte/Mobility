Disability payments and benefits

People's Benefit Programme

Government of Antigua and Barbuda (Community Development Department)

A monthly benefit, running since 2009, for people with disabilities and people in economically vulnerable households (household income below about US$300 a month). In 2018 it was XCD 215 a month, paid onto a People's Benefit Card for food and personal items. It is run with PDV Caribe Antigua and Barbuda, and the amount and rules may have changed since.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Support and Referral Centre crisis hotline

Support and Referral Centre, hosted by the Directorate of Gender Affairs

A 24-hour crisis hotline of the Support and Referral Centre (SARC), whose open house the Directorate of Gender Affairs hosted in November 2024. The centre offers counselling, legal referrals and community outreach in one place for survivors of gender-based violence. It is a violence line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 268-463-5555 at any hour. In an emergency, call 999 or 911.
