Disability resources in the Dominican Republic

Living with a disability in the Dominican Republic, or caring for someone who is? Here is what is listed for the Dominican Republic, checked against official pages:

Where to start

Disability certificate and support devices

Consejo Nacional de Discapacidad (CONADIS)

CONADIS, the national disability council, assesses and registers persons with disabilities and issues the disability certificate (Valoración, Registro y Certificado de Discapacidad). It also assesses people for support devices and hands them out, gives legal help on disability rights, refers certified children to the Supérate programme and sends applications for solidarity pensions to the Dirección de Pensiones. Its 2024 service sheet said it had no support devices in stock to hand out that year.

Next step: Ask CONADIS in Santo Domingo, near Avenida 27 de Febrero, for an assessment. In Spanish.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Defensor del Pueblo (Ombudsman)

Defensor del Pueblo de la República Dominicana

Takes complaints (reclamaciones) against public institutions and public service providers and gives citizens guidance on their rights.

Next step: Call or WhatsApp, email, or visit the office on Av. 27 de Febrero in Santo Domingo, Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Línea Vida

Listed by UNFPA

A free line, open 24 hours a day, every day, for reporting violence. UNFPA lists it on its leaflet for victims of violence in the Dominican Republic, with Línea Mujer (*212) for emergencies. It is a violence line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 809-200-1202, free, any hour. In an emergency, Línea Mujer is *212, also free and open any hour. In Spanish.
