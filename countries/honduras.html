Crisis and someone to talk to

Línea 114 Mujer Vivir sin Miedo

Government of Honduras

A national line for women facing violence, open 24 hours a day, every day of the year, with legal advice and guidance, psychosocial support and crisis intervention. It was relaunched in January 2024 and took 18,381 calls by the time of Honduras’s 2024 report to UN Women; the Secretary of Security names it, with 911, as the number for women and girls facing violence. Whether calls are free is not stated. It is a violence line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 114, any hour. In danger right now, call the emergency number at the top of this page.

Where to start

Directorate for Persons with Disabilities (DIGEDEPDI)

Secretariat of Social Development (SEDESOL)

The government’s main disability office, part of the Secretariat of Social Development and the national focal point for the UN disability convention. SEDESOL’s 2025–2035 disability policy says its social programs include grants and scholarships for children and adolescents with disabilities, and that 72 municipal disability units have been created but are not yet working.

Next step: Ask SEDESOL’s Directorate for Persons with Disabilities (DIGEDEPDI) which support you can apply for.
