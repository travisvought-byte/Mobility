Work

Half fees for students with disabilities

Ministry of Higher and University Education; Ministry for Persons Living with Disabilities and Other Vulnerable Persons

An order of June 2, 2025 cuts academic fees and housing costs by half for students living with disabilities at all higher education institutions in the country, from the following academic year. It applies Organic Law 22/003 of 2022 on the rights of persons with disabilities.

Next step: Ask your university or institute's administration how to claim the reduction.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Green line 122

Call centre presented by the President's special adviser on gender-based violence

A free line, running since 2020 and presented in April 2026 by the office of the President's special adviser on youth, gender-based violence and trafficking, for victims and witnesses of gender-based violence. Its staff listen, record and follow up each case, and arrange free psychological, medical and legal help. Its hours are not stated. It is a violence line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 122. In an emergency in Kinshasa, call 112 for the police.
