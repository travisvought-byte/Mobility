Disability resources in Chile

Where to start

National Disability Register and disability credential

COMPIN and Registro Civil

COMPIN assesses and certifies your disability, then the Registro Civil enters you in the National Disability Register (RND) and issues a credential. Registration is free. It opens the door to SENADIS programmes, reserved parking, housing subsidies, job inclusion benefits and more.

Next step: Apply online at compin.cerofilas.gob.cl with your RUT and ClaveÚnica, or at your local COMPIN office, with the medical and social reports it asks for. COMPIN aims to decide in about 20 working days. In Spanish.

Equipment, home changes and getting around

SENADIS assistive devices programme (Programa Regular de Ayudas Técnicas)

SENADIS

Pays for up to two assistive devices for disabled people aged 10 and over who are on the National Disability Register and in the most vulnerable 70% of the Registro Social de Hogares. It opens in yearly calls; the 2026 call closed on 7 April 2026.

Next step: Watch for the next call, or email the programme. In Spanish.

Crisis and someone to talk to

*4141 Suicide Prevention Line

Ministerio de Salud (MINSAL)

A free line, open 24 hours, Monday to Sunday, for anyone in a mental health crisis linked to suicide, including suicidal thoughts, intense emotional pain, panic or wanting to self-harm. A psychologist answers.

Next step: Dial *4141 from a mobile phone on any network. In Spanish.
