Línea 133

Ministerio de la Familia

The Ministry of the Family's line, which UNICEF lists as Nicaragua's helpline for violence against children and adolescents. The National Police still gave 133 in its public notices in 2026. Its hours are not stated. It is not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 133. In an emergency, call the emergency number at the top of this page.

Where to start

Programa Todos con Voz

Ministerio de Salud (MINSA)

The Ministry of Health's national disability program. It visits people with disabilities at home at least twice a year, offers medical, psychological and rehabilitation care, delivers assistive devices to people's homes after an assessment of need, and issues a disability ID card. You must be in the ministry's register of persons with disabilities.

Next step: Ask at your local health centre (centro de salud) to be registered with Todos con Voz.
