Crisis and someone to talk to

Inter-agency Suicide Prevention Helpline

Guyana's suicide prevention helpline, open 24/7. You can also call or text 600-7896 or 623-4444. The Ministry of Human Services and Social Security also runs a toll-free suicide prevention hotline on 914.

Next step: Call 223-0001 or 223-0009 at any hour, or text 600-7896. In an emergency, call the emergency number at the top of this page.

Disability payments and benefits

Invalidity Benefit

National Insurance Scheme

A National Insurance Scheme pension for insured people under 60 who have been unable to work for at least 26 weeks, not because of a work injury, and are likely to stay so. It needs at least 150 paid contributions and 250 paid or credited, and is never less than 40 percent of the minimum wage. People with at least 50 contributions who do not qualify can get a one-time grant.

Next step: Fill in the Claim for Invalidity Benefit form and take it to your nearest National Insurance office.

Equipment, home changes and getting around

Rehabilitation and Disability Services

Ministry of Health

The Ministry of Health's programme for physiotherapy, occupational therapy, speech and language therapy and audiology, including the Ptolemy Reid Rehabilitation Centre and the National Commission on Disability.

Next step: Call the programme on 223-7087, or ask at your health centre.
