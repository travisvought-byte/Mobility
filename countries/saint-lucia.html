Crisis and someone to talk to

National Helpline 203

Ministry of Health

The Ministry of Health's free national helpline for people in suicidal crisis or emotional distress, and for mental, social and emotional problems. Trained health professionals answer 24 hours a day, seven days a week, and can refer you to counselling, social work or psychiatry. The ministry was still pointing people to it in December 2024.

Next step: Call 203 at any hour, from a landline or mobile. In an emergency, call the emergency number at the top of this page.

Disability payments and benefits

Invalidity benefit

National Insurance Corporation

Under the National Insurance Corporation Act, an insured person certified as an invalid gets an invalidity pension if they have the required contributions, are under pensionable age and no longer qualify for sickness benefit, or a one-time invalidity grant if they do not meet those conditions. The pension continues while the invalidity lasts.

Next step: Claim from the National Insurance Corporation with a medical certificate stating that your incapacity is likely to be permanent. The corporation may ask for an examination by its panel doctor.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

National Council of and For Persons with Disabilities (NCPD)

National Council of and For Persons with Disabilities

An umbrella organization coordinating disability groups across Saint Lucia that works for equal rights and also makes subsidized prosthetic legs, offers physical therapy and referrals, and distributes mobility aids.

Next step: Call or email the office at Apricot Lane, Carellie, Castries.
