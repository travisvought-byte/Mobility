Disability resources in Taiwan

In danger or in crisis right now?

In Taiwan, call 119 (Fire, disaster relief and ambulance, 24 hours a day).

1925 Anxin (peace of mind) line (安心專線)

Ministry of Health and Welfare

Free psychological counselling by phone, 24 hours a day, every day of the year.

Next step: Call 1925 from a landline or mobile, at any hour. It is free. The Ministry's page is in Chinese.

Where to start

1957 welfare consultation line (1957福利諮詢專線)

Ministry of Health and Welfare

A free line for questions about disability welfare and other social welfare, including social assistance, emergency aid and National Pension insurance. Staff give advice and refer you on.

Next step: Call 1957, every day 8:00 to 22:00, Lunar New Year included. Social workers speak Mandarin, Taiwanese, Hakka and English. Deaf, hard of hearing and speech-impaired people can use the LINE text service or online consultation.
