Crisis and someone to talk to

Gender-based violence helpline 199

Gender Management Information System call centre, listed by UNFPA

A call centre for survivors of gender-based violence, answered at any time and from anywhere in the Gambia, UNFPA reported in August 2023. Its cost is not stated. It is a violence line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 199, any hour. In an emergency, call 117 (police) or 116 (medical).

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Persons with Disabilities Act 2021

Ministry of Gender, Children and Social Welfare

The law that sets out the rights of persons with disabilities in the Gambia. It sets up an Advisory Council for Persons with Disabilities and a Disability Unit headed by the Director of Social Welfare, who keeps a national register of persons with disabilities; every local government authority also keeps a register and must help persons with disabilities to become self-reliant. It creates a National Fund for persons with disabilities, and gives private employers with ten or more employees with disabilities a 15 per cent tax deduction.

Next step: Ask the Disability Unit of the Director of Social Welfare, or your local government authority, to be put on the register.
