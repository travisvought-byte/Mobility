EGYPT · DISABILITY RESOURCES

Disability resources in Egypt

Living with a disability in Egypt, or caring for someone who is? Here is what is listed for Egypt, checked against official pages:

  • First step: بطاقة الخدمات المتكاملة للأشخاص ذوي الإعاقة (Integrated Services Card for Persons with Disabilities)
  • If you are treated unfairly: National Council for Persons with Disabilities (NCPD)

Where to start

بطاقة الخدمات المتكاملة للأشخاص ذوي الإعاقة (Integrated Services Card for Persons with Disabilities)

Ministry of Social Solidarity

A card for persons with disabilities that gives access to benefits such as the 5% government jobs quota, free hospital examinations, school and university inclusion, and customs and tax exemptions on assistive devices and a vehicle.

Next step: Apply online for a first card or check a request on the Ministry's card portal, or call the card hotline 15044.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

National Council for Persons with Disabilities (NCPD)

National Council for Persons with Disabilities

Takes complaints and questions from disabled people and their families across Egypt, studies them legally and follows them up with government and private bodies. Its complaints portal lets you track a complaint, and it takes requests for sign-language interpretation.

Next step: Call the hotline 16736, call 02 2453 0081, email info@ncpd.gov.eg, or register a complaint on the council’s complaints portal with your national ID number, phone number and email, a clear account of the problem and a photo of your ID card. In Arabic.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Psychological support hotline 105

Ministry of Health and Population (mental health and addiction treatment administration)

Consultations by phone from psychiatrists, psychologists, social workers and nurses, 24 hours a day, every day. The ministry’s mental health line was joined to 105 in March 2026.

Next step: Call 105, at any hour.
