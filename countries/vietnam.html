Disability payments and benefits

Monthly social allowance for people with disabilities (trợ cấp xã hội hàng tháng)

People’s Committee of your commune or ward

A monthly payment for people with disabilities who qualify, worked out from the national social assistance base rate of 500,000 dong a month (since 1 July 2024). Provinces can set a higher rate.

Next step: Apply at the one-stop desk (bộ phận một cửa) of the People’s Committee where you live, in person, by post or online where offered, with the application form, your ID card and your disability certificate (giấy xác nhận khuyết tật). There is no fee. In Vietnamese.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Ngày Mai hotline

Ngày Mai, a volunteer non-profit

Free psychological first aid and emotional support by phone from volunteers. It is not open around the clock; in an emergency, call 113.

Next step: Call 096 306 1414, Wednesday to Sunday, 13:00 to 20:30. In Vietnamese; English is also listed.
