Disability resources in Japan

In danger or in crisis right now?

In Japan, call 119 (Fire and ambulance. For the police, call 110).

Yorisoi Hotline (よりそいホットライン): 0120-279-338. TELL Lifeline: 0800-300-8355.

Calling without your voice? Deaf or speech-impaired: where your local fire department has joined Net119, you can report a fire or medical emergency from your phone’s browser and continue by text chat.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Yorisoi Hotline (よりそいホットライン)

Listed by the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare

A free line open 24 hours a day for any worry. The recorded guidance lets you choose specialist help, including help in foreign languages.

Next step: Call 0120-279-338, free. From Fukushima Prefecture, call 0120-279-226; from an IP phone, 050-3655-0279. Choose a foreign language in the guidance if you need one.

TELL Lifeline

TELL

A free, confidential English-language support line for anyone in Japan, with chat as well. It is not open around the clock.

Next step: Call 0800-300-8355, free. Phone hours: Saturday 9:00 through Monday 23:00, Tuesday to Thursday 9:00 to 23:00, and Friday 9:00 to 2:00. Chat opens daily from 16:00. In English.

Where to start

Disability certificates (障害者手帳)

Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare; your city, town or village office

Three certificates: for physical disability (身体障害者手帳), intellectual disability (療育手帳) and mental illness (精神障害者保健福祉手帳). Any of them opens support under the Comprehensive Support Act for persons with disabilities, and they bring discounts such as reduced JR fares and toll roads, depending on the certificate.

Next step: Ask the disability welfare desk at your city, town or village office. The physical disability certificate needs a report from a designated doctor and a photo. In Japanese.
