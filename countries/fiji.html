Disability resources in Fiji

Disability payments and benefits

Disability Allowance Scheme

Ministry of Women, Children and Social Protection

A monthly allowance from the government for people living with a permanent disability. The amount is set in the national budget each year.

Next step: Ask the Ministry of Women, Children and Social Protection; its officials also register people on outreach visits.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Fiji Human Rights and Anti-Discrimination Commission (FHRADC)

Fiji Human Rights and Anti-Discrimination Commission

Fiji's national human rights and anti-discrimination body takes complaints through an online form and has a disability rights section explaining the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2018, including disability discrimination.

Next step: Lodge a complaint with the online form on the website, call 330 8577, or visit Level 2 Naibati House, 9 Goodenough Street, Suva.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Lifeline Fiji 1543

Lifeline Fiji

Lifeline Fiji's national crisis toll-free code, for anyone in crisis or thinking about suicide. The President of Fiji, speaking in 2023, described Lifeline Fiji's helpline as a nationwide 24-hour toll-free crisis intervention line with qualified crisis workers.

Next step: Call 1543 at any hour. In an emergency, call the emergency number at the top of this page.
