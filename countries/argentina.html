Disability resources in Argentina

Living with a disability in Argentina, or caring for someone who is? Here is what is listed for Argentina, checked against official pages:

  • First step: Certificado Único de Discapacidad (CUD)

Where to start

Certificado Único de Discapacidad (CUD)

Secretaría Nacional de Discapacidad

A free national document certifying disability, issued after an assessment by an interdisciplinary board. It gives access to full coverage of rehabilitation linked to the disability, free travel on public ground transport, family allowances and some tax and toll exemptions.

Next step: Gather your medical reports, use the online check to see what to bring and where to go, book an appointment with the evaluation board, then collect the CUD in person. It also appears in the Mi Argentina app. In Spanish.

Disability payments and benefits

Non-contributory pension for work disability (pensión no contributiva por invalidez laboral)

Secretaría Nacional de Discapacidad, applied for through ANSES

A pension for people with a total and permanent disability (work capacity reduced by 66% or more) who have no other pension or registered job and not enough income. Foreign residents need 10 years of residence. Applying is free; do not pay agents or lawyers to do it.

Next step: Get the digital official medical certificate (CMO) at a public hospital or health centre, then apply in Mi ANSES or at an ANSES office. Check on your case on WhatsApp at +54 11 3910-1010. In Spanish.

Equipment, home changes and getting around

International Symbol of Access for vehicles

Secretaría Nacional de Discapacidad

A free digital symbol, linked to a number plate, for the vehicle a person with a valid CUD travels in. It lets that vehicle drive and park under each town’s traffic rules. You do not need to own a car.

Next step: Request it in Mi Argentina, under Mis Trámites, with your CUD and the plate number. Questions go to simbolos@senadis.gob.ar. In Spanish.

Crisis and someone to talk to

National mental health urgent support line

Ministerio de Salud de la Nación

A free, confidential line open 24 hours every day across the country, answered by trained mental health professionals. It helps with suicide risk, self-harm and crises linked to substance use, violence or disasters, and can send emergency services.

Next step: Call 0800 999 0091. In Spanish. The government also lists 135 as a suicide prevention line.

In danger or in crisis right now?

In Argentina, call 911 (Police and ambulance. Fire brigade: 100. In the City of Buenos Aires and parts of Buenos Aires Province, 107 is the SAME ambulance).
