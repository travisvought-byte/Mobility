Disability resources in Denmark

Living with a disability in Denmark, or caring for someone who is? Here is what is listed for Denmark, checked against official pages:

  • Payments: Disability pension (førtidspension)
  • Equipment and getting around: Support for a car (støtte til handicapbil)
  • If you are treated unfairly: Diskriminationslinjen (Discrimination Helpline)

Disability payments and benefits

Disability pension (førtidspension)

Your municipality (kommune); paid by Udbetaling Danmark

For people whose ability to work is permanently reduced so much that they cannot work on ordinary terms. You must meet residence rules. The municipality decides, after a resource clarification process and a rehabilitation plan made with you.

Next step: Contact your municipality to start. Once it is granted, Udbetaling Danmark pays it to your NemKonto. The life in Denmark guide explains it in English.

Equipment, home changes and getting around

Support for a car (støtte til handicapbil)

Your municipality (kommune)

An interest-free loan towards the cheapest suitable car (up to 231,000 kroner in 2026) for people whose lasting physical or mental disability makes them depend on a car to work, study or take part in activities. You can also get help with adaptations such as hand controls or a swivel seat, and with driving lessons. Parents can apply for a disabled child.

Next step: Apply to your municipality through the “Ansøg om støtte til bil” self-service on borger.dk, or contact the municipality if you cannot apply online. In Danish.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Diskriminationslinjen (Discrimination Helpline)

Danish Institute for Human Rights

Free legal advice if you have been discriminated against, including because of a disability, or are unsure whether you have. Its lawyers can help with complaints to the Equal Treatment Board (Ligebehandlingsnævnet).

Next step: Call Monday to Thursday, 12:00 to 15:00, or email diskriminationslinjen@humanrights.dk with “Diskriminationslinjen” in the subject. In Danish.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Livslinien

An anonymous line for people with thoughts of suicide and for those worried about someone. It is open every day of the year from 9:00 to 5:00 the next morning, not around the clock. A chat is open in the evenings and weekend afternoons.

Next step: Call 70 201 201 between 9:00 and 5:00. Chat runs Monday to Friday, 17:00 to 21:00, and Saturday and Sunday, 13:00 to 17:00. Its website is in Danish.
