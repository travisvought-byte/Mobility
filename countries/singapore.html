Disability resources in Singapore

Living with a disability in Singapore, or caring for someone who is? Here is what is listed for Singapore, checked against official pages:

  • First step: SG Enable and the Enabling Guide

  • Work: Tripartite Alliance for Fair and Progressive Employment Practices (TAFEP)

Calling without your voice? Deaf, hard of hearing or speech-impaired: text 70995 for an ambulance or fire engine, or text 70999 for the police when it is not safe to talk.

Where to start

SG Enable and the Enabling Guide

SG Enable

SG Enable runs several of Singapore's disability schemes, including the Assistive Technology Fund and the Taxi Subsidy Scheme. Its Enabling Guide website lists the schemes for assistive technology, transport, care and work in one place.

Next step: Call the hotline on weekdays, 9am to 5.30pm, or email contactus@sgenable.sg.

Equipment, home changes and getting around

Assistive Technology Fund (ATF)

SG Enable

Pays up to 90% of the cost of devices such as wheelchairs and hearing aids, including repairs, upgrades and replacements, up to S$40,000 in a lifetime. It is for Singapore citizens and permanent residents with a permanent disability and a monthly household income of S$4,800 or less per person.

Next step: A therapist or social worker at a public hospital or social service agency assesses what you need and applies for you. If no one is looking after you yet, ask a polyclinic to refer you to a public hospital.

Taxi Subsidy Scheme

SG Enable

Pays part of taxi fares to school, work or SG Enable-supported training for people with a permanent physical, visual or intellectual disability, or autism, who cannot use public transport. It is for citizens and permanent residents with a monthly household income of S$4,800 or less per person who do not own a vehicle.

Next step: Apply online with Singpass, or email the form and documents to tss@sgenable.sg. SG Enable says it decides within 15 working days of getting everything.

Persons with Disabilities Concession Card

SG Enable and SimplyGo

Up to 55% off adult bus and train fares, plus monthly passes for unlimited travel, for citizens and permanent residents aged 7 to under 60 with a verified permanent disability.

Next step: Check on SupportGoWhere, with Singpass, whether your disability is already verified. If not, have a registered healthcare professional fill in a Disability Verification Form and submit it there. Then apply through SimplyGo.

Work

Tripartite Alliance for Fair and Progressive Employment Practices (TAFEP)

Tripartite Alliance Limited

Helps workers who face discrimination at work, including because of a disability or a mental health condition. If it finds discrimination, it refers the employer to the Ministry of Manpower. The Workplace Fairness Act, which covers disability, is due to take effect by the end of 2027.

Next step: Try your employer's grievance process or your union first. If that does not fix it, report it on TAFEP's online form, or call on weekdays, 8.30am to 5.30pm; an officer may call you back.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Disabled People's Association (DPA)

Disabled People's Association

A cross-disability advocacy group led by and for people with disabilities. It speaks to decision-makers on disability policy and runs access guides and disability awareness training.

Next step: Call or email to ask for help or to join as a member.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Samaritans of Singapore (SOS)

Samaritans of Singapore

A 24-hour hotline for anyone in crisis, in distress or thinking about suicide. Its CareText service on WhatsApp is also open at any hour.

Next step: Call 1767. To write instead, send a WhatsApp message to 9151 1767.
