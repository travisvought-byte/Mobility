Disability resources in Sweden

In danger or in crisis right now?

In Sweden, call 112 (Emergency number for ambulance, fire and police).

Självmordslinjen (Suicide Hotline): 90101.

Calling without your voice? Deaf, hard of hearing or speech-impaired: register in advance with SOS Alarm to reach 112 by text (SMS112), call through the text telephone service at texttelefoni.se, or reach 112 with sign-language interpreting through Bildtelefoni.net. Relay service website (new tab)

Crisis and someone to talk to

Självmordslinjen (Suicide Hotline)

Mind

An anonymous line for people with thoughts of suicide, answered by volunteers 24 hours a day, every day. You can also chat.

Next step: Call 90101. Mind publishes this information in English; its page does not say which languages the volunteers speak.

Disability payments and benefits

Additional cost allowance (merkostnadsersättning)

Försäkringskassan (Swedish Social Insurance Agency)

A tax-free monthly payment for extra costs caused by a disability that has lasted or will last at least a year. Each cost is assessed on its own, and your extra costs need to come to at least 14,800 kronor a year. You apply yourself from 18 (parents apply for 18 to 21 year olds still at school).

Next step: Add up your extra costs, get a medical statement, then apply through the e-service or on form 3085. A decision normally takes about five months. Customer service is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 16:00. In Swedish.

Equipment, home changes and getting around

Disabled parking permit (parkeringstillstånd för rörelsehindrade)

Your municipality (kommun), under rules from Transportstyrelsen

A permit for people with a lasting disability that makes it very hard to get around on their own, as a driver or as a passenger who needs the driver’s help. It is valid across Sweden for up to five years and lets you park longer where parking is limited, and in reserved spaces.

Next step: Apply to the municipality where you are registered. The municipality may not charge a fee for handling the application. In Swedish.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Equality Ombudsman (Diskrimineringsombudsmannen, DO)

Diskrimineringsombudsmannen

The government agency that takes complaints about discrimination, including because of a disability (functional impairment).

Next step: Use the complaint form on do.se, email do@do.se or call. DO can also be reached by sign-language interpreted call, text telephone or interpreted phone call. Its site has pages in English.
