Crisis and someone to talk to

Social emergency line 123

State Welfare Organization, listed by UNHCR

The national social emergency line, free and open 24 hours, 7 days a week.

Next step: Call 123, free, any hour. In an emergency, call 115 (ambulance) or 110 (police).

Where to start

State Welfare Organization (Behzisti)

Government of Iran

The government body that helps people with disabilities, with rehabilitation services and work to prevent disability, an Iranian government page said in 2022. It also runs the 123 and 1480 lines above.

Next step: Ask at your local State Welfare Organization office, or call 123.
