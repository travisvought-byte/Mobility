Disability payments and benefits

Assistance and attendance allowance (dodatek za pomoč in postrežbo)

Pension and Disability Insurance Institute of Slovenia (ZPIZ)

A benefit for pensioners who need another person's help with basic daily needs, and for some people working in Slovenia who are blind, partially sighted or have a physical disability. Being blind or partially sighted, or having at least 70 percent reduced ability to move, can qualify.

Next step: Your doctor, you, or your legal or authorised representative files the claim with ZPIZ, with your medical records; ZPIZ's website has an online form. In Slovenian.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Advocate of the Principle of Equality (Zagovornik načela enakosti)

Zagovornik načela enakosti

An independent state body that gives independent help, advice and legal aid to people who have been discriminated against, in the public and private sectors. In its annual report, disability was among the grounds of discrimination it found, and most of its 127 recommendations concerned the rights of people with disabilities.

Next step: Call 01 473 55 31 or write to Železna cesta 16, 1000 Ljubljana. In Slovenian.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Emotional support helpline 116 123

Association of Slovenian Helpline Advisers for Mental Distress

Slovenia's telecoms regulator lists 116 123 as the emotional support helpline for people in mental distress. The child helpline 116 111 is listed beside it.

Next step: Call 116 123. The regulator's list does not give its hours. In an emergency, call 112.
