Disability resources in Kazakhstan

Mobility Talk began in the United States and is now adding other countries. These are the first listings for Kazakhstan: 2 national programs and lines, each checked against its own page or an official directory (most recently on 2026-11-21). Local and regional services are not listed yet.

In danger or in crisis right now?

In Kazakhstan, call 112 (Emergency and rescue service; police 102, fire 101, ambulance 103).

Contact centre 111: 111.

Numbers are written the way they are dialed inside Kazakhstan. Calling from another country? Use the country code, and note that some free numbers only work inside the country.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Contact centre 111

Government contact centre (State Corporation "Government for Citizens")

A round-the-clock line, with no breaks, weekends or holidays, for family problems and the rights of women and children. Operators give psychological support to people facing domestic violence, bullying and other problems, and can bring in operational teams to help.

Next step: Call 111 at any hour. In an emergency, call 112.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Ombudsman for Human Rights of Kazakhstan

Ombudsman for Human Rights of the Republic of Kazakhstan

Kazakhstan's human rights ombudsman takes appeals from citizens, including on the rights of persons with disabilities, which it reviewed in a September 2026 country report.

Next step: Send an appeal through the E-Otinish system (eotinish.kz, with a digital signature) or dialog.egov.kz (no signature needed), or call the call centre on 8 (7172) 74-05-48. In-person reception: Mangilik El Avenue 8, entrance 15, Astana, Tuesday to Friday 10:00 to 16:00.
