Disability resources in the United Arab Emirates

Living with a disability in the United Arab Emirates, or caring for someone who is? Here is what is listed for the United Arab Emirates, checked against official pages:

  • First step: People of Determination card
  • If you are treated unfairly: National Human Rights Institution (NHRI)

Where to start

People of Determination card

Ministry of Community Empowerment (MOCE)

A personal card issued by the ministry to people with disabilities (called people of determination in the UAE), so they can take part in education, work, health care and social life on equal terms.

Next step: Apply to the ministry with your Emirates ID, a passport-style photo on a white background, and a medical or psychological diagnosis report approved by a People of Determination welfare and rehabilitation centre. The ministry’s line is toll-free.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

National Human Rights Institution (NHRI)

UAE National Human Rights Institution

The UAE's national human rights institution; the government's official portal directs human rights complaints to it.

Next step: Use the institution's website, or report a human rights issue through the channels listed on the u.ae 'Report discrimination' page.

Crisis and someone to talk to

800 SAKINA mental health support line

Department of Health – Abu Dhabi, with Sakina (SEHA)

A mental health support line open 24 hours a day to anyone in the community, offering psychological first aid and access to qualified mental health professionals for anxiety, panic, stress and sleep problems, with services for children and families.

Next step: Call 800 725462 (800-SAKINA), at any hour. In Arabic or English.
