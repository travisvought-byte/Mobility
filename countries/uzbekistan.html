Disability resources in Uzbekistan

Living with a disability in Uzbekistan, or caring for someone who is? Here is what is listed for Uzbekistan, checked against official pages:

  • First step: National Agency of Social Protection
  • If you are treated unfairly: Authorized Person of the Oliy Majlis for Human Rights (Ombudsman)

Where to start

National Agency of Social Protection

The national agency for disability. It establishes disability, supplies prosthetic and orthopaedic products and rehabilitation equipment, runs rehabilitation centres, and supports employment and home-based social services. Pensions, allowances, wheelchairs, hearing and visual aids, rehabilitation, sanatorium treatment and medicines are provided on the findings of medical and social expert commissions.

Next step: Ask the agency’s office in your district. Sanatorium referrals can be applied for on my.gov.uz.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Authorized Person of the Oliy Majlis for Human Rights (Ombudsman)

Ombudsman of the Republic of Uzbekistan

The national human rights ombudsman receives citizens' appeals, including complaints about officials' actions, labour and legal matters, at its central office and through regional representatives.

Next step: Call the short number 1096 or (71) 200-10-96, email ombudsman1@umail.uz, or visit the Tashkent office or a regional representative's reception (Mon-Fri 09:00-13:00, 14:00-18:00).

Crisis and someone to talk to

Helpline 1146

A short trust helpline listed by the government and the Ombudsman for people who have suffered harassment or violence. Its hours are not stated. It is not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 1146. In an emergency, call 112, or 102 for the police.
