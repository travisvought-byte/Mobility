Disability resources in Samoa

Mobility Talk began in the United States and is now adding other countries. These are the first listings for Samoa: 3 national programs and lines, each checked against its own page or an official directory (most recently on 2026-11-21). Local and regional services are not listed yet.

In danger or in crisis right now?

In Samoa, call 911 (Ambulance, fire and police).

Samoa Victim Support Group helpline: 800-7874. Mental Health and Psychosocial Services: 21212.

Numbers are written the way they are dialed inside Samoa. Calling from another country? Use the country code, and note that some free numbers only work inside the country.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Samoa Victim Support Group helpline

Samoa Victim Support Group

A free 24-hour helpline for victims of violence, run by the Samoa Victim Support Group, a non-governmental organisation, and promoted with UN support. The Office of the Ombudsman lists it for anyone affected by sexual violence. It is a violence line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 800-7874 at any hour. In an emergency, call the emergency number at the top of this page.

Call 800-7874

Listing: confirm details before you rely on it.

Mental Health and Psychosocial Services

Ministry of Health

The Ministry of Health's mental health and psychosocial services, reached through the ministry's main number (extensions 602 and 603). Its hours are not stated, so it may answer only in working hours.

Next step: Call 21212 and ask for extension 602 or 603.

Call 21212

Checked 2026-11-21 against: health.gov.ws (new tab). Report a problem

Disability payments and benefits

Disability Social Welfare and Security Scheme

Ministry of Women, Community and Social Development

A government welfare scheme for registered persons with disabilities. In the 2023/24 financial year the ministry reported that it reached 2,380 people and registered 266 new beneficiaries. The report does not give the amount.

Next step: Call the ministry on 27752 to ask about registering as a person with a disability.
