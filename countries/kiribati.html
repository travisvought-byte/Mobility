Disability payments and benefits

Disability support allowance

Ministry of Women, Youth, Sport and Social Affairs

A monthly allowance for persons with disabilities, higher for severe disabilities; 2,569 people received it, the government told the UN disability committee in 2025. People with psychosocial disabilities who have had at least six months of treatment and cannot find work are eligible. The government also gives about 300 assistive devices a year through the Tungaru Rehabilitation Service and repairs wheelchairs free of charge.

Next step: Ask the Ministry of Women, Youth, Sport and Social Affairs about the allowance, and the Tungaru Rehabilitation Service about devices.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Kiribati Women and Children Support Centre

Listed by the United Nations and UN Women

Free, confidential counselling by trained counsellors for women and children affected by violence, with case coordination and referrals. The centre is in Bairiki, Tarawa, and also counsels by phone; when it opened in 2018 it announced a 191 helpline. Its hours are not stated. It is a violence support service, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 750 21000, or the 191 helpline. In an emergency, call 999.
