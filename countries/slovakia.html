Disability payments and benefits

Cash allowances to compensate for a severe disability (peňažné príspevky na kompenzáciu ŤZP)

Ministry of Labour, Social Affairs and Family

Help for people with a severe disability (ťažké zdravotné postihnutie). One-time allowances cover buying, adjusting or repairing an aid, a lifting device, buying or adapting a car, and adapting a flat, house or garage. Recurring allowances cover personal assistance, transport, increased expenses and caregiving. The severe-disability card and the parking permit are part of the same system.

Next step: Apply at your local Office of Labour, Social Affairs and Family (Úrad práce, sociálnych vecí a rodiny); its medical assessor and a social worker assess you. Income can reduce the allowances, and none is paid if your property is worth more than 50,000 euros. In Slovak.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Commissioner for Persons with Disabilities (Komisár pre osoby so zdravotným postihnutím)

Úrad komisára pre osoby so zdravotným postihnutím

An independent office that examines complaints from people with disabilities who believe a Slovak authority or institution has harmed their rights.

Next step: Submit a complaint (podnet) through the office website, call 02 2042 0309, email sekretariat@komisar.sk, or write to Račianska 153, 831 54 Bratislava. The site does not list opening hours. In Slovak.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Nezábudka mental health line (Linka dôvery Nezábudka)

Liga za duševné zdravie

Slovakia's Ministry of Justice lists Nezábudka as a mental health hotline in its important contacts for victims, and the government's child-protection centre lists it among its help lines.

Next step: Call 0800 800 566. The official lists do not give its hours. In an emergency, call 112.
