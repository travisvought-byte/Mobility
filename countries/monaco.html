Disability resources in Monaco

In danger or in crisis right now?

In Monaco, call 112 (Ambulance, fire and police from a mobile. From a landline, dial 18).

Crisis and someone to talk to

Information line for victims of violence

Government of Monaco, with the victim support association AVIP

A free number for victims of any kind of violence, including domestic and sexual violence and harassment, from a landline or a mobile. It replaced the old 116 919. Its hours are not stated. It is a violence line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 0800 91 90 10, free. In an emergency, call 112.

Where to start

Priority card for people with disabilities

Department of Social Welfare and Social Services (DASO)

A card giving priority seating on public transport and in waiting areas, and priority in queues at places and events open to the public. It is for people granted disability status at 80% or more, or whose disability makes standing difficult.

Next step: Get the form and medical certificate from DASO or its page, have your doctor fill in the certificate (dated within 3 months), and hand them in with a copy of your ID or residence permit and 2 ID photos. DASO is at 5 Promenade Honoré II, Hall A, 4th floor, open Monday to Friday, 9:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.

Disability payments and benefits

Disability allowance for adults

Department of Social Welfare and Social Services (DASO)

A means-tested allowance for adults living in Monaco who have been granted disability status and are no longer eligible for the special education allowance. Once you are told you have disability status, DASO's financial social support office contacts you for the means test.

Next step: Call DASO on 98 98 41 00, Monday to Friday, 9:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.
