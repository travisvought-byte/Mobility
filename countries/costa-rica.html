In danger or in crisis right now?

In Costa Rica, call 911 (Emergencies that threaten life, property or the environment. You can also report through the Emergencias 9-1-1 CR app).

Crisis and someone to talk to

Línea Aquí Estoy

Colegio de Profesionales en Psicología de Costa Rica

A free emotional support line from the professional college of psychologists. It is not open around the clock; in an emergency, call the emergency number.

Next step: Call 800-AQESTOY (800-2737869), Monday to Friday 14:00 to 22:00, or Saturday 9:00 to 16:00.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Defensoría de los Habitantes

Defensoría de los Habitantes de la República

The national ombudsman. It takes complaints when a public institution fails or violates people’s rights, and it is the national body that monitors the UN disability convention. Most of the disability complaints it receives concern health services, pensions and due process.

Next step: Call free on 800-258-7474 (switchboard 4000-8500), email correspondencia@dhr.go.cr, use the complaint form on its website, or visit its San José office. In Spanish.
