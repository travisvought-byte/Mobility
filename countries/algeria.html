Disability resources in Algeria

In danger or in crisis right now?

In Algeria, call 17 (Police (or 1548). Ambulance: 1021; fire: 14).

Crisis and someone to talk to

Child protection green line 1111

National Body for the Protection and Promotion of Childhood (ONPPE)

A free national number, running since 2018, for reporting harm to a child. A child, a parent or anyone else can call. Psychologists, sociologists and legal specialists answer in shifts through the day; the caller’s identity is kept confidential and the call does not show on the itemised phone bill. It is a children’s line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 1111, free, from a landline or a mobile. In an emergency, call 17 (police) or 1021 (ambulance).

Where to start

Special needs card

Ministry of National Solidarity, Family and Women’s Affairs

The ministry’s card for people with special needs, including people with disabilities. Since August 2026 you can apply for a first card, change its details or replace a lost or damaged card online, on the National Portal of Digital Services, Algeria’s state radio reported.

Next step: Apply on the National Portal of Digital Services, or ask the Ministry of National Solidarity.
