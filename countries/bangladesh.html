Disability resources in Bangladesh

Living with a disability in Bangladesh, or caring for someone who is? Here is what is listed for Bangladesh, checked against official pages:

  • First step: Disability identification and ID card
  • Payments: Disability allowance

Where to start

Disability identification and ID card

Department of Social Services

Under the Rights and Protection of Persons with Disabilities Act 2013, persons with disabilities are registered and given an identity card after a medical check. The card helps you join government programmes.

Next step: Apply online at dis.gov.bd, or take the form to the Upazila or City Social Service Office where you live. You need a birth registration or national ID card, a mobile number and an email address. The office sets a date for the medical check and sends an SMS when your card is ready.

Disability payments and benefits

Disability allowance

Department of Social Services, Ministry of Social Welfare

A monthly allowance for persons with disabilities on low incomes who are over six years old. Local committees choose who gets it, with priority for older people, homeless people, women with multiple disabilities and poor children with intellectual disabilities.

Next step: Ask at your Upazila Social Service Office.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Kaan Pete Roi

Bangladesh's emotional support and suicide prevention helpline. Trained volunteers listen in confidence to anyone in despair, alone or thinking about suicide.

Next step: Call 09612 119911 any day from 3pm to 3am, except on holidays. In an emergency, call 999.
