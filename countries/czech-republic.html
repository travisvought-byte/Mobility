Disability resources in the Czech Republic

Living with a disability in the Czech Republic, or caring for someone who is? Here is what is listed for the Czech Republic, checked against official pages:

Disability payments and benefits

Care allowance (příspěvek na péči)

Úřad práce ČR (Labour Office) and the Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs

A monthly payment for people from age one who, because of a long-term health condition, need help with basic needs such as getting around, communicating, eating or personal hygiene. It is set by degree of dependency: for adults 1,300, 5,400 or 14,800 Kč, and 23,000 or 27,000 Kč at the highest degree; for under-18s 4,900, 8,200 or 16,100 Kč, and 23,000 or 27,000 Kč.

Next step: Apply in writing on the ministry’s form at a Labour Office contact point for where you have permanent residence, saying who will help you. A social worker visits you at home before your degree of dependency is assessed. The Labour Office call centre is free. In Czech.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Public Defender of Rights (Veřejný ochránce práv)

Public Defender of Rights (the ombudsman)

The ombudsman assesses whether you were discriminated against, including because of disability, explains your rights, and if it recommends going to court may arrange free legal representation.

Next step: Call the hotline every day, 8:00 to 16:00, email podatelna@ochrance.cz, or write to Údolní 39, 602 00 Brno. Describe what happened and keep any evidence.

Crisis and someone to talk to

First psychological aid line (linka první psychické pomoci)

116 123, a European helpline number listed on the government portal

A first psychological aid line, open around the clock. Calls to 116 numbers are free.

Next step: Call 116 123, free, at any hour.

In danger or in crisis right now?

In the Czech Republic, call 112 (The single European emergency number, free and open 24 hours a day. Ambulance: 155, fire: 150, police: 158).
