Crisis and someone to talk to

SOS Criança 15015

Instituto Nacional da Criança (INAC)

Angola's line for reporting violence against children and other violations of people's rights, run by the National Children's Institute (INAC). It recorded about 32,000 incidents in 2024. Its hours and cost are not stated. It is not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 15015. In an emergency, call 112 for an ambulance or 110 for the police.

Equipment, home changes and getting around

Mobility aids and technical aids for persons with disabilities

Ministério da Acção Social, Família e Promoção da Mulher (MASFAMU)

A government programme, budgeted in the 2024 state budget, that gives persons with disabilities mobility aids (meios de locomoção) and technical aids (ajudas técnicas). Some provincial governments also buy mobility equipment for people with physical disabilities.

Next step: Ask the social action office of your municipality or MASFAMU.
