Disability resources in Italy

Mobility Talk began in the United States and is now adding other countries. These are the first listings for Italy: 4 national programs and lines, each checked against its own page or an official directory (most recently on 2026-11-21). Local and regional services are not listed yet.

In danger or in crisis right now?

In Italy, call 112 (Any emergency. The older numbers still work too: 113 police, 115 fire brigade, 118 ambulance).

Telefono Amico Italia: 02 2327 2327.

Numbers are written the way they are dialed inside Italy. Calling from another country? Use the country code, and note that some free numbers only work inside the country.

Calling without your voice? Deaf: the free 112Sordi service works across Italy, with text chat and location sharing. The free Where ARE U app can also reach 112 by voice call, silent call or chat.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Telefono Amico Italia

A listening line answered by trained volunteers for anyone who feels alone or distressed, or is thinking about suicide. It is open every day from 9:00 to midnight, not 24 hours. You can also write on WhatsApp.

Next step: Call 02 2327 2327; the service is free and you pay only the normal cost of the call. Or message 324 011 7252 on WhatsApp. In Italian.

Disability payments and benefits

Civil invalidity recognition (invalidità civile)

INPS

Recognition of civil invalidity is the first step to Italy’s disability payments, such as the monthly allowance for partly disabled people aged 18 to 67 with low incomes. The application and your doctor’s medical certificate go to INPS online.

Next step: Ask your doctor for the medical certificate, then apply online to INPS. For help with INPS online services, call its contact centre. In Italian.

Equipment, home changes and getting around

European disabled parking permit (CUDE)

Lets the holder use disabled parking, and is valid across Italy and in the European Union. It is for people whose walking ability is seriously reduced, and for blind people.

Next step: Get a certificate from the forensic medicine office of your local health authority (ASL), then apply to your town council (Comune). Permanent permits last five years. In Italian.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

National Authority for the Rights of Persons with Disabilities

Autorità Garante nazionale dei diritti delle persone con disabilità

An independent national authority set up in 2024. Its work includes countering direct and indirect discrimination against disabled people. In 2025 it took up more than 1,300 reports, mostly about school.

Next step: See its website for how to send a report. In Italian.
