SOUTH KOREA · DISABILITY RESOURCES

Disability resources in South Korea

Living with a disability in South Korea, or caring for someone who is? Here is what is listed for South Korea, checked against official pages:

  • First step: Health and Welfare Call Centre 129 (보건복지상담센터)
  • Payments: Disability pension (장애인연금)
  • If you are treated unfairly: National Human Rights Commission of Korea

Where to start

Health and Welfare Call Centre 129 (보건복지상담센터)

Ministry of Health and Welfare

The national line for questions about health and welfare programmes, including the disability pension. There is also a chat with its counsellors.

Next step: Call 129 with no area code. In Korean.

Disability payments and benefits

Disability pension (장애인연금)

Ministry of Health and Welfare

A monthly pension for severely disabled people aged 18 or over on low incomes and assets (in 2025, up to 1.38 million won a month for a single person). It paid up to 432,510 won a month in 2025.

Next step: Apply at your local community service centre (읍·면·동 행정복지센터) or online through Bokjiro. Questions: call 129. In Korean.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

National Human Rights Commission of Korea

National Human Rights Commission of Korea (NHRCK)

Takes complaints about unlawful discrimination, including because of a disability, under the Act on the Prohibition of Discrimination against Persons with Disabilities.

Next step: Call 1331 in Seoul and Gyeonggi, or 02-1331 elsewhere. You can also send a complaint by mail, fax or email to hoso@humanrights.go.kr; an English complaint form is on its site.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Suicide prevention line 109 (자살예방 상담전화)

Government of the Republic of Korea

The national suicide prevention counselling line, answered by trained counsellors 24 hours a day.

Next step: Call 109, any hour. The government page describing it is in Korean and does not say which other languages are offered.

Call 109

In danger or in crisis right now?

In South Korea, call 119 (Fire, disasters and medical emergencies, answered by the National Fire Agency. You can also report to 119 by text message with photos, through the 119 reporting app or by video call).
