Disability resources in Saint Kitts and Nevis

In danger or in crisis right now?

In Saint Kitts and Nevis, call 911 (Ambulance and police; fire 333).

National Counselling Centre: 465-5000.

Crisis and someone to talk to

National Counselling Centre

Ministry of Social Development and Gender Affairs

Free counselling for the public for any personal issue, including depression, anxiety, anger, trauma and abuse, for children, teenagers, adults, couples and families. The unit also responds to crises. It is open Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m., at Government Headquarters, Church Street, Basseterre; it is not a 24-hour line.

Next step: Call 465-5000 on a weekday. In an emergency, call the emergency number at the top of this page.

Disability payments and benefits

Non-contributory assistance pension (disability)

Social Security Board

A means-tested monthly payment for ordinary residents with a disability aged 16 to 62, and for people over 62 who have no contributory pension. It is paid by the Social Security Board and was XCD 250 a month in 2020; check the current amount. A long absence from the country may lead to disqualification.

Next step: Apply to the Social Security Board.
