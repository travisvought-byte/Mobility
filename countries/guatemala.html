Disability resources in Guatemala

Disability payments and benefits

IGSS disability pension (pensión por invalidez)

Instituto Guatemalteco de Seguridad Social (IGSS)

A pension for insured workers whose disability is confirmed by the IGSS. If it is caused by illness, you need 36 contributions paid in the 6 years before the disability began; if it is caused by an accident, you must have been covered when it happened and have 3 months of contributions in the 6 months before.

Next step: Bring your DPI and the completed work history form (IVS-102) to the IGSS at 7.ª avenida 22-72, zona 1, Centro Cívico, Guatemala City; in the metropolitan area, book through CATAFI, and elsewhere go to your nearest IGSS office. Update your details, including your NIT, first. It is free and you do not need a lawyer or agent. In Spanish.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Procurador de los Derechos Humanos (PDH) (Human Rights Ombudsman)

Procuraduría de los Derechos Humanos de Guatemala

Guatemala's human rights ombudsman ensures respect for people's rights and takes complaints (denuncias) from anyone who has suffered a human rights violation.

Next step: Call the complaints line 1555 or use the denuncia and call-center links on the PDH website.

Call 1555Website (new tab)

Checked 2026-11-23 against: pdh.org.gt (new tab), help.unhcr.org (new tab). Report a problem

Crisis and someone to talk to

1582 Te Escucha

FUNDEGUA (Fundación Desarrolla Guatemala para la Educación y la Salud)

A free crisis and suicide prevention line, open 24 hours a day since 10 October 2026, with referrals to other services.

Next step: Call 1582, or send a WhatsApp message to 3007-1582. It is free for the caller.
