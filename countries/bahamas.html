BAHAMAS · DISABILITY RESOURCES

Disability resources in the Bahamas

Living with a disability in the Bahamas, or caring for someone who is? Here is what is listed for the Bahamas, checked against official pages:

  • First step: Department of Social Services
  • If you are treated unfairly: National Commission for Persons with Disabilities

Where to start

Department of Social Services

Ministry of Social Services

The government department whose work includes services for persons with disabilities. Its Disability Affairs Division helps persons with disabilities and their families.

Next step: Call the Department, or visit it at Aventura Plaza, John F. Kennedy Drive and Bethel Avenue, Nassau.

Call 397-8600

Checked 2026-11-21 against: bahamas.gov.bs (new tab), bahamascustoms.gov.bs (new tab). Report a problem

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

National Commission for Persons with Disabilities

The national commission for persons with disabilities. Its AccessAbility app lets you register with the Commission for free and has a way to make complaints.

Next step: Call or send a WhatsApp message to the Commission's line, or email Disabilitiescommission@bahamas.gov.bs.

Call 376-8328Email

Crisis and someone to talk to

Bahamas Crisis Centre

The Bahamas Crisis Centre

A 24-hour hotline, answered by volunteers, for women, men and children facing domestic violence, abuse or any other crisis.

Next step: Call 328-0922.

About these listings

Mobility Talk began in the United States and is now adding other countries. These are the first listings for the Bahamas: national programs and lines, each checked against its own page or an official directory (most recently on 2026-11-21). Local and regional services are not listed yet.
