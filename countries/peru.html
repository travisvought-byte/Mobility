Crisis and someone to talk to

Línea 113 Salud, option 5 (mental health)

Ministerio de Salud (Minsa)

A free mental health line open 24 hours every day across the country, answered by psychologists experienced in emotional crises.

Next step: Call 113 and choose option 5. You can also write on WhatsApp or Telegram to 955 557 000. In Spanish.

Where to start

Disability certificate and CONADIS registration card

Ministry of Health centres and CONADIS

First get a disability certificate from a health centre certified by the Ministry of Health; some tests may cost money. Then register with CONADIS, the national authority on disability, for its card, which unlocks benefits such as free public transport for severe disability and a vehicle sticker.

Next step: Register online on the CONADIS platform by uploading your certificate. You get a digital card by email, and can collect a printed one in Lima or at a regional office. In Spanish.

Disability payments and benefits

Contigo pension for severe disability

Programa Nacional Contigo (Ministry of Development and Social Inclusion)

A pension for people with a severe disability living in poverty or extreme poverty who have no other income or pension, public or private.

Next step: You need a disability certificate rated severe from a Ministry of Health centre and a current poverty rating, which you request from your district municipality. Your district’s OMAPED office can check whether you are enrolled. Payments are collected at Banco de la Nación. In Spanish.
