Where to start

Yusr national digital platform for persons with disabilities

Ministry of Social Development and Family (MSDF), developed with Mada Center for Assistive Technology

A single digital gateway giving persons with disabilities, their families and caregivers access to specialised services and accessible information from the relevant authorities.

Next step: Go to yusr.msdf.gov.qa.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Law No. 22 of 2025 on Persons with Disabilities

Ministry of Social Development and Family (MSDF)

The law bans discrimination because of disability and requires reasonable accommodation. It covers accessible buildings and transport, education, health care and rehabilitation, digital access, and job quotas in government and private work, and it provides a disability ID card for the benefits the law sets. The Ministry of Social Development and Family oversees it.

Next step: Contact the ministry on 16080 or info@msdf.gov.qa. The ministry also offers an online "To Whom It May Concern" certificate for people with disabilities.

Crisis and someone to talk to

National Mental Health Helpline 16000

Hamad Medical Corporation

The main entry point for mental health support in Qatar, open to anyone regardless of age, background or nationality. Calls are answered by mental health nurses, psychologists and psychiatrists and are fully confidential. It is not open around the clock.

Next step: Call 16000 and choose option 4 for mental health, Saturday to Thursday 8:00 to 18:00. Outside those hours, or in an emergency, call 999.

In danger or in crisis right now?

In Qatar, call 999 (Police, fire and medical emergencies, 24 hours, with translators in several languages. Deaf people can use 992 by SMS, video call or email to 992@moi.gov.qa).
