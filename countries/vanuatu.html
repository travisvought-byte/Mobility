Disability resources in Vanuatu

Mobility Talk began in the United States and is now adding other countries. These are the first listings for Vanuatu: 3 national programs and lines, each checked against its own page or an official directory (most recently on 2026-11-21). Local and regional services are not listed yet.

In danger or in crisis right now?

In Vanuatu, call 111 (Police; ambulance 115, fire 113).

Vanuatu Women's Centre 24-hour helpline: 161. Child Desk Helpline: 163.

Numbers are written the way they are dialed inside Vanuatu. Calling from another country? Use the country code, and note that some free numbers only work inside the country.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Vanuatu Women's Centre 24-hour helpline

Vanuatu Women's Centre

A free 24-hour helpline for people facing violence, run by the Vanuatu Women's Centre and listed in the victims' charter of the Office of the Public Prosecutor. The centre's paid line is 24000. It is a violence line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 161 free at any hour, or 24000 (paid). In an emergency, call 111.

Child Desk Helpline

A free helpline for child protection listed in the Office of the Public Prosecutor's victims' charter. Its hours are not stated. It is a children's line, not a general mental health line.

Where to start

Disability Desk

Ministry of Justice and Community Services

The government office that coordinates disability policy and Vanuatu's international disability commitments with government bodies and NGOs. It works with provincial disability committees and a community-based rehabilitation working group, and helps NGOs reach people with disabilities in rural communities.

Next step: Call 33615, or visit the Ministry of Justice and Community Services in the Namba 2 area, Port Vila.
