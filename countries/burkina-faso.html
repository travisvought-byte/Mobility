Disability resources in Burkina Faso

In danger or in crisis right now?

In Burkina Faso, call 17 (Police; fire and ambulance 18. Outside Ouagadougou, emergency services may not respond).

Numéro vert 116 (child protection): 116. Numéro vert for gender-based violence: 80 00 12 87.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Numéro vert 116 (child protection)

Government of Burkina Faso

A free hotline (numéro vert) set up by the government for child protection: to report violence against a child and get help. The government listed it in its 2024 replies to the UN Committee on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities. It is a children's line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 116. In an emergency, call 17 (police) or 18 (fire and ambulance).

Numéro vert for gender-based violence

Government of Burkina Faso

A free hotline for reporting gender-based violence. The government's 2024 reports to the United Nations say it took 1,139 reports between March 2021 and 2023. It is a violence line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 80 00 12 87.

Where to start

Disability card (carte d'invalidité)

Ministry in charge of national solidarity

A card for persons with disabilities. Holders get free or reduced-cost health care, with free care and tests for holders who cannot pay, priority enrolment at preschool, primary and secondary schools, and reduced import duties on mobility equipment. The government told the UN in 2024 that 9,699 people held one.

Next step: Send a written request to the minister in charge of solidarity through official channels, with a 200 FCFA fiscal stamp, a medical certificate, two ID photos and a certified copy of your national ID card or birth certificate.
