Disability resources in Saint Vincent and the Grenadines

Living with a disability in Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, or caring for someone who is? Here is what is listed for Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, checked against official pages:

Where to start

Ministry of National Mobilisation, Social Development, Gender Affairs and Persons with Disabilities

Government of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines

The ministry responsible for persons with disabilities; in March 2025 the government announced a disability unit within it to provide support, advocacy and services.

Next step: Contact the ministry on Halifax Street, Kingstown, by phone or email. On the main line, ask for extension 3863.

Disability payments and benefits

Public assistance and monthly disability grants

Government of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines

The government pays monthly disability and social protection grants through its public assistance programme, and helps with school meals and transport, medical costs and utility bills; persons with disabilities who cannot leave home get care under the home help programme, the government’s information agency said in December 2024.

Next step: Ask the Ministry of National Mobilisation about public assistance and home help.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Gender Affairs Division

Government of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines

The government’s gender affairs office, to which it has asked the public to report gender-based violence and violence against women. It is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:15 p.m., and closed at weekends. It is a government office, not a crisis line.

Next step: Call 784-457-2789 in office hours. In an emergency, call 911 or 999.
