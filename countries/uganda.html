Crisis and someone to talk to

Mental Health Uganda toll-free line

Mental Health Uganda

Free, confidential support from trained counsellors for anyone struggling with their mental health, with information and referrals to further care. You can talk in several local languages.

Next step: Call 0800 21 21 21, Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 5pm. At other times, in an emergency, call 999.

Disability payments and benefits

Special Grant for Persons with Disabilities

Ministry of Gender, Labour and Social Development

Grants for money-making projects run by groups of 5 to 15 people in the same sub-county: persons with disabilities aged 15 and over, carers of children with disabilities, or registered homes for persons with disabilities. Groups have used it for things like rearing animals, mobile money and buying and selling produce.

Next step: Plan the project as a group, fill in the disability grant project application form, and hand it in at your sub-county through the community development officer.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Equal Opportunities Commission

Investigates and tries to settle complaints of discrimination, including because of disability. Complain within six months of what happened.

Next step: Use the online complaint form, call the toll-free line, or email info@eoc.go.ug.

National Union of Disabled Persons of Uganda (NUDIPU)

NUDIPU

The national umbrella of organisations of persons with disabilities, formed in 1987. Its members are 146 district unions and 17 national disability organisations. It advocates for disability rights and runs training, business and job programmes.

Next step: Call or email NUDIPU, or ask your district union of persons with disabilities.
