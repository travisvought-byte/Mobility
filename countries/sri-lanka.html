Crisis and someone to talk to

National Mental Health Helpline 1926

National Institute of Mental Health

The national mental health helpline run from the National Institute of Mental Health.

Next step: Call 1926. The Institute's pages do not give its hours; for other questions, call the Institute on 011 2578234.

1990 Suwa Seriya free ambulance

1990 Suwa Seriya Foundation

A free 24-hour emergency ambulance service covering the whole island, run by a foundation fully owned by the Government of Sri Lanka.

Next step: Call 1990, at any hour. It is free.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Human Rights Commission of Sri Lanka (HRCSL)

Human Rights Commission of Sri Lanka

Takes complaints from a victim or group of victims of a violation, or threatened violation, of fundamental rights, or from someone representing them.

Next step: Call the hotline 1996, or complain in person, by post, by email to secretary@hrcsl.lk, by fax or with the online form. Complaint forms are free from the head office (14 R. A. De Mel Mawatha, Colombo 4; 011 2505580), regional offices or the website. In Sinhala, Tamil or English.
