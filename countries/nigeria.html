Disability resources in Nigeria

Where to start

National Commission for Persons with Disabilities (NCPWD)

National Commission for Persons with Disabilities

The federal agency set up by the Discrimination Against Persons with Disabilities (Prohibition) Act, 2018. It registers persons with disabilities, links them to social protection programmes, promotes affordable assistive technology and takes up cases where the Act is not being followed, including discrimination.

Next step: Call or email the Commission, or visit its head office at Plot 79 Ralph Shodeinde Street, Central Business District, Abuja.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

National Human Rights Commission

Takes complaints about human rights violations, free of charge, at its headquarters and state offices, including violations against persons with disabilities.

Next step: Call the toll-free line or the short code 6472, file a complaint online, or write to the Executive Secretary at any NHRC office.
