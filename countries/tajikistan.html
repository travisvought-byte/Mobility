Crisis and someone to talk to

Crisis Hotline 1313

Committee on Women and the Family

A line for preventing domestic violence, launched in 2018 by the government’s Committee on Women and the Family with Eurasia Foundation Tajikistan. Calls from mobile phones are free. It answers Monday to Friday from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. and Saturday from 8 a.m. to noon, and is closed on Sunday. It is a violence line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 1313 from a mobile, free, during its hours. In an emergency, call 103 (ambulance) or 102 (police).

Equipment, home changes and getting around

Free assistive products

State Enterprise of Orthotics and Prosthetics

Free assistive products such as wheelchairs, hearing aids and prosthetics from the State Enterprise of Orthotics and Prosthetics, under a national list of 30 essential products. WHO reports that a pilot in Rudaki district gave them out through health facilities, with repairs at primary health care workshops.

Next step: Ask your district primary health care centre, or the State Enterprise of Orthotics and Prosthetics.
