Disability resources in Benin

In danger or in crisis right now?

In Benin, call 118 (Fire and ambulance. Police: 117. Emergency response is very limited).

Child helpline 138 (Ligne d'Assistance aux Enfants): 138.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Child helpline 138 (Ligne d'Assistance aux Enfants)

Ministry of Social Affairs and Microfinance

A free line, open 24 hours a day from landlines and mobile phones, for reporting violence and abuse against children; anyone who sees or suffers violence against a child can call. The ministry launched it in March 2020 with UNICEF funding. It is a children’s line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 138, free, any hour. In an emergency, call 118 (fire and ambulance) or 117 (police).

Where to start

Carte d'égalité des chances (equal opportunity card)

Ministry of Family and Social Action

The disability card that shows which rights and preferential measures a person with a disability can claim, based on their assessed level of disability. The cards are made by the national identification agency (ANIP) and handed out by the Ministry of Family and Social Action; in September 2026 the ministry said 4,000 new applications for 2026 would go to the interministerial commission on inclusion measures.

Next step: Ask the Ministry of Family and Social Action, or an association in the federation of disability associations (FAPHB), how to apply.
