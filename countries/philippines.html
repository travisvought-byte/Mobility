Crisis and someone to talk to

NCMH Crisis Hotline

National Center for Mental Health

The government's mental health crisis line, open any hour, for anyone in emotional distress or thinking about suicide.

Next step: Dial 1553 toll-free from a landline. From a mobile, call 0919 057 1553 (Smart/TNT) or 0917 899 8727 (Globe/TM).

Where to start

PWD ID card

Your city or municipality (PDAO), with the National Council on Disability Affairs

The identification card for persons with disability. Under Republic Act 10754 it gives at least a 20% discount and VAT exemption on many goods and services, such as medicines and medical and dental services. It is registered in the Philippine Registry for Persons with Disabilities.

Next step: Apply at the Persons with Disability Affairs Office (PDAO) or the social welfare office of your city or municipality, with the application form and a certificate of disability from a doctor where the disability is not apparent. To report a fake PWD ID, email NCDA.

Equipment, home changes and getting around

AICS medical and assistive device help

Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD)

Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations (AICS) gives one-time financial help to people facing a crisis, including medical help that can cover medicine and assistive devices. DSWD says it is not only for the poor.

Next step: Go to a DSWD Crisis Intervention Unit or your local social welfare office with a valid ID, a medical certificate or clinical abstract, a prescription or request for the device, and a price quotation. A social worker assesses your situation.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Commission on Human Rights

Commission on Human Rights of the Philippines

The national human rights institution. It takes complaints and gives legal help, and monitors the rights of persons with disabilities under the Magna Carta for Persons with Disability and the UN disability rights convention.

Next step: Call the head office (press 2) or its Public Assistance and Complaints Desk on 0936 068 0982 (Globe) or 0920 506 1194 (Smart), or email publicassistance@chr.gov.ph.
