Crisis and someone to talk to

Emergency psychological help line 133

Health care system of Belarus

A national emergency psychological support line, answered 24 hours a day by psychologists from the health care system. Calls are free from all telephone operators. For children and teenagers in crisis, the national children's line 8-801-100-16-11 is also open 24 hours a day.

Next step: Call 133 at any hour. In an emergency, call 103 for an ambulance or 102 for the police.

Equipment, home changes and getting around

Technical means of social rehabilitation

Local social protection services

Aids from the State Register of technical means of social rehabilitation (Council of Ministers Resolution No. 1722 of 2007), given to people living in Belarus on the basis of a disabled person's individual rehabilitation programme or a medical advisory commission (VKK) conclusion. Some items are free and others carry a 20%, 25% or 50% charge.

Next step: Bring your rehabilitation programme or VKK conclusion, your passport and your disability certificate to the social service that issues technical aids where you live (in Gomel, the city's centre for social services).
