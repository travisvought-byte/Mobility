Emergency line 14 44 (violence)

Government of the Republic of the Congo

A short emergency number for reporting violence, in service since 5 March 2018. The government told UN Women in June 2024 that it had moved to a dedicated platform in 2023 and was being publicised in all twelve departments. Its hours and cost are not stated. It is a violence line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 14 44. In an emergency, call the police on 06 665 4804.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Law 18-2025 on the rights of persons living with disabilities

Ministry of Social Affairs, Solidarity and Humanitarian Action

Law no. 18-2025 of 25 July 2025 protects and promotes the rights of persons living with disabilities. It creates a disability card (carte d’invalidité), whose conditions and benefits a decree is to set; gives the right to health, rehabilitation and social protection; guarantees access to ordinary and specialised schooling; requires public bodies and public and private companies to reserve a quota of jobs; and gives fare reductions and priority on public transport, plus tax relief on imported assistive devices.

Next step: Ask the ministry in charge of social action about the disability card and support.
