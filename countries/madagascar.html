Equipment, home changes and getting around

Equipment for persons with disabilities

Government of Madagascar

In its November 2024 report to the UN Human Rights Council, the government said 19,616 persons with disabilities in 15 regions had been given equipment, including assistive devices such as wheelchairs, crutches and hearing aids, and 7,651 had received food, blankets and other materials. The 1997 law on the rights of people living with disabilities is being amended.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Commission Nationale Indépendante des Droits de l'Homme (CNIDH, National Independent Human Rights Commission)

CNIDH Madagascar

Madagascar's national human rights institution; it receives and examines complaints about human rights violations.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Free lines 147 and 113

Listed by the UN in Madagascar

Free numbers (lignes vertes) for reporting any form of violence, named by the UN system in Madagascar in 2022. Their hours and who answers them are not stated. They are violence lines, not general mental health lines.

Next step: Call 147 or 113, free. In an emergency, call 117 from a mobile.
