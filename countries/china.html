Disability payments and benefits

Living allowance and nursing allowance for people with disabilities

Ministry of Civil Affairs, Ministry of Finance and China Disabled Persons' Federation

Two national allowances (残疾人两项补贴): a living allowance for people with disabilities in low-income (dibao) households, which local governments may extend to households just above that line, and a nursing allowance for people with severe disabilities. You need a valid disability certificate. Payment starts in the month you apply once you are found eligible.

Next step: Apply at your township or street (subdistrict) office or the county civil affairs department; someone can apply for you. If you live away from your registered home, you can apply where you live and the case is sent to your home area, which must decide within 10 working days.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

中国残疾人联合会 (China Disabled Persons' Federation, CDPF)

China Disabled Persons' Federation

National federation that represents and safeguards the rights and interests of persons with disabilities; disabled persons' federations run legal aid stations and a nationwide 12385 service hotline for persons with disabilities.

Next step: Call the 12385 hotline for persons with disabilities or contact your local disabled persons' federation.

Crisis and someone to talk to

12356 psychological assistance hotline

National Health Commission

China's nationwide psychological assistance hotline (心理援助热线), set up by the National Health Commission. Each city-level area runs at least one line, staffed for at least 18 hours a day, and all 31 provinces had opened 12356 by May 1, 2025, when it came into nationwide use. Whether calls are free is not stated.

Next step: Call 12356. In an emergency, call 120 for an ambulance or 110 for the police.
