Disability payments and benefits

Disability Assistance and Severe Disability Assistance

Department of Social Security

Non-contributory payments for people aged 16 to under 65 whose disability began before 60, rated by the Inter-Professional Board: 45 to 59 percent for Disability Assistance, 60 to 74 percent for Severe Disability Assistance, and 75 percent or more for Increased Severe Disability Assistance. There is no means test, so you can qualify while working. Paid every four weeks.

Next step: Your treating specialist applies for you through the online Medical Report form; the Inter-Professional Board then assesses you.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Report disability discrimination to the CRPD

Commission for the Rights of Persons with Disability (CRPD)

People with disabilities, and family members directly affected, can report discrimination on the grounds of disability. The report is vetted within 10 days and assessed within 30, with a decision within 180 days.

Next step: Fill in the online e-form on servizz.gov.mt.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Mental Health Helpline 1579

Mental Health Services, Ministry for Health

A 24-hour national helpline giving free emotional support, advice and practical guidance to anyone in need, including people in crisis and people worried about a family member or friend. It is answered by psychology professionals from Mental Health Services.

Next step: Call 1579 at any hour. In an emergency, call 112.
