Disability resources in the Solomon Islands

In danger or in crisis right now?

In the Solomon Islands, call 999 (Police; ambulance 111, fire 988).

Crisis and someone to talk to

SafeNet 24-hour Helpline

A 24-hour helpline that the Solomon Islands Government lists for victims of violence, alongside the Family Support Centre, the police Family Violence Unit and the Ministry of Health and Medical Services' Gender Based Violence Unit. It is a violence line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 132 at any hour. In an emergency, call 999.

Integrated Mental Health Clinic

Ministry of Health and Medical Services

The Ministry of Health and Medical Services' mental health clinic, listed on the government's emergency contacts page. Its hours are not stated, so it may answer only in working hours.

Next step: Call 20690.

Equipment, home changes and getting around

Rehabilitation and assistive devices

Ministry of Health and Medical Services

The rehabilitation department of the National Referral Hospital is the main provider of assistive devices such as crutches and wheelchairs. The ministry's Community Based Rehabilitation (CBR) division works with CBR and disability coordinators in the provinces.

Next step: Call the National Referral Hospital on 23601 and ask for the rehabilitation department, or ask your provincial health office about its CBR and disability coordinator.
