Disability resources in Malawi

In danger or in crisis right now?

In Malawi, call 998 (Ambulance. Police: 990 or 997; fire: 999. These numbers are unreliable, so ask local police for other numbers for your area).

Crisis and someone to talk to

National Child Helpline 116

Listed by UNICEF Malawi

A toll-free national line to report violence against a child or get help for a child. Its hours are not stated. It is a children's line, not a general mental health line. UNICEF also lists the police Mthetsa Nkhanza line, 932, for reporting violence.

Next step: Call 116, free. In an emergency, call the emergency number at the top of this page.

Disability payments and benefits

Social Cash Transfer Programme

Government of Malawi

The government told the UN disability committee in 2023 that around 30,000 persons with disabilities receive social cash transfers, and that the programme had been scaled up to all 28 districts. It also said it has funded a Disability Trust Fund for disability programmes since 2019/2020, and that assistive devices are on the essential list of medical equipment.

Next step: Ask your district council how households are selected for the programme.
