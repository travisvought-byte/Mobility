Disability resources in Latvia

Living with a disability in Latvia, or caring for someone who is? Here is what is listed for Latvia, checked against official pages:

  • First step: State Medical Commission for the Assessment of Health Condition and Working Ability (VDEAVK)
  • Payments: Disability pension (invaliditātes pensija)
  • If you are treated unfairly: Ombudsman of the Republic of Latvia (Tiesībsargs)

Where to start

State Medical Commission for the Assessment of Health Condition and Working Ability (VDEAVK)

Veselības un darbspēju ekspertīzes ārstu valsts komisija

The state medical commission for assessing health and working ability. Its page on support for persons with disabilities covers benefits for adults and for families of a child with a disability, the disabled person's parking card, free intercity public transport, exemption from vehicle operating tax and the invalidity pension, with a booklet that summarises them.

Next step: Call +371 6761 4885 or +371 2963 2807, email pasts@vdeavk.gov.lv, use the question form on its website, or write to Ventspils iela 53, Rīga, LV-1002.

Disability payments and benefits

Disability pension (invaliditātes pensija)

State Social Insurance Agency (VSAA)

A pension for socially insured people living in Latvia, below retirement age, whose disability has been determined in Latvia and who have at least three years of insurance. It is paid in full while you work. From 1 January 2026, Group III is 213 euros a month (255 euros for a disability since childhood); Groups I and II depend on past earnings and insurance, with minimums of 340.80 and 298.20 euros. Disability caused by a work accident or occupational disease is handled separately.

Next step: Apply on the Latvija.gov.lv portal, at any VSAA customer service centre with your passport or ID card, or by email with a secure electronic signature. Applying is free and takes up to 22 working days. Apply from the date your disability is determined: a late application is paid back at most 6 months. VSAA: +371 6450 7020.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Ombudsman of the Republic of Latvia (Tiesībsargs)

Tiesībsarga birojs

Handles discrimination (unequal treatment without an objective reason) and the rights of people with long-term physical, mental, intellectual or sensory impairments.

Next step: Submit an application online, by email to tiesibsargs@tiesibsargs.lv (signed with a secure electronic signature), through the Latvija.lv e-address, by post, or in person at Baznīcas iela 25, Rīga, LV-1010, Monday to Friday 9:00 to 17:00.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Psycho-emotional support line 116123

National Health Service (Nacionālais veselības dienests)

A telephone line for psycho-emotional support for adults, completely free and available 24 hours a day, in Latvian, Russian and English. Through it, any adult resident of Latvia can also get up to 10 free consultations with a certified psychologist.

Next step: Call 116123 at any hour. Children and teenagers can call the Child and Adolescent Helpline 116111, and victims of crime the helpline 116006. In an emergency, call 112.
