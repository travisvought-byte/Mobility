Mental health hotline 1323

Ministry of Public Health

Free mental health consultation with experts, open 24 hours a day, every day, for people in Thailand and visitors. English-language services are available for foreign nationals.

Next step: Call 1323, free, at any hour.

Disability payments and benefits

Welfare help for people with disabilities

Ministry of Social Development and Human Security

Registered people with disabilities facing hardship, such as living alone or without family support, can ask for help in goods or cash of up to 3,000 baht at a time, up to 3 times a year.

Next step: Apply at a Disability Service Centre in your province or in Bangkok with your disability ID card or certificate, a bank book and a copy of the applicant’s ID. There is a home visit, then a committee decides. Disabled persons hotline: 1479; social assistance hotline: 1300.
