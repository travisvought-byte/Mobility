Disability payments and benefits

Financial assistance for people with disabilities (MmeB)

Ministry of Social Affairs and Housing

Monthly financial assistance (financiële bijstand) for registered people with disabilities, paid out by the ministry's AUSV payment service to clients with a valid decision (beschikking). The ministry announces payment dates and places in public notices. The amount is not stated in those notices.

Next step: Ask the Ministry of Social Affairs and Housing (Sozavo) office in your district about registering. To collect a payment, bring your valid decision and ID card; someone collecting for you needs a stamped authorisation from the district head and both ID cards.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Helpline 114

Ministry of Health, Welfare and Labour

Suriname's mental health crisis line, launched on June 5, 2026 by the Ministry of Health, Welfare and Labour with PAHO/WHO support, for anyone in emotional distress, with suicidal thoughts or in a mental health crisis. It is free, confidential and anonymous and open around the clock.

Next step: Call 114 at any hour. In an emergency, call 115.
