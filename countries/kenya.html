Where to start

Register with the National Council for Persons with Disabilities (NCPWD)

National Council for Persons with Disabilities

Registration with NCPWD is free and is the key to its services: assistive devices, education help, economic empowerment grants, job placement, legal advice and the income tax exemption. You need a disability assessment report from a government-gazetted hospital, signed by the county medical director.

Next step: Get assessed at a gazetted hospital, then register on eCitizen (ncpwd.ecitizen.go.ke) with the report, your national ID or a child's birth certificate, and a passport photo. Or ask at your county NCPWD office.

Disability payments and benefits

Inua Jamii cash transfer for persons with severe disabilities

State Department for Social Protection

Regular payments to households caring for a person with a severe disability who needs permanent care, through the Inua Jamii programme. Recipients must not be in another government cash transfer programme.

Next step: Ask your local social development officer about enrolment, or email inuajamii@socialprotection.go.ke. Paid recipients can check and withdraw funds by dialling *222#.

Income tax exemption for persons with disabilities

National Council for Persons with Disabilities, with the Kenya Revenue Authority

Registered persons with disabilities who have taxable income can be exempted from tax on the first KES 150,000 of income a month.

Next step: Apply through the NCPWD portal on eCitizen with your NCPWD certificate, KRA PIN, a valid tax compliance certificate and the other listed documents. NCPWD says it takes one to three months.

Equipment, home changes and getting around

NCPWD assistive devices

National Council for Persons with Disabilities

Registered members can apply for assistive devices, including motorized wheelchairs. For a motorized wheelchair, NCPWD covers up to KES 100,000 and you pay anything above that.

Next step: Fill in the assistive device form, get a recommendation from the right professional (a physiotherapist or orthopaedic technologist, an audiologist, or an ophthalmologist), attach your NCPWD card, ID and assessment report, and hand it in at your county NCPWD office.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Kenya National Commission on Human Rights

Takes complaints about human rights violations, including against persons with disabilities. It monitors Kenya's duties under the UN disability rights convention and the Persons with Disabilities Act, 2025.

Next step: Call the toll-free line and say you want to lodge a complaint; the Commission calls you back at its cost. You can also SMS 22359, email complaints@knchr.org, or complain on the website.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Kenya Red Cross 1199

Kenya Red Cross Society

A free 24-hour line run by the Kenya Red Cross. Its counsellors give emotional and psychosocial support to people in distress.

Next step: Dial 1199. If it does not connect, call the Red Cross on +254 703 037 000.
