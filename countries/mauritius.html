Crisis and someone to talk to

Domestic violence hotline 139

Ministry of Gender Equality and Family Welfare

A free hotline, open 24 hours a day, every day, for reporting domestic violence. It is not a general mental health line. For family problems the ministry also runs hotline 119.

Next step: Call 139 at any hour. In an emergency, call 999.

Disability payments and benefits

Invalid's Basic Pension

Ministry of Social Integration, Social Security and National Solidarity

A monthly pension of Rs 15,555 (from January 2026) for people under retirement age certified by a Medical Board or Medical Officer as at least 60 percent incapacitated for at least 12 months. Beneficiaries under 18 get an extra Rs 3,000 a month. It is not paid to inmates of subsidised charitable institutions.

Next step: Register at the Social Security Office nearest you with your birth certificate, National Identity Card, medical certificates, bank details and a passport photo. For information, call 172 (9:00 to 16:00).
