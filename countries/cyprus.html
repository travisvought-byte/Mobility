CYPRUS · DISABILITY RESOURCES

Disability resources in Cyprus

Living with a disability in Cyprus, or caring for someone who is? Here is what is listed for Cyprus, checked against official pages:

  • First step: Department for Social Inclusion of Persons with Disabilities (Τμήμα Κοινωνικής Ενσωμάτωσης Ατόμων με Αναπηρίες)
  • If you are treated unfairly: Commissioner for Administration and the Protection of Human Rights (Ombudsman)

Where to start

Department for Social Inclusion of Persons with Disabilities (Τμήμα Κοινωνικής Ενσωμάτωσης Ατόμων με Αναπηρίες)

Ministry of Labour, Welfare and Social Insurance, Republic of Cyprus

The national disability department; it carries out disability and functioning assessments and certification and grants social benefits to persons with disabilities.

Next step: Call or email the department.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Commissioner for Administration and the Protection of Human Rights (Ombudsman)

Office of the Commissioner for Administration and the Protection of Human Rights

Examines complaints of discrimination, including when a public or private body violates your rights (for example to employment, education or health care) because of disability. You can complain for yourself or, with their consent, for someone else. It is free.

Next step: Use the online form, email ombudsman@ombudsman.gov.cy, or deliver or post your complaint (2 Diagorou, Nicosia). Call 22 405500 to check whether the Office can look into your complaint.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Cyprus Samaritans

Emotional support for adults aged 18 and over. The Youth Board of Cyprus lists it among free helplines.

Next step: Call 8000 7773 (free). The official list does not give its hours. Children and teenagers can call 116111. In an emergency, call 112.
