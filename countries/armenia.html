Disability payments and benefits

Disability benefit

National Services Gateway of Armenia

A monthly benefit for people with disabilities who are not entitled to a pension, and for children recognized as having disabilities: AMD 39,000 for a profound limitation, AMD 37,500 for severe and AMD 36,000 for moderate. Disability is recognized through a functionality assessment (a limitation of 25% or more). It is not paid to people living in state-run social protection institutions.

Next step: First apply online for a functionality assessment, then apply for the benefit online with your ID, social security card and the disability certificate. Assistive devices and personal assistant services are requested at regional centres of the Unified Social Service.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Human Rights Defender of Armenia

Human Rights Defender (Ombudsman)

Armenia's ombudsman, which takes complaints when a public body has violated your rights, with a hotline that answers around the clock.

Next step: Call the hotline 116 (or +374 96 116-100), or email ombuds@ombuds.am.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Women's Support Center hotline

Women's Support Center NGO

A 24-hour hotline for anyone facing domestic violence, listed by UNHCR and in UNICEF Armenia's list of mental health and psychosocial support services. It is not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 099 887 808 at any hour. In an emergency, call 112.

FAR Children's Support Center line

FAR Children's Support Center

A free 0800 line for children and families, in UNICEF Armenia's list of mental health and psychosocial support services (list updated May 2024). UNICEF does not give its hours.

Next step: Call 0800 61111. In an emergency, call 112.
