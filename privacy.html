MOBILITY TALK · PRIVACY

Privacy.

What this site records when you visit, what it does not, and how advertising works. Updated November 23, 2026.

In short

  • There is no sign-in and no account.
  • Nothing you type or choose on this site is saved or sent anywhere.
  • The maps show pictures from OpenStreetMap, which sees which area of the map you look at.
  • A visit counter counts page views without cookies.
  • Pages that show Google ads load Google’s ad code, which can set its own cookies. We ask Google for non-personalized ads only.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Who your browser talks to

A visit to this site can reach these services. Each name links to its part of this page.

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, it may keep server logs, including IP addresses, for security.
  • GoatCounter counts page views without cookies: the page, the site you came from, browser and screen size, and an approximate country. It does not store your IP address.
  • OpenStreetMap sends the map pictures on the home page and the local help page, so it sees your IP address and the area of the map you look at.
  • Google AdSense loads only on the home page, the topic guides and the state and country pages. Google’s ad code can set its own cookies.
  • themif.org sends the Mobility Independence Foundation’s logo shown on some pages.

Visit counting

Every page uses GoatCounter to count visits. It counts page views without cookies and without storing personal information. For each view it records:

  • the page,
  • the site you came from, if any,
  • your browser and screen size,
  • an approximate country, worked out from your IP address. GoatCounter does not store the IP address.

How it is set up here:

  • The counter records only the page address, not the part after a question mark. Choices such as the state or kind of help you picked are not counted.
  • For the site you came from, it keeps only that site and page, not the rest of the address.
  • The counts go to a GoatCounter dashboard shared with the maker’s other websites, with this site’s pages kept under their own name.
  • The counter script loads from GoatCounter’s own server (gc.zgo.at).

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep server logs, including IP addresses, for security. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement (new tab).

Some pages load more files from this site as you use them, such as the program list or a state’s county outlines. Those requests go to GitHub Pages like any page view.

Some pages show the Mobility Independence Foundation’s logo, which your browser loads from the foundation’s own website (themif.org).

Maps

The home page and the local help page show a map. The outlines of the states and counties come from this site. The map pictures underneath them (streets, towns and place names) come from OpenStreetMap's map servers (tile.openstreetmap.org), which your browser asks for the area you are looking at. Like any website, OpenStreetMap sees your IP address with each request, and so the area of the map you view. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy (new tab).

  • “Use my location” asks your browser for your location only when you press it. The location is used inside the page to find your state and county, and is never stored or sent to this site or anyone else. The map then shows that area, so OpenStreetMap is asked for the pictures around it.
  • If you would rather not load the map pictures, choose your state and county from the menus or the plain state pages instead; they show the same programs.

What you type or choose

  • Nothing you type or choose on this site is saved or sent anywhere (the map pictures are the one exception; see Maps above). Message drafts and notes stay in your open page until you close it.
  • This site’s own code sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. Pages that show Google ads load Google’s ad code, which can set its own cookies (see Advertising below).
  • Links you copy to share carry only choices such as the state and the kind of help, never your own words.
  • A message to lawmakers is sent only if you copy it or open it in your own email and send it yourself.
  • Phone, email and website links go to other organizations, which have their own privacy practices.

Advertising

This site shows ads. We use Google AdSense and ask Google to show only non-personalized ads, which are chosen from the page you are on and your general location, not from your visits to other sites.

  • Google’s ad code loads only on the home page, the topic guides and the state and country pages. It never loads on the local help directory, the printable help list, the Spanish help page, or the about, partner and privacy pages.
  • On those pages Google and its partners may still use cookies or similar storage, for example to limit how often you see an ad, to count ads and to stop fraud. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services (new tab) and policies.google.com/technologies/ads (new tab).
  • You can manage the ads Google shows you at adssettings.google.com (new tab), or opt out of many networks at optout.aboutads.info (new tab).

Any ad sold directly is labeled “Advertisement” and kept apart from the directory. Ads never change what is listed or the order it is listed in. How directly sold ads are chosen is on the partner page.

Selling and children

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • This site is not directed to children under 13.
