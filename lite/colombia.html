Disability help in Colombia

Emergency in Colombia: call 123 (Single emergency line).

Where to start

Disability certification (certificación de discapacidad)

Ministerio de Salud; requested through your municipal or district health secretariat

A team of health and social professionals assesses you and issues a disability certificate, and your details go into the national register of disabled people (RLCPD).

Next step: Ask your municipal or district health secretariat for a certification order, with your medical history and diagnosis from your treating doctor. The secretariat checks your papers within about four working days, and the assessing clinic must schedule you within 10 working days. For questions, call the Ministry. In Spanish.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Defensoría del Pueblo

The national ombudsman takes complaints, free of charge, about human rights violations by public officials or service providers.

Next step: Call the national toll-free line, or use the online services on its website. In Spanish.

Call 01 8000 914 814defensoria.gov.co

Crisis and someone to talk to

Línea 106 mental health line

Ministerio de Salud y Protección Social

A free, confidential line open 24 hours, 7 days a week. Mental health professionals listen, give first support and point you to help.

Next step: Dial 106 from a landline or mobile. Video calls can also be booked. In Spanish.
