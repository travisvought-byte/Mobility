Disability help in North Macedonia

Emergency in North Macedonia: call 112 (All emergency services; the police, fire and ambulance numbers also still work).

Where to start

Министерство за социјална политика, демографија и млади (Ministry of Social Policy, Demography and Youth)

Government of North Macedonia

Ministry responsible for social protection, including protection of people with developmental disabilities, civilian and war-disabled persons, and pension and disability insurance.

Next step: Contact the ministry by phone or email, or at 14 Dame Gruev Str., Skopje.

Call 02 3117 288mtsp.gov.mkmtsp@mtsp.gov.mk

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Commission for Prevention and Protection against Discrimination

Комисија за спречување и заштита од дискриминација

North Macedonia's equality body, which handles reports of discrimination, including on grounds of disability.

Next step: Report discrimination through the Commission's website or email contact@kszd.mk. The office is at Dame Gruev 1, 1000 Skopje.

kszd.mkcontact@kszd.mk

Crisis and someone to talk to

La Strada national SOS helpline

La Strada / Open Gate

A free 24-hour helpline for victims of human trafficking or sexual abuse, listed by UNHCR. It is not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 0800 11111 free at any hour. From abroad, call +389 2 2777 070. In an emergency, call 112.

Call 0800 11111
