Disability help in Morocco

Emergency in Morocco: call 19 (Police. Ambulance (SAMU) and fire service: 15).

Where to start

Disability certificate and the Social Cohesion Support Fund

Ministry of Solidarity, Social Development, Equality and Family

The ministry lists its services for persons with disabilities: a disability certificate (certificat d’invalidité), requested online, and the Social Cohesion Support Fund (Fonds d’appui à la cohésion sociale), which helps with equipment and technical aids, schooling for children with disabilities, work and income-generating activities, and reception centres. It also lists reduced import duty on cars for persons with disabilities, public-sector job quotas, and help at stations, airports and ports.

Next step: Request the certificate online at khadamty.social.gov.ma, and ask the ministry about the fund.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Mécanisme national de protection des droits des personnes en situation de handicap (National Mechanism for the Protection of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities)

Conseil National des Droits de l'Homme (CNDH)

Hosted by Morocco's National Human Rights Council, it accepts complaints from persons with disabilities who are victims of rights violations (or from their representatives or third parties), investigates them and decides on each case.

Next step: Contact the CNDH in Rabat by phone or email, or write to 22, Rue Riyad, Quartier Riyad, BP 21527, Rabat.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Central unit for women victims of violence

Ministry of Solidarity, Social Development, Equality and Family

The ministry’s central unit for the care of women victims of violence (Cellule centrale de prise en charge des femmes victimes de violence). It is a government office, not a crisis line: its hours and cost are not stated, so it may answer only in working hours.

Next step: Call 05 37 27 65 66. In an emergency, call 19 (police) or 15 (ambulance).
