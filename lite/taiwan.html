Disability help in Taiwan

Emergency in Taiwan: call 119 (Fire, disaster relief and ambulance, 24 hours a day).

Where to start

1957 welfare consultation line (1957福利諮詢專線)

Ministry of Health and Welfare

A free line for questions about disability welfare and other social welfare, including social assistance, emergency aid and National Pension insurance. Staff give advice and refer you on.

Next step: Call 1957, every day 8:00 to 22:00, Lunar New Year included. Social workers speak Mandarin, Taiwanese, Hakka and English. Deaf, hard of hearing and speech-impaired people can use the LINE text service or online consultation.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

National Human Rights Commission (國家人權委員會)

National Human Rights Commission, Control Yuan

Takes complaints from people who have experienced human rights violations, torture or discrimination, and runs Taiwan's independent mechanism for monitoring the Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (CRPD).

Next step: File a complaint online through the NHRC Complaint Filing System, by email or post using the complaint form, or in person at the Complaint Receipt Center on the 1st floor of the Control Yuan (weekdays 9:00-12:00 and 13:30-17:00).

Crisis and someone to talk to

1925 Anxin (peace of mind) line (安心專線)

Ministry of Health and Welfare

Free psychological counselling by phone, 24 hours a day, every day of the year.

Next step: Call 1925 from a landline or mobile, at any hour. It is free. The Ministry's page is in Chinese.
