Disability help in the Maldives

Emergency in the Maldives: call 102 (Ambulance. Police: 119; fire: 118).

Disability payments and benefits

National Disability Registry and Disability Allowance

National Social Protection Agency

A monthly cash allowance for Maldivians with disabilities who are registered in the National Disability Registry under the Disability Act (8/2010). It is not means-tested and has no conditions or contributions. It is run by the National Social Protection Agency.

Next step: Register in the National Disability Registry and apply through the oneGov portal, or ask the National Social Protection Agency.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Human Rights Commission of the Maldives (HRCM)

Human Rights Commission of the Maldives

Takes human rights complaints and is listed on its own disability page among the agencies that assist persons with disabilities.

Next step: Call the 1424 hotline, submit a complaint through the website, or visit the office in Malé (08:00-14:00).

Crisis and someone to talk to

National Mental Health Helpline 1677

National Mental Health Department

A free and confidential helpline answered 24/7 by the National Mental Health Department, for anyone who needs support or someone to listen.

Next step: Call 1677 at any hour. In an emergency, call 119 for the police or 102 for an ambulance.
