Disability help in Greece

Emergency in Greece: call 112 (The single European emergency number for the police, fire service, coastguard and ambulance (EKAV), free and open 24 hours a day. Operators answer in Greek, English and French).

Calling without your voice? Deaf or speech-impaired: you can also reach 112 by text message (SMS or MMS), or by email at contact@112.gov.gr.

Disability payments and benefits

Mobility allowance (επίδομα κίνησης)

OPEKA (Organisation of Welfare Benefits and Social Solidarity)

A welfare allowance for people certified with a disability of 80% or more because of amputation of both legs or both arms, or paralysis of both legs, who live legally and permanently in Greece and do not get a mobility allowance from another body.

Next step: Apply in person or by post through a municipal Community Centre (Κέντρο Κοινότητας) or an OPEKA regional office, with your ID, a signed declaration, a Greek bank account in your name and your disability certification (the KEPA decision). It cannot be done online. It is free. In Greek.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Line 197 for urgent psychosocial support

National Centre for Social Solidarity (EKKA)

A free national line for adults and families in crisis, answered by social workers and psychologists 24 hours a day, every day of the year. It offers counselling and emotional support, information on welfare and mental health services, and can arrange urgent social help. It also takes reports of neglect or abuse of older or disabled adults.

Next step: Call 197, free from landlines and mobiles, at any hour.

Suicide intervention line 1018

Klimaka

A suicide intervention line open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Next step: Call 1018 from a mobile or landline anywhere in Greece, at the local call rate.
