Tanzania Federation of Disabled People's Organisations (SHIVYAWATA)

SHIVYAWATA

The national federation of organisations of persons with disabilities. It speaks for disabled people on policy and law.

Hatua inayofuata: Call or email the federation in Dar es Salaam.

Piga 0718 645 526shivyawata.or.tzinfo@shivyawata.or.tz

Imekaguliwa 2026-11-21 kwenye shivyawata.or.tz.

Tanzania League of the Blind

A national membership organisation for people with visual impairment. It advocates for their rights and supports education, assistive technology, digital skills and job training.

Hatua inayofuata: Call or email, Monday to Saturday, 9am to 6pm. The office is on Chui Street, Kariakoo, Dar es Salaam.

Piga 0655 688 833tlb.or.tzinfo@tlb.or.tz

Ministry of Health line 199

Ministry of Health (Wizara ya Afya)

The Ministry of Health's free line, which has a psychological support desk where you can get counselling. Most information is in Swahili.

Hatua inayofuata: Call 199 for free, or send a text message to 15061.

Piga 199

Imekaguliwa 2026-11-21 kwenye moh.go.tz.

National Child Helpline 116

C-Sema

A free, confidential line open 24 hours for children and young people, and for adults worried about a child.

Hatua inayofuata: Call 116, or send a WhatsApp message to +255 624 100 100.

Piga 116
