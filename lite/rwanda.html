Disability help in Rwanda

Emergency in Rwanda: call 112 (Emergency. For an ambulance, call 912; for traffic accidents, call 113).

Where to start

National Council of Persons with Disabilities (NCPD)

National Council of Persons with Disabilities

The national body for disability advocacy and for including disabled people in mainstream services. Through IremboGov it issues free recommendation letters that support disabled people in taking part in activities that benefit them.

Next step: Email info@ncpd.gov.rw, or visit the council in Kacyiru, Kigali. To get a recommendation letter, apply on IremboGov under Governance, NCPD Services, with your NCPD disability card and a document showing the reason; it takes 2 days and no Irembo account is needed.

ncpd.gov.rwinfo@ncpd.gov.rw

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

National Commission for Human Rights (NCHR)

National Commission for Human Rights, Republic of Rwanda

Receives, examines and investigates complaints about human rights violations; its monitoring covers persons with disabilities among other groups.

Next step: File a complaint online through the 'file a complaint' option on the Commission's website, or call the toll-free line 3430.

Call 3430cndp.org.rwinfo@cndp.org.rw

Crisis and someone to talk to

Health call centre 114

Rwanda Biomedical Centre (RBC)

RBC's mental health service tells people to call 114, its call centre with operators trained in different health fields. It is open Monday to Saturday, not around the clock.

Next step: Call 114, Monday to Saturday. RBC's page does not give the times. In an emergency, call 112.
