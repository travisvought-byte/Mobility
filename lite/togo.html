Disability help in Togo

Français

Emergency in Togo: call 8200 (Ambulance. Police: 117 or 177; fire: 118).

Equipment, home changes and getting around

Wheelchairs and tricycles, and the disability directorate

Ministry of Social Action

The government told the UN disability committee in 2023 that since 2012 it has handed out nearly 350 wheelchairs and tricycles a year to people with disabilities living in poverty, and that the social action ministry has a directorate for the protection of persons with disabilities. It also funds centres that teach children with disabilities.

Next step: Ask the social action ministry’s directorate for the protection of persons with disabilities, or your regional social action office.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Allô 1011 green line

Government of Togo

A free national line, open 24 hours a day, that takes reports of violence against women, refers callers to help and arranges care. UNICEF reports that the platform also gives psychosocial support and online counselling on sexual and reproductive health. It is a violence line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 1011, free, any hour. In an emergency, call 117 (police) or 8200 (ambulance).

Gender-based violence line 8284

Government of Togo

A toll-free line the government set up for reporting cases of gender-based violence, named in Togo’s 2024 progress report to UN Women. Its hours are not stated. It is a violence line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 8284, free. In an emergency, call 117 (police).
