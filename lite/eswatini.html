Disability help in Eswatini

Emergency in Eswatini: call 999 (Police. Ambulance: 977; fire: 933).

Where to start

Social Welfare Department

Deputy Prime Minister’s Office

The government department for social welfare services to vulnerable groups, including persons with disabilities, with regional offices across the country. Eswatini pays disability grants: a 2026 UN report counts about E496 million spent on disability and elderly grants in 2024/25, and names a Disability Unit in the Deputy Prime Minister’s Office.

Next step: Call the head office in Mbabane on 2404 2726, or a regional office: Mbabane 2404 4640, Manzini 2505 6444, Lubombo (Siteki) 3434 457.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Commission on Human Rights and Public Administration

Government of Eswatini

Investigates complaints of violations of constitutional rights and freedoms, and complaints of injustice, abuse of power or unfair treatment by public officers or failures in public services.

Next step: File a complaint in writing through the office of the commissioner at Mbabane Office Park, Sibekelo Building (P.O. Box 8795, Mbabane), or call the office.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Emergency lines 999 and 977 for counselling and support

Listed by the World Health Organization in Eswatini

For emotional distress, depression, anxiety or thoughts of suicide, WHO in Eswatini says to go to your nearest health facility or call the toll-free emergency line 999 or 977 for counselling and support (November 2026). These are the country’s emergency numbers; no separate mental health line could be confirmed.

Next step: Call 977 or 999, free, or go to your nearest health facility.
