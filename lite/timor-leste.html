Disability help in Timor-Leste

Emergency in Timor-Leste: call 112 (Police (or +670 331 1380). Ambulance: 110; fire: 115. The UK government says these numbers are unreliable).

Disability payments and benefits

Subsidy for older persons and persons with disabilities

National Institute of Social Security (INSS)

A government subsidy paid to older people and persons with disabilities, under the responsibility of the National Institute of Social Security. The Ombudsman for Human Rights and Justice monitored payments in Oé-Cusse in September 2025.

Next step: Ask the National Institute of Social Security (INSS) or your local administrative post about the subsidy.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Provedor de Direitos Humanos e Justiça (PDHJ, Ombudsman for Human Rights and Justice)

Provedoria dos Direitos Humanos e Justiça

Takes complaints from people whose rights were violated by a decision, action or official of a public authority, and reports of poor governance.

Next step: Complain online, phone the Dili headquarters, write to or visit Dili or a regional office (Baucau, Maliana, RAEOA, Manufahi), or use the complaint box at district administration offices.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Mental health line 12123

Listed by UNICEF

A free mental health line (Liña Kontaktu Saúde Mental) on the child protection referral network contact list that UNICEF published in 2022. It answers Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 17:30, not at night or at weekends.

Next step: Call 12123, free, Monday to Friday. In an emergency, call 112 (police) or 110 (ambulance).
