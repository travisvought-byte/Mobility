Disability help in Hungary

Emergency in Hungary: call 112 (The free European emergency number; tell the operator exactly where you are. Deaf or hard of hearing people can send an SMS to the police on +36 20 9000 107).

Disability payments and benefits

Fogyatékossági támogatás (Disability support)

Magyar Államkincstár (Hungarian State Treasury) and the government offices (kormányhivatal)

A monthly support payment for severely disabled adults aged 18 or older, covering motor, visual, hearing and intellectual disability, severe autism and chromosomal disorders.

Next step: Submit the application form with a GP referral and medical proof to the government office for your area, in person at a kormányablak, by post or electronically; call 1811 for information.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

MEOSZ (National Federation of Associations of People with Physical Disabilities)

Mozgáskorlátozottak Egyesületeinek Országos Szövetsége

A national federation that speaks up for people with mobility impairments: it puts proposals on benefits to the government and has reported to the Ombudsman on wheelchair access to trains.

Next step: Call 06-1-388-2387 or 06-1-388-2388, email meosz@meosz.hu, or write to 1032 Budapest, San Marco u. 76. The site does not list opening hours. In Hungarian.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Telephone emotional first aid (Lelki Elsősegély Telefonszolgálat) 116-123

Magyar Lelki Elsősegély Telefonszolgálatok Szövetsége (LESZ)

Someone to talk to when you are in crisis or need emotional support, 24 hours a day, every day.

Next step: Call 116-123, at any hour. It is free from every network inside Hungary. In Hungarian. You can also write in English to the email address the association gives on its site; it says it replies within 72 hours.
