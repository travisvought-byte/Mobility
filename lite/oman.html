Disability help in Oman

العربية

Emergency in Oman: call 9999 (Emergency).

Where to start

Card for persons with disabilities

Government of Oman (gov.om)

The card is the way in to other services: with it you can request assistive devices, a disabled parking permit, vocational rehabilitation and employment opportunities. It is free and takes 14 working days.

Next step: First get a medical examination and a medical report from a Ministry of Health centre giving the type and level of the disability and when it began. Then apply with the medical examination form, the report, the card application form and the disabled person's data form.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Oman Human Rights Commission (OHRC)

Oman Human Rights Commission

Receives and follows up reports of human rights issues in Oman, and runs work on the rights of persons with disabilities such as sign language programmes and training on the disability convention.

Next step: Submit a report through the online complaint form (written or voice) on the Commission's website.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Al Masarra Hospital psychiatric emergency

Ministry of Health

The Ministry of Health's psychiatric hospital in Muscat, open around the clock. Its emergency department supports people in a mental health crisis or with addiction withdrawal. This is a hospital emergency line, not a counselling helpline.

Next step: Call 2487 3268, the hospital's number for psychiatry and addiction emergencies. The hospital is in Al-Heshya, Al Amerat, Muscat Governorate. In an emergency, call 9999.
