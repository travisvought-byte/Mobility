Allocations et aides liées au handicap

Annual allowance for children with disabilities

Ministry of Social Affairs

Children with disabilities counted in the national census of December 2023 can receive an annual allowance of 75,000 FCFA and use existing social protection, UNICEF reported in December 2024. The government also said it would put the disability card (carte d’invalidité) into effect.

Étape suivante : Ask the Ministry of Social Affairs, or your local social affairs office, whether your child is registered.

Free line 1404 for violence against women

Government of Gabon

A free number, working since April 2021, that the government set up so people can report violence and victims can get support; it took more than 2,000 calls in its first nine months, Gabon told the UN women’s rights committee in 2022. Its hours are not stated. It is a violence line, not a general mental health line.

Étape suivante : Call 1404, free. In an emergency, call 117 (ambulance) or 177 (police).
