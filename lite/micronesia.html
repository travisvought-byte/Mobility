Emergency in the Federated States of Micronesia: call 911 (Ambulance, fire and police).

Disability payments and benefits

Social Security disability benefits

FSM Social Security Administration

Monthly disability benefits for workers who cannot do any substantial paid work because of a medical condition expected to last at least 12 months, and who have been disabled for at least three full calendar months. You must be fully insured (from 2010, at least 45 quarters and $1,500 in contributions) and currently insured (20 of the last 25 quarters).

Next step: Visit your state’s Social Security branch office with proof of age, your doctors’ and clinics’ details and your work history for the past 15 years; staff can help with the form.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Behavioral health office

Department of Health and Social Affairs

The national Department of Health and Social Affairs’ behavioral health office, which promotes mental health services and runs community mental health, substance abuse prevention and treatment, and opioid prevention programs. Its hours are not stated, so it may answer only in working hours. It is a government office, not a crisis line.

Next step: Call 320 2619. In an emergency, call the emergency number at the top of this page.

Tongen Inepwineu Counselling Centre (Chuuk)

Chuuk Women’s Council, listed by the United Nations

A 24-hour counselling line run by the Chuuk Women’s Council in Weno, Chuuk State, with advocacy, counselling, and help getting to other services. The United Nations lists it for people facing domestic violence. It serves Chuuk State; it is a violence support line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 930 3495 or 930 3492, any hour. The centre’s landline is 330 3657. In an emergency, call the emergency number at the top of this page.

Call 930 3495
