Emergency in Nepal: call 100 (Police. Tourists can call the toll-free Tourist Police hotline, 1144).

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

National Federation of the Disabled Nepal (NFDN)

National Federation of the Disabled Nepal

The national umbrella body of organisations of persons with disabilities, with more than 350 member organisations, working to promote and protect disabled people's rights. Its site has a grievance and feedback channel.

Ministry of Health and Population, at the Mental Hospital, Lagankhel (operated by TPO Nepal with WHO support)

Counsellors answer round the clock. Calls through the national telecom operator are free; callers on private operators pay standard rates.
