Disability help in Senegal

Français

Emergency in Senegal: call 15 (Ambulance. Police: 17; fire: 18; in the countryside the military police on 123 may respond faster).

Where to start

Carte d'égalité des chances (equal opportunity card)

Ministère de la Santé et de l'Action sociale

A card created by the 2010 social orientation law on disability (loi d’orientation sociale n° 2010-15). It gives people with disabilities access to seven basic services: health, rehabilitation, education, training, transport, employment and finance. The Ministry of Health and Social Action issued 23,037 cards in 2024 and also supplies assistive devices through its rehabilitation programme.

Next step: Ask the social action service (service de l’action sociale) in your department, or the Ministry of Health and Social Action.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Médiateur de la République (Ombudsman of the Republic)

Médiature de la République du Sénégal

Independent authority that takes written complaints from any person who believes a public body (state administration, local authority or public service organisation) failed them; referral is free.

Next step: Send a written complaint (réclamation) or visit 22 Rue Vincens angle Faidherbe, Dakar, Monday to Friday 8:00-16:00.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Child protection line 116

A line for reporting violence against and exploitation of children (also promoted as Wallu Allo 116), listed in the government's child protection reports. Its hours and cost are not stated. It is not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 116. In an emergency, call 17 for the police or 15 for an ambulance.
