Disability help in Lithuania

Emergency in Lithuania: call 112 (Emergency, when there is an actual threat to life, health, safety, the environment or property. It works without a SIM card and with an empty account).

Where to start

Agency for the Protection of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities

Ministry of Social Security and Labour

The state agency for the rights of persons with disabilities, under the Ministry of Social Security and Labour.

Next step: Call +370 5 233 3320 or write to Švitrigailos St. 11E, LT-03228 Vilnius. The ministry page does not give opening hours.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Equal Opportunities Ombudsperson

Office of the Equal Opportunities Ombudsperson

Investigates discrimination complaints, including discrimination because of disability, and must answer within three months.

Next step: Send a written complaint within three months of what happened: by the electronic form, email lygybe@lygybe.lt, post, or in person at S. Konarskis st. 35, Vilnius. Phone +370 5 205 0640, Monday to Thursday 8:00 to 17:00 and Friday 8:00 to 15:45, break 12:00 to 12:45.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Vilties linija (Hope line) 116 123

Vilties linija

Emotional support if you are feeling mentally unwell, by phone 24 hours a day, every day. UNHCR's Lithuania help page also lists Jaunimo linija for young people (+370 800 28888, 24 hours).

Next step: Call 116 123, at any hour. There is also an online chat in Lithuanian. If you or someone else has acute thoughts of suicide, call 112.
