Disability help in New Zealand

Emergency in New Zealand: call 111 (Police, fire and ambulance).

Calling without your voice? Deaf, hard of hearing or speech disability: use New Zealand Relay to reach any number listed here.

Where to start

Disability Support Services

Ministry of Social Development

The government's disability support service, including needs assessment (through NASC), Enabling Good Lives, carer support, and equipment and modification services. Your first point of contact is usually your provider, your NASC or your Enabling Good Lives site.

Next step: Call Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 8am to 5pm, Wednesday 9:30am to 5pm, or email info@disabilitysupport.govt.nz.

Call 0800 566 601disabilitysupport.govt.nzinfo@disabilitysupport.govt.nz

Disability payments and benefits

Work and Income: Disability Allowance and Supported Living Payment

Work and Income (Ministry of Social Development)

Disability Allowance is a weekly payment for regular, ongoing costs because of a disability; you do not have to be on a benefit to get it. Supported Living Payment is for people permanently and severely restricted in their ability to work because of a health condition, injury or disability, or who are totally blind.

Next step: Call Monday to Friday 7am to 6pm, or Saturday 8am to 1pm. A health practitioner fills in a medical certificate for either payment.

Call 0800 559 009workandincome.govt.nz

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Te Kāhui Tika Tangata Human Rights Commission

Human Rights Commission

A free, confidential information and dispute resolution service for discrimination complaints, including disability discrimination under the Human Rights Act 1993.

Next step: Call the InfoLine or use the online form on the Commission's website.
