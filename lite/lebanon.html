Disability help in Lebanon

العربية

Emergency in Lebanon: call 112 (Police. Ambulance: 140; fire: 175).

Where to start

Personal Disability Card

Ministry of Social Affairs

The Ministry of Social Affairs' disability card, issued and renewed at its eight Rights and Access Centres. Holders registered in the Rights and Access programme could get the National Disability Allowance, which started in 2023 as a pilot paying US$40 a month for 12 months.

Next step: Visit a Rights and Access Centre of the Ministry of Social Affairs to get or renew the card and to ask whether the allowance is open.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

الهيئة الوطنية لحقوق الإنسان (National Human Rights Commission)

National Human Rights Commission of Lebanon

Lebanon's national human rights institution; it takes human rights complaints and has a committee on the rights of persons with disabilities.

Next step: Submit a complaint on the online form (victim, or a representative or family member with consent), or call the office or hotline in Beirut.

Crisis and someone to talk to

1564, the Lifeline

Ministry of Public Health, with Embrace

Lebanon's national hotline for psychological support and suicide prevention, run with Embrace and available 24/7, for anyone in severe distress or thinking about self-harm or suicide. Staff are trained and supervised by mental health professionals and can refer you to mental health services.

Next step: Call 1564 at any hour. In an emergency, call 112, or 140 for an ambulance.
