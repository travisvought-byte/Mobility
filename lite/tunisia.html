Disability help in Tunisia

العربية

Emergency in Tunisia: call 190 (Ambulance; police in towns 197, National Guard in rural areas 193, fire 198).

Where to start

Disability card

Ministry of Social Affairs

The disability card is requested from your local Social Development Unit, by the person with a disability or a guardian. A social worker prepares a study of your social and economic situation, and the Regional Committee for Persons with Disabilities examines the file.

Next step: Apply at your local Social Development Unit.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Green line 1809

Ministry of Family, Women, Childhood and Elderly Persons

A free green line for listening, psychological help and guidance for children and families, staffed by psychologists. When UNICEF announced it in April 2020 it answered every day from 8:00 to midnight.

Next step: Call 1809. In an emergency, call 190 for an ambulance or 197 for the police.
