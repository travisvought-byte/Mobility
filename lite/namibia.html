Disability help in Namibia

Emergency in Namibia: call 10111 (Police emergency).

Disability payments and benefits

Disability Grant

Ministry of Gender Equality, Poverty Eradication and Social Welfare

A monthly cash grant of N$1,600 for Namibian citizens or permanent residents aged 16 to 59 whom a state doctor has diagnosed as temporarily or permanently disabled. Children with disabilities can get a separate Special Maintenance Grant.

Next step: Apply in person at any district or constituency office of the ministry, weekdays 8:00 to 17:00. Bring certified copies of your birth certificate and ID (and marriage or citizenship certificate if they apply) and the medical report from a state doctor. An official fills in the form and gives you an acknowledgement of receipt.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Office of the Ombudsman

Office of the Ombudsman, Namibia

Investigates complaints from individuals about human rights violations and maladministration in the public sector.

Next step: Lodge a complaint in person, by phone or email, through the online complaint form, by Facebook message or by SMS.

Crisis and someone to talk to

LifeLine/ChildLine toll-free line

LifeLine/ChildLine Namibia

The Namibian Police list LifeLine/ChildLine's toll-free numbers among their emergency contacts, which they describe as available 24/7, confidential and with support in several languages.

Next step: Call 116 or 106, free. The police page also lists the gender-based violence toll-free line, 061 209 5226. In an emergency, call 10111.
