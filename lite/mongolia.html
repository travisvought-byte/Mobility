Disability help in Mongolia

Emergency in Mongolia: call 105 (Emergency response. Fire: 101; police: 102; ambulance: 103).

Disability payments and benefits

Social welfare pension for adults with disabilities

Ministry of Family, Labour and Social Protection

Under the Law on Social Welfare (article 12.1.3), adults with disabilities who have lost 50 percent or more of their ability to work can get a monthly social welfare pension. The law also provides care allowances for people caring for disabled children and for citizens who need constant care.

Next step: The law text does not say where to apply. The ministry's public relations division can be reached on +976 51 266324 or international@mlsp.gov.mn.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

National Human Rights Commission of Mongolia

Independent national human rights institution that receives complaints about human rights violations; children and people with limited legal capacity can complain through a guardian or representative.

Next step: File a complaint in writing or in person, or through the Commission's official email or hotline, generally within two years of the violation.

Crisis and someone to talk to

108 Child Helpline

General Authority for Child and Family Development and Protection

Mongolia's children's emergency line, under the Ministry of Family, Labour and Social Protection. In December 2025 the government merged child protection response services and temporary shelter into the 108 centre, which gives rapid child protection help, counselling and information. No general crisis line for adults could be confirmed on an official page.

Next step: Call 108. The ministry's pages do not give its hours. In an emergency, call 105.
