Disability help in Brunei

Emergency in Brunei: call 991 (Ambulance. Police: 993; fire: 995; search and rescue: 998).

Where to start

Community psychology services

Ministry of Health

The Ministry of Health's Community Psychology Division sees people in health centres by appointment.

Next step: Ask your nearest public or private clinic or hospital for a referral.

Disability payments and benefits

Disability allowance

Department of Community Development (JAPEM)

A monthly allowance under the Old Age and Disability Pensions law for residents aged 15 or older who are unable to work because of a physical or mental disability, including blind people, with extra for some dependants. It is run by the Department of Community Development (JAPEM) of the Ministry of Culture, Youth and Sports. Check the current amount with JAPEM.

Next step: Ask the Department of Community Development (JAPEM).

Crisis and someone to talk to

Talian Harapan 145

Ministry of Health

The Ministry of Health's hope line for emotional and crisis support and information about mental health services.

Next step: Call 145. In an emergency, call 991 for an ambulance or 993 for the police.
