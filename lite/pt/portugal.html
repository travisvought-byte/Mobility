Information and mediation for people with disabilities

Instituto Nacional para a Reabilitação (INR)

Free advice from the national rehabilitation institute on the rights, benefits and services open to disabled people and their families, with referral and mediation when you are dealing with public or private bodies.

Próximo passo: Call 217 929 500 or 215 952 770, or go in person to the INR or to a town council that works with it. Services are in Portuguese.

Pagamentos e benefícios por deficiência

Social Benefit for Inclusion (Prestação Social para a Inclusão)

Segurança Social (social security)

A monthly payment for disabled people with a certified degree of incapacity of 60% or more, with an extra component for people on low incomes. It is open from birth; if you apply at 55 or over, the disability must have begun before 55. You need the multi-purpose medical incapacity certificate (Atestado Médico de Incapacidade Multiuso), or proof that you have asked for it.

Próximo passo: Apply on the Segurança Social portal after you sign in, at any Segurança Social service desk, or by post to the district centre for where you live. The Segurança Social line is open weekdays, 9:00 to 18:00 (also 300 502 502). In Portuguese.

Equipamentos, adaptações em casa e mobilidade

Parking card for people with disabilities (Cartão de estacionamento)

Instituto da Mobilidade e dos Transportes (IMT)

A parking card that belongs to the person, not the car, so it can be used in any vehicle carrying you. It lets the vehicle use spaces reserved for disabled people. It is for people with a permanent motor or physical disability, an intellectual disability or autism, each at 60% incapacity or more, a visual impairment of 95% or more, and some others.

Próximo passo: Apply online on IMTonline (sign in with your Citizen Card, Chave Móvel Digital or tax login), choose the parking card under “Outros”, and upload your proof as a PDF. The card is posted to you. In Portuguese.

Direitos, discriminação e defesa

Complaints about disability discrimination

Instituto Nacional para a Reabilitação (INR)

The INR takes complaints about discrimination because of disability and about places that do not meet accessibility rules, and passes them to the body that must act. You can also write in the complaints book (Livro de Reclamações) of any place that serves the public.

Próximo passo: Fill in the complaint form on the INR’s website and email it to inr@inr.mtsss.pt. In Portuguese.

Crise e alguém com quem conversar

SNS 24 psychological counselling line

SNS 24, Portugal’s national health service line

Counselling by phone from psychologists, open 24 hours a day, every day, through the national health line. Since 2022 it also answers in English.

Próximo passo: Call 808 24 24 24 and choose option 4. In Portuguese or English.
