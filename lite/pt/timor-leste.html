Subsidy for older persons and persons with disabilities

National Institute of Social Security (INSS)

A government subsidy paid to older people and persons with disabilities, under the responsibility of the National Institute of Social Security. The Ombudsman for Human Rights and Justice monitored payments in Oé-Cusse in September 2025.

Próximo passo: Ask the National Institute of Social Security (INSS) or your local administrative post about the subsidy.

Provedoria dos Direitos Humanos e Justiça

Takes complaints from people whose rights were violated by a decision, action or official of a public authority, and reports of poor governance.

Próximo passo: Complain online, phone the Dili headquarters, write to or visit Dili or a regional office (Baucau, Maliana, RAEOA, Manufahi), or use the complaint box at district administration offices.

Crise e alguém com quem conversar

Mental health line 12123

Listed by UNICEF

A free mental health line (Liña Kontaktu Saúde Mental) on the child protection referral network contact list that UNICEF published in 2022. It answers Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 17:30, not at night or at weekends.

Próximo passo: Call 12123, free, Monday to Friday. In an emergency, call 112 (police) or 110 (ambulance).
