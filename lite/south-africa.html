Disability help in South Africa

Emergency in South Africa: call 10111 (Police. From a cellphone, 112. Ambulance, 10177).

Disability payments and benefits

Disability Grant

South African Social Security Agency (SASSA)

A monthly grant if a physical or mental disability makes you unfit to work for more than six months. It is temporary (6 to 12 months) or permanent, depending on a medical assessment by a SASSA doctor. A caregiver of a child under 18 who needs permanent care can apply for the Care Dependency Grant instead.

Next step: Apply in person at your nearest SASSA office; the form is not online. Bring your ID and any medical reports. Applying is free. Email: GrantEnquiries@sassa.gov.za.

Call 0800 60 10 11gov.zaGrantEnquiries@sassa.gov.za

Care Dependency Grant

South African Social Security Agency (SASSA)

A monthly grant for the parent or caregiver of a child under 18 with a severe disability who needs full-time care. It is means tested, and a medical assessment must confirm the disability.

Next step: Apply free at your nearest SASSA office with your ID, the child's birth certificate and the medical reports. Forms are not online.

Call 0800 60 10 11sassa.gov.zaGrantEnquiries@sassa.gov.za

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

South African Human Rights Commission

Takes complaints about human rights violations, including against people with disabilities, which is one of its focus areas. Complaining is free, and there are offices in all nine provinces.

Next step: Use the online complaint form, email complaints@sahrc.org.za, call the head office, or visit a provincial office.

Call 011 877 3600sahrc.org.zacomplaints@sahrc.org.za

Crisis and someone to talk to

Suicide Crisis Helpline

South African Depression and Anxiety Group

A toll-free crisis line, open any hour, for anyone thinking about suicide or worried about someone.

Next step: Call toll-free from any network.
