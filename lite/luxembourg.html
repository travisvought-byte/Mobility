Disability help in Luxembourg

Français

Emergency in Luxembourg: call 112 (Fire brigade or ambulance, free of charge, 24 hours a day, from landlines and mobiles. Police: 113).

Disability payments and benefits

Allowance for the severely disabled (RPGH)

National Solidarity Fund (FNS) and the ADEM Medical Commission

An income for adults living in Luxembourg whose disability is so severe that they cannot work: at least 30 percent reduced work capacity before age 65, and work either medically ruled out or impossible to adapt. Its gross amount equals the social inclusion income (REVIS); other income counts against it. Non-EU, EEA or Swiss nationals need 5 years of legal residence in the last 20.

Next step: Get the application form from the secretariat of the Medical Commission at ADEM, then email it with a recent medical report and the other documents to commissionmedicale@adem.etat.lu. The Commission decides within 2 months, then the FNS checks age, residence and income. ADEM: 247 88 888. FNS: 49 10 81 1.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Centre for Equal Treatment (CET)

Centre pour l'égalité de traitement

An independent body that promotes, analyses and monitors equal treatment without discrimination on grounds including disability, race or ethnic origin, gender, sexual orientation, religion or belief, and age.

Next step: Email info@cet.lu, call 28 37 36 35, or write to 65, route d'Arlon, L-1140 Luxembourg. You can write in Luxembourgish, French, German or English.
