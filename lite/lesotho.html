Disability help in Lesotho

Emergency in Lesotho: call 112 (Ambulance, fire and police).

Disability payments and benefits

Disability grant

Ministry of Gender, Youth and Social Development

A government grant for persons with disabilities, set up under the Persons with Disability Equity Act 2021 and paid after a medical assessment. In March 2026 the ministry announced that the grant would be paid monthly, at M650, instead of M1,800 every quarter, and that about 2,400 of the 3,200 people assessed as qualifying would receive it because of the budget limit.

Next step: Ask the Ministry of Gender, Youth and Social Development about the medical assessment and how to be considered for the grant.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Office of the Ombudsman

Office of the Ombudsman, Lesotho

Receives and investigates complaints from anyone who feels wronged by a government agency, statutory corporation or their officials.

Next step: Contact the office at Moposo House, Maseru, by phone or email.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Child Helpline Lesotho 116

Ministry of Social Development

A free line open 24 hours a day, managed by the Ministry of Social Development and relaunched in 2022, where children, parents and guardians can report violence against a child and get help. It is a children's line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 116 at any hour. In an emergency, call 112.
