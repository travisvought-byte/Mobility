Disability help in Zambia

Emergency in Zambia: call 999 (Police, fire and ambulance).

Where to start

Zambia Agency for Persons with Disabilities (ZAPD)

Zambia Agency for Persons with Disabilities

The government agency set up under the Persons with Disabilities Act, 2012. It keeps the national register of persons with disabilities and can run schemes for self-employment and jobs.

Next step: Contact the head office on Leopards Hill Road, Lusaka, to register or to ask about its programmes.

Call +260 211 265 233Head.Office@zapd.org.zm

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Human Rights Commission

Human Rights Commission of Zambia

Investigates human rights complaints for free, including from persons with disabilities. The Persons with Disabilities Act, 2012 bans discrimination because of disability.

Next step: Call the toll-free line 8181 from Airtel, MTN or Zamtel, send a WhatsApp message to 0954 443 010, email complaints@hrc.org.zm, or visit a provincial office.

Call 8181hrc.org.zmcomplaints@hrc.org.zm

Crisis and someone to talk to

Lifeline Zambia 933

Lifeline/Childline Zambia

A free 24-hour counselling line. Lifeline/Childline Zambia also does crisis support and suicide prevention work. Children can call Childline on 116.

Next step: Call 933. It is toll-free.

Call 933
