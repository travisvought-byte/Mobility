Disability help in Russia

Emergency in Russia: call 112 (Ambulance, fire and police).

Equipment, home changes and getting around

Technical means of rehabilitation (TSR)

Social Fund of Russia (SFR)

People with a recognised disability get the equipment listed in their individual rehabilitation programme (IPRA), such as wheelchairs and other aids, from the Social Fund. The fund buys the item and you collect it or have it delivered at no cost, or you can take an electronic certificate (valid for a year for most items) and buy it yourself, paying any difference. Equipment bought on your own after January 1, 2025 is no longer refunded.

Next step: Apply through the Gosuslugi portal, at a multifunctional centre (MFC) or at a Social Fund client office. The fund's national contact centre is 8-800-10-000-01.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Всероссийское общество инвалидов (ВОИ) (All-Russian Society of Disabled People)

All-Russian Society of Disabled People

A national membership organization of people with disabilities with UN ECOSOC consultative status; its site offers free legal help information and guidance on registering disability and getting assistive devices.

Next step: Contact the Moscow office by phone or use the citizen appeals page on the website.

Crisis and someone to talk to

EMERCOM psychological help line

Ministry of Emergency Situations (EMERCOM of Russia)

A free, anonymous psychological help line answered around the clock by the ministry's psychologists, running since 2006. Only long-distance charges apply. The ministry also answers questions online.

Next step: Call +7 (495) 989-50-50 at any hour. In an emergency, call 112.
