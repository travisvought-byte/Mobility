Disability help in Turkey

Emergency in Turkey: call 112 (The single emergency number for police, health emergencies, fire, gendarmerie, coast guard, forest fires and AFAD, free from home, mobile and pay phones at any time).

Disability payments and benefits

Home care support (evde bakım desteği)

Ministry of Family and Social Services

Support for the care at home of a person with a severe disability (marked “ağır engelli” on the hospital disability health board report) who cannot live without another person’s help, when the household’s average income per person is below two-thirds of the monthly net minimum wage.

Next step: Apply to the Provincial Directorate of Family and Social Services or the Social Service Centre where the disabled person lives, or to the District Governorship where there is neither. You need the disability health board report, a care board report and an income test. In Turkish.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Complaints about disability discrimination

Human Rights and Equality Institution of Türkiye (TİHEK) and the Ombudsman Institution (KDK)

If you think you were discriminated against because of disability in education, justice, health, transport, social services or similar services, you can apply to TİHEK free of charge. For discrimination by a public body you can also apply free of charge to the Ombudsman Institution (KDK). Discrimination can also be reported to the police or a prosecutor as a crime.

Next step: The ministry’s guidance page explains the routes; apply to TİHEK or the KDK directly. In Turkish.

Crisis and someone to talk to

ALO 183 Social Support Line

Ministry of Family and Social Services

Guidance and counselling about services for families, women, children, disabled and older people, and a line to report neglect, abuse or violence; urgent cases are passed to emergency teams. Open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Next step: Call 183, free from inside Turkey. Calls are also answered in Kurdish and Arabic. Deaf and speech-impaired callers can make a sign-language video call to 0 549 381 0 183 (weekdays 8:00 to 24:00, Saturdays 8:00 to 17:00) or send a free SMS to 183 with name, ID number and request.
