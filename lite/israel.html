Disability help in Israel

Emergency in Israel: call 101 (Magen David Adom ambulance. Police: 100. Fire and rescue: 102).

Calling without your voice? Deaf or speech-impaired: you can reach Magen David Adom by text message at 052-7000101 or by fax at 1-800-500-101.

Disability payments and benefits

General disability pension

National Insurance Institute (Bituach Leumi)

A monthly pension for residents from 18 to retirement age who do not work or earn little, with a medical disability of at least 60% (or 40% with several impairments) set by a National Insurance doctor, and whose earning capacity is reduced by at least half.

Next step: Claim online or at a branch by appointment. The call centre is *6050, or 04-8812345. Its site explains the pension in English.

Call *6050btl.gov.il

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Commission for Equal Rights of Persons with Disabilities

Ministry of Justice, State of Israel

Handles inquiries and complaints about discrimination against disabled people and about accessibility; it can investigate, take civil or criminal action, and give advice and representation in employment matters.

Next step: Contact the Commission's public inquiries unit, or submit an inquiry or complaint through its gov.il service pages.

Crisis and someone to talk to

ERAN emotional first aid

ERAN

A volunteer emotional support line, open 24 hours a day, every day of the year, anonymously. Chat, WhatsApp and text are open from 8:00 to 2:00 in Hebrew, Arabic and Russian.

Next step: Call 1201, any hour. The phone line is in Hebrew; for other languages, use the chat or ERAN’s helpline for new immigrants.

Call 1201en.eran.org.il
