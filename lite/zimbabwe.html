Disability help in Zimbabwe

Emergency in Zimbabwe: call 999 (Police, fire and ambulance).

Where to start

Disability Affairs, Department of Social Development

Ministry of Public Service, Labour and Social Welfare

Through district Social Welfare offices it helps persons with disabilities with medical treatment vouchers (AMTO), assistive devices such as wheelchairs and crutches through the Disability Fund, loans and grants, school fees under BEAM and vocational training at National Rehabilitation Centres.

Next step: Apply at your Department of Social Welfare district office with a birth certificate, national ID and proof of disability from a government hospital, or call the toll-free line 1980.

Call 1980mpslsw.gov.zwsupport@mpslsw.gov.zw

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Zimbabwe Human Rights Commission

Takes and investigates complaints about human rights violations. The Persons with Disabilities Act, passed in 2025, bans discrimination because of disability and requires reasonable accommodation.

Next step: Lodge a complaint on the website, email info@zhrc.org.zw, or call on weekdays, 8am to 5pm.

Call +263 77 183 8656zhrc.org.zwinfo@zhrc.org.zw

Crisis and someone to talk to

Youth Advocates Zimbabwe helpline 393

Youth Advocates Zimbabwe

A free 24-hour line where young people can talk to peers about suicide, depression, stress, abuse and other worries.

Next step: Dial 393 from any mobile network, or call 0777 469 107. Children can also call Childline on 116.
