Disability help in Belgium

Emergency in Belgium: call 112 (Fire brigade, ambulance or police. For urgent police help you can also call 101).

Disability payments and benefits

Disability allowances and recognition (DG Personnes handicapées)

Federal Public Service Social Security, Directorate-General Persons with Disabilities

The federal office that recognises disability and pays allowances to disabled adults: the income replacement allowance (allocation de remplacement de revenus) if you are 18 to 66 and your earning capacity has fallen by at least two-thirds, and the integration allowance for the extra costs of taking part in daily life. It also issues certificates for social and tax benefits and the disability parking card.

Next step: Call Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday, 8:30 to 12:30, or Tuesday, 13:00 to 16:00. Your commune or CPAS can help you apply. Follow your file online in MyHandicap. In French, Dutch or German.

Call 02 202 02 02handicap.belgium.be

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Unia

Unia, the Interfederal Centre for Equal Opportunities

Belgium’s equality body. You can report discrimination because of a disability to it, for example being treated differently because you use a wheelchair.

Next step: Call free on 0800 12 800 or use the reporting form on its website. Its site has pages in English.

Call 0800 12 800unia.be

Crisis and someone to talk to

Zelfmoordlijn 1813

A free, anonymous line for anyone thinking about suicide or worried about someone, open 24 hours a day. There is also a chat in the evenings.

Next step: Call 1813, any hour. Chat runs every day from 17:00 to midnight, and on Wednesdays from noon. In Dutch.

Call 1813zelfmoord1813.be

Suicide prevention listening line (Ligne d’écoute)

Centre de Prévention du Suicide

A free, anonymous listening line run by volunteers of the Centre de Prévention du Suicide, open 24 hours a day.

Next step: Call 0800 32 123. In French.
