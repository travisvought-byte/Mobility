Disability help in Finland

Emergency in Finland: call 112 (The single emergency number for police, ambulance, fire and emergency social services, free and open 24 hours a day).

Disability payments and benefits

Disability allowance for persons aged 16 or over

Kela (the Social Insurance Institution of Finland)

A tax-free monthly payment when a doctor has diagnosed a chronic illness or disability that limits your functioning for at least 12 months and brings extra costs or a need for help or guidance. Your income and assets do not affect it. There are three rates: €110.49, €257.82 and €499.93 a month. From 1 January 2027 the lower age limit rises to 18.

Next step: Apply in the OmaKela e-service on form EV 256e with a medical statement (BC) issued in the last 6 months, or by post, by phone or at a Kela service point. Kela can pay up to 6 months back. Kela publishes its instructions in English.

Call 020 634 2650kela.fi

Equipment, home changes and getting around

Parking card for people with reduced mobility

Traficom (Finnish Transport and Communications Agency)

A personal card that lets the vehicle you are driving or riding in park in disabled spaces, in paid spaces free of charge and in some no-parking areas. A doctor must assess that you cannot move around independently, or your sight must be severely impaired; it can also be issued to someone who often has to be driven and cannot manage alone afterwards. A Finnish card can be used in all EU countries.

Next step: Get a medical certificate on Traficom form F123, then apply in Traficom’s My e-Services (Personal services, then Services for people with reduced mobility) and pay the fee there, or apply in person at an Ajovarma service point by appointment. Applications sent by post are not processed. The card arrives by post in about two weeks. You can apply for exemption from the base vehicle tax at the same time.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Non-Discrimination Ombudsman (Yhdenvertaisuusvaltuutettu)

Non-Discrimination Ombudsman

Advice and instructions about discrimination, including on the basis of disability or state of health, and help investigating suspected cases.

Next step: Call the customer service line, Tuesdays to Thursdays 10:00 to 12:00. In Finnish, Swedish or English, and other languages if needed; sign-language users can send a video by email.

Call 0295 666 817yhdenvertaisuusvaltuutettu.fi

Crisis and someone to talk to

MIELI Crisis Helpline (Kriisipuhelin)

MIELI Mental Health Finland

Crisis support by phone. The Finnish-language line is open 24 hours a day, every day; there are also lines in Swedish and English.

Next step: Call 09 2525 0111 for Finnish, at any hour. Swedish: 09 2525 0112. English: 09 2525 0116, Mondays 16:00 to 20:00 and Thursdays and Fridays 9:00 to 13:00. MIELI does not charge for calls; your operator’s normal rate applies.

Call 09 2525 0111mieli.fi
