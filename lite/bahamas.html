Disability help in the Bahamas

Emergency in the Bahamas: call 919 (Police, fire and ambulance. 911 also works).

Where to start

Department of Social Services

Ministry of Social Services

The government department whose work includes services for persons with disabilities. Its Disability Affairs Division helps persons with disabilities and their families.

Next step: Call the Department, or visit it at Aventura Plaza, John F. Kennedy Drive and Bethel Avenue, Nassau.

Call 397-8600

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

National Commission for Persons with Disabilities

The national commission for persons with disabilities. Its AccessAbility app lets you register with the Commission for free and has a way to make complaints.

Next step: Call or send a WhatsApp message to the Commission's line, or email Disabilitiescommission@bahamas.gov.bs.

Call 376-8328Disabilitiescommission@bahamas.gov.bs

Crisis and someone to talk to

Bahamas Crisis Centre

The Bahamas Crisis Centre

A 24-hour hotline, answered by volunteers, for women, men and children facing domestic violence, abuse or any other crisis.
