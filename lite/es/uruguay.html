Pagos y beneficios por discapacidad

Disability pension (pensión por invalidez)

Banco de Previsión Social (BPS)

A monthly pension for people with a total incapacity, as assessed by a BPS doctor, who have lived in Uruguay for at least 10 of the last 20 years (children are exempt from this). With a severe incapacity finding there is no means test; otherwise your own and your family’s income is checked.

Siguiente paso: Apply at a BPS branch, weekdays 9:15 to 16:00, or ask by phone: 0800 1997 from a landline or *1997 from a mobile, weekdays 8:00 to 18:00. In Spanish.

Llamar al 0800 1997bps.gub.uy

Derechos, discriminación y defensa

National Human Rights Institution and Ombudsman (INDDHH)

Institución Nacional de Derechos Humanos y Defensoría del Pueblo

Anyone can bring a complaint to the INDDHH, free of charge, when their rights have been violated, including through discrimination. It also runs a monitoring mechanism on disability issues.

Siguiente paso: Call (598 2) 1948, email denuncias@inddhh.gub.uy, or visit Bv. Artigas 1532, Montevideo; complaints are taken weekdays 10:00 to 16:00. The number shown is for urgent complaints. In Spanish.

Llamar al 091 364 764gub.uydenuncias@inddhh.gub.uy

Crisis y alguien con quien hablar

Suicide prevention line (Línea de prevención del suicidio)

Government of Uruguay (Ministry of Public Health)

A free, confidential line open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, answered by professionals such as psychologists and nurses. It offers listening, support and information, and arranges help in an emergency.

Siguiente paso: Call 0800 0767 from any phone, or *0767 from a mobile, free, at any hour. In Spanish.

Llamar al 0800 0767

From a mobile you can also dial *0767.
