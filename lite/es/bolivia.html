Pagos y beneficios por discapacidad

Monthly disability payment (Bono de 250 bolivianos)

Ministry of Labour, Employment and Social Security; Ministry of Health and Sports

A monthly payment of 250 bolivianos for people whose disability is rated severe (grave) or very severe (muy grave). It depends on being in the national disability register (SIPRUNPCD) with a disability card (carnet). In October 2025 the Ministry of Health and Sports said 80,657 people received it. The Ministry of Labour's Eustaquio Moto Méndez platform draws up the lists of people who get it.

Siguiente paso: Get assessed and registered for a disability card through the health system, then ask the Ministry of Labour's office in your department about the payment.

Derechos, discriminación y defensa

Defensoría del Pueblo (Ombudsman)

Defensoría del Pueblo del Estado Plurinacional de Bolivia

Bolivia's ombudsman takes complaints through a complaint form and its Sistema de Servicio al Pueblo, has a section on persons with disabilities, and offers service in Bolivian Sign Language.

Siguiente paso: Call the free line 800 10 8004, email correspondencia@defensoria.gob.bo, use the complaint form on the website, or visit Calle Colombia N°440, Zona San Pedro, La Paz.

Crisis y alguien con quien hablar

Familia Segura

UNICEF Bolivia

A free, confidential line run by UNICEF Bolivia since 2020, where psychologists give emotional support to children, young people and adults, including for suicidal thoughts, family problems and violence. It is open every day from 6 a.m. to midnight, not 24 hours. You can also write to it on WhatsApp at 77797667.

Siguiente paso: Call 800 11 30 40 from a landline or mobile, any day from 6 a.m. to midnight. In an emergency, call 118 for an ambulance or 110 for the police.
