Derechos, discriminación y defensa

CONAIPD (National Council for the Inclusion of Persons with Disabilities)

Consejo Nacional para la Inclusión de las Personas con Discapacidad

The national disability council. Among its free services, it advises people with disabilities on getting their right to education and follows up with the Ministry of Education and other bodies.

Siguiente paso: Email recepcion@conaipd.gob.sv to ask for an appointment, or go to Pol. Industrial Plan de La Laguna, Calle Circunvalación, Lote No. 20, Antiguo Cuscatlán, La Libertad, 7:30 to 15:30. It answers within 15 working days. Free. In Spanish.

Crisis y alguien con quien hablar

Telecentro MINSAL 131 (mental health option)

Ministerio de Salud (MINSAL)

The Ministry of Health's phone line. Its mental health campaign asks anyone who feels emotionally overwhelmed to call 131 or visit the nearest health facility, and UNICEF lists option 4 for mental health and option 5 for adolescents and young people.

Siguiente paso: Call 131, choose your area, then press 4 for mental health or 5 for adolescents and young people. The official pages do not give hours or cost. In Spanish.
