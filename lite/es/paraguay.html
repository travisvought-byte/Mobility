Por dónde empezar

Disability certificate (Certificado de Discapacidad)

SENADIS (Secretaría Nacional por los Derechos Humanos de las Personas con Discapacidad)

The certificate SENADIS issues after a multidisciplinary evaluation of a sensory, physical, intellectual or speech disability. The same Portal Paraguay services include registration in the national disability register and free-travel cards for land transport.

Siguiente paso: Register as a SENADIS user (bring a copy of your cédula to the Admissions Department; up to 20 numbers are given each day from 07:00), then ask the Disability Assessment Directorate to start the certificate. SENADIS is at Jacaranda y Yeruti, Fernando de la Mora, Monday to Friday 07:00 to 13:00. It takes 15 to 20 days after the evaluations. It can also be requested on Portal Paraguay with an electronic identity.

Derechos, discriminación y defensa

Defensoría del Pueblo (Ombudsman)

Defensoría del Pueblo de la República del Paraguay

Takes complaints from anyone who has suffered an unjust situation or a violation of their human rights.

Siguiente paso: Fill in the online complaint form (denuncia) on its website, write by email, message by WhatsApp, or visit the office in Asunción.

Crisis y alguien con quien hablar

Línea 155 Salud Mental

Ministerio de Salud Pública y Bienestar Social

Free, confidential, nationwide crisis line for people in psychological distress, at risk of suicide or needing urgent support, 24 hours a day all year. A team trained in crisis care, including psychologists and psychiatrists, gives emotional support and crisis intervention and refers people to specialised services.

Siguiente paso: Call 155, at any hour. It is free. In Spanish.
