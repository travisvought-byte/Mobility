Por dónde empezar

Recognised degree of disability (certificado de discapacidad)

Your autonomous community; IMSERSO in Ceuta and Melilla

A team assesses your disability and gives it a percentage. A degree of 33% or more is what counts as having a disability for things like income tax relief. The recognised degree is valid across Spain.

Siguiente paso: Apply to the department in charge in your autonomous community, in person, by post or online. In Ceuta and Melilla, apply to the IMSERSO regional office. In Spanish.

Pagos y beneficios por discapacidad

Non-contributory invalidity pension (PNC de invalidez)

Run by the autonomous communities; rules set by IMSERSO

A pension for people aged 18 to 64 with a disability or chronic illness of 65% or more, who have lived legally in Spain for five years (the last two in a row) and do not have enough income. In 2025 the full amount was €564.70 a month. People with 75% or more who need another person’s help get a supplement.

Siguiente paso: Apply to the social services of your autonomous community, which manages the pension. In Spanish.

Equipos, cambios en la casa y movilidad

Parking card for people with reduced mobility

Lets people with a disability and reduced mobility park in reserved spaces anywhere in Spain; a card from any region is valid nationwide. A one-year provisional card exists for people with a very serious illness who cannot wait for an assessment.

Siguiente paso: Ask the public administration that issues the card where you live. In Spanish.

Derechos, discriminación y defensa

Office for Disability Care (OADIS)

Ministerio de Derechos Sociales

Advises on and studies complaints from disabled people about discrimination in public administration, goods and services open to the public, buildings and streets, transport, cultural heritage or the justice system, when the problem reaches two or more autonomous communities. It does not cover employment, dependency benefits or matters already in court.

Siguiente paso: Send your complaint or question in writing through the online form. In Spanish.

CERMI: Spanish Committee of Representatives of Persons with Disabilities

CERMI

The national platform that speaks for disabled people in Spain, with more than 800 member organisations. It offers legal guidance to disabled people, their families and organisations that meet its requirements.

Siguiente paso: Call or email to ask about legal guidance. In Spanish.

Crisis y alguien con quien hablar

024: line for suicidal behaviour

Ministerio de Sanidad

A free, confidential national line, open 24 hours a day, every day, for anyone thinking about suicide and for family and friends. Its professionals listen, give emotional support and refer you to health services, or to 112 in an emergency.

Siguiente paso: Call 024. The line is in Spanish. Deaf callers can reach it in Spanish Sign Language through the SVisual video interpreting service.
