Por dónde empezar

Consejo Nacional de Personas con Discapacidad (CONAPDIS) (National Council of Persons with Disabilities)

Government of Costa Rica

National disability council that issues disability certification (certificación de discapacidad) and the disability card; first-time applicants attach a medical certificate or similar document from the CCSS.

Siguiente paso: Apply to CONAPDIS for disability certification with a CCSS medical certificate, epicrisis or a report from your treating specialist.

Derechos, discriminación y defensa

Defensoría de los Habitantes

Defensoría de los Habitantes de la República

The national ombudsman. It takes complaints when a public institution fails or violates people’s rights, and it is the national body that monitors the UN disability convention. Most of the disability complaints it receives concern health services, pensions and due process.

Siguiente paso: Call free on 800-258-7474 (switchboard 4000-8500), email correspondencia@dhr.go.cr, use the complaint form on its website, or visit its San José office. In Spanish.

Línea Aquí Estoy

Colegio de Profesionales en Psicología de Costa Rica

A free emotional support line from the professional college of psychologists. It is not open around the clock; in an emergency, call the emergency number.

Siguiente paso: Call 800-AQESTOY (800-2737869), Monday to Friday 14:00 to 22:00, or Saturday 9:00 to 16:00.
