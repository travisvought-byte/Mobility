Disability help in Croatia

Emergency in Croatia: call 112 (The single emergency number for police, ambulance, fire, mountain rescue and sea rescue, free and open 24 hours a day by phone or text message. You can report an emergency in Croatian, English, German, Italian, Hungarian or Slovak).

Disability payments and benefits

Inclusive allowance (inkluzivni dodatak)

Croatian Institute for Social Work (Hrvatski zavod za socijalni rad)

A monthly payment that helps disabled children and adults take part in society on an equal basis. It is paid at five levels of support: €720, €480, €432, €162 or €138 a month, depending on the degree of disability. It is not paid to people living in a care home or organised housing, or to people receiving the personal disability allowance (osobna invalidnina) or certain care allowances.

Next step: Apply to the regional office (područni ured) of the Croatian Institute for Social Work. The Institute sets your level of support after an assessment by the Institute for Medical Expertise (Zavod za vještačenje). The government page is in Croatian.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Ombudsperson for Persons with Disabilities (Pravobranitelj za osobe s invaliditetom)

Ombudsperson for Persons with Disabilities

Takes individual complaints from disabled people and people acting for them. The office reviews your case, warns anyone found violating your rights and recommends how to fix it, can ask for slow proceedings to be speeded up, and tells you which institution to turn to. In cases of discrimination, violence or abuse it reports to the state attorney.

Next step: Call 01 6102 170 or 01 6177 901, email ured@posi.hr, or write to or visit Savska cesta 41/3, 10000 Zagreb. In Croatian.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Plavi telefon helpline (Linija pomoći Plavi telefon)

Plavi telefon

A free helpline for children, young people and adults anywhere in Croatia. It is not open around the clock.

Next step: Call 01 4833 888, Monday to Friday 09:00 to 20:00. In a crisis outside those hours, call 112. The website is in Croatian.
