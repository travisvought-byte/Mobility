Disability help in Moldova

Emergency in Moldova: call 112 (Ambulance, police and firefighters, available 24/7).

Where to start

Support line for persons with disabilities

Listed on the government services portal (EVO)

Free information and psychological counselling for persons with disabilities.

Next step: Call 0800 10808 or 060 601 549.

Call 0800 10808

Crisis and someone to talk to

Free psychological support line

Listed on the government services portal (EVO)

Free phone counselling for stress, anxiety, panic attacks and other emotional crises. The portal does not give its hours.

Next step: Call 068 157 984 or 060 925 267. In an emergency, call 112.

Call 068 157 984

Child protection line 116 111

Listed on the government services portal (EVO)

A line for child protection. You can also call 0800 11116.

Next step: Call 116 111. In an emergency, call 112.

Call 116 111

Line for women and girls facing violence

Listed on the government services portal (EVO)

Confidential help for women and girls affected by any kind of abuse: physical, psychological, sexual and more.

Next step: Call 0800 88008. From abroad, call +373 22 240 624. In an emergency, call 112.
