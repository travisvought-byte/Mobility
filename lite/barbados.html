Disability help in Barbados

Emergency in Barbados: call 211 (Police. Ambulance: 511. Fire: 311).

Where to start

Register with the National Disabilities Unit

National Disabilities Unit

The government unit for persons with disabilities uses its register to plan policies and programmes. Adults must be registered with it to use Call-A-Ride, which takes persons with disabilities to hospital appointments.

Next step: Call 310-1650 and leave your contact details. Unit staff, carrying Unit picture IDs, will visit you to take the rest.

Equipment, home changes and getting around

Barbados Council for the Disabled

Helps people with all kinds of disabilities. Its services include accessible transport, sign language interpreters, Braille translation, beach wheelchairs and checks of how accessible a property is.

Next step: Call the Council. It is at Harambee House, The Garrison, St Michael.

Call 629-0574
