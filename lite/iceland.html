Disability help in Iceland

Emergency in Iceland: call 112 (The national emergency number, on call 24 hours all year round. You can also reach it by text message (SMS), the 112 Iceland app or web chat).

Disability payments and benefits

Disability pension (örorkulífeyrir)

Social Insurance Administration (Tryggingastofnun, TR)

For people aged 18 to 67 assessed, through an integrated expert assessment, as able to take part in the labour market to only 0 to 25 percent. The full pension before tax is 416,950 krónur a month, or 486,076 with the household allowance; income above 105,200 krónur a month reduces it. People already on a disability pension can ask to move to a partial disability pension.

Next step: Your doctor sends a medical certificate to TR; the application then appears on Ísland.is for you to complete and submit, and TR's letters and decision come to My Pages. TR: 560 4400, weekdays 10:00 to 15:00, Hlíðasmári 11, 201 Kópavogur.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Rights protection for people with disabilities (Réttindagæsla fatlaðs fólks)

Réttindagæsla fatlaðs fólks

Rights advocates who protect the legal and human rights of disabled people: they look into how a case was handled, attend meetings or police questioning with you, prepare complaints and appeals, and help people who need support with legal capacity or want a personal spokesperson.

Next step: Call 554-8100, weekdays 9:00 to 16:00 (you can leave a message outside those hours), or email rettindagaesla@mannrettindi.is.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Red Cross helpline 1717 (Hjálparsíminn 1717)

Icelandic Red Cross

A confidential phone line and web chat for anyone who needs someone to talk to, about anything causing concern, including anxiety, depression, suicidal thoughts, abuse and loneliness. Open 24 hours and free; ask to speak English if you do not speak Icelandic.

Next step: Call 1717 at any hour, or use the web chat. In an emergency, call 112.
