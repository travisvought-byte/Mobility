Disability help in Bulgaria

Emergency in Bulgaria: call 112 (The single European emergency number, for urgent help when life, health, safety or property is at risk).

Equipment, home changes and getting around

Agency for People with Disabilities

Agency for People with Disabilities (Ministry of Labour and Social Policy)

The national agency that funds projects for rehabilitation, integration and an accessible environment, supports economic initiative by people with disabilities, and registers and oversees providers of assistive and medical devices.

Next step: See the agency's site for its programmes and registers. The page does not give contact details.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Ombudsman of the Republic of Bulgaria

Takes complaints from people with disabilities about benefits and disability pensions, social services, accessible buildings, transport and public services, medical assessment of disability, municipal housing and parking, work and education.

Next step: File a complaint with the online form, call the reception room on 02 810 6955, email priemna@ombudsman.bg, or write to 22 George Washington Str., 1202 Sofia. The page does not list reception hours.

Crisis and someone to talk to

National Telephone Line for Children 116 111

Ministry of Labour and Social Policy

A free line that works around the clock, every day. Anyone can report a child at risk, and callers can also get consultation and psychological support. It is a line about children; Bulgaria has no general adult crisis line that could be confirmed on an official page.

Next step: Call 116 111, at any hour. It is free. Operators alert the child-protection authorities straight away when a child is at risk. In Bulgarian. For any emergency, call 112.
