Disability help in Canada

Emergency in Canada: call 911 (Police, fire and ambulance).

Disability payments and benefits

Canada Disability Benefit

Service Canada

A monthly payment for working-age people with disabilities, 18 to 64, who have low income. You must already be approved for the Disability Tax Credit.

Next step: Apply online, by phone, or at a Service Canada Centre. If you do not have the Disability Tax Credit yet, apply for it first through the Canada Revenue Agency. TTY: 1-833-467-2700.

Canada Pension Plan disability benefits

Service Canada

A monthly payment if you paid into the Canada Pension Plan and can no longer work regularly at any job because of a severe and prolonged disability. Your children may also get benefits.

Next step: Apply online, or call for an application kit. TTY: 1-800-255-4786. From outside Canada and the United States, call collect 1-613-957-1954.

Call 1-800-277-9914canada.ca

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Canadian Human Rights Commission

Takes discrimination complaints against federally regulated organizations, such as federal government departments, banks, airlines and phone companies. For other employers, landlords, stores and services, complain to your province's or territory's human rights agency. Filing is free and you do not need a lawyer.

Next step: Call to check that the Commission is the right place, then fill in its complaint form. Lines are open Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm Eastern. TTY: 1-888-643-3304.

Crisis and someone to talk to

9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline

Call or text 9-8-8 any hour, anywhere in Canada, if you are thinking about suicide or are worried about someone. Free, in English and French.

Next step: Call or text 988. If 988 does not work on your phone, call 1-833-456-4566.
