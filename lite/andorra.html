Disability help in Andorra

Emergency in Andorra: call 116 (Ambulance. Police: 110; fire: 118; mountain rescue: 112).

Disability payments and benefits

Pensió d'invalidesa (invalidity pension)

Caixa Andorrana de Seguretat Social (CASS)

A CASS pension for insured people who lose at least 60% of their earning capacity through illness or an accident outside work. It pays 50% of the theoretical retirement pension if you can keep working and 100% if you cannot. The contribution period needed depends on your age (12 months under 21, up to 120 months over 57). For a work accident or occupational illness, no contribution period is needed.

Next step: Ask CASS about a claim; you must be insured with CASS when the illness or accident happens.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Raonador del Ciutadà (Citizen's Ombudsman)

Institució del Raonador del Ciutadà del Principat d'Andorra

Andorra's ombudsman: handles complaints and claims against Andorran institutions and bodies, and does human rights promotion work.

Next step: Use the online complaint form on the website or call the free number 18003030 (Monday to Friday, 8:00-15:00).

Crisis and someone to talk to

Línia verda 177

Ministeri de Salut

Andorra's suicide prevention line, opened by the Ministry of Health on September 10, 2024 for anyone at risk of suicide or having suicidal thoughts, and part of the national suicide prevention strategy for 2025 to 2030. Its hours and cost are not stated.

Next step: Call 177. In an emergency, call 116 for an ambulance or 110 for the police.
