Disability help in Pakistan

Emergency in Pakistan: call 1122 (Rescue 1122: ambulance and fire. Police: 15).

Where to start

Identity card with the disability logo (NADRA)

National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA)

NADRA issues identity cards (CNICs) to persons with disabilities that carry the international disability logo and are valid for life. Pakistan Bait-ul-Mal accepts this card as proof of disability.

Next step: Apply at a NADRA office. NADRA has said persons with disabilities are served without queueing and that it offers registration at home.

Disability payments and benefits

Pakistan Bait-ul-Mal: help for families with a person with disabilities

Pakistan Bait-ul-Mal

Yearly cash help for families with a person with disabilities: Rs. 20,000 a year for one person with disabilities, Rs. 30,000 for two or more.

Next step: Fill in the PBM application form and attach a copy of the CNIC (or a child's B-Form) and a disability certificate or a NADRA CNIC with the disability logo.

Equipment, home changes and getting around

Pakistan Bait-ul-Mal: wheelchairs and assistive devices

Pakistan Bait-ul-Mal

Standard and customised wheelchairs, hearing aids, white canes and artificial limbs. People aged 60 and over can also get a standard wheelchair.

Next step: Apply with a CNIC copy and a disability certificate. A standard wheelchair needs a government medical officer's recommendation; hearing aids and artificial limbs need an estimate from a government hospital. In some districts, including Islamabad, customised wheelchairs are applied for online.

Punjab Assistive Devices and Wheelchair Program

Social Welfare and Bait-ul-Maal Department, Punjab

For residents of Punjab: wheelchairs, prosthetics, orthotics and other assistive devices, fitted to each person after an assessment.

Next step: Apply on the program's website or app, or at your nearest Social Welfare and Bait-ul-Maal office. You need a CNIC or B-Form and a Disability Assessment Certificate. For wheelchairs and artificial limbs, an orthopaedic specialist at a DHQ or teaching hospital assesses what you need.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Rozan Counseling Helpline

Rozan

Telephone counselling for emotional and mental health worries, with referrals to other services. Rozan also offers counselling in person, especially for women and children, and by email.

Next step: Call 0304 111 1741, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm, or email helpline@rozan.org. In an emergency, call 1122 or 15.
