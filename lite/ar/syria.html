Care programme and medical aids

UNHCR and its partners

Through its partners, UNHCR runs a care programme for persons with disabilities, older persons and their families and caregivers, to help them stay self-reliant and included, and gives some people medical aids such as crutches and wheelchairs; strict eligibility criteria apply. UNHCR services are free: never share bank details with anyone offering help.

الخطوة التالية: Ask at the UNHCR community centre nearest you; the UNHCR Syria help site lists them.

Family Protection Unit helpline

Family Protection Unit, supported by UNFPA, listed by UNHCR

A free helpline, open 24 hours, 7 days a week, for people who have experienced violence: psychological support, legal advice, referral to the nearest mobile team or medical facility where possible, and a safe shelter for survivors of gender-based violence when needed and feasible. It is a violence line, not a general mental health line.

الخطوة التالية: Call 0119416 or 0951444190, free, any hour. In an emergency, call 112 (police) or 110 (ambulance).
