مدفوعات ومزايا الإعاقة

Disability Allowance

Ministry of Social Development

A monthly allowance for Bahraini citizens (and non-Bahraini children of Bahraini mothers) living in Bahrain with a lifelong physical, mental, visual or hearing disability, autism, cerebral palsy or multiple disabilities, certified by a disability assessment committee.

الخطوة التالية: Register at any social centre with your ID, a bank statement showing your IBAN, the social service application form and an original medical report from a government hospital. It takes about 20 working days.

Bahrain's independent national human rights body; it receives and examines complaints from individuals, relatives and representatives and monitors places of detention.

Child Helpline 998

Child Protection Centre, Ministry of Social Development

A free, round-the-clock line to report a child up to 18 who is exposed to violence, abuse, neglect or danger, with counselling, listening and referral. It is for children, not a general adult crisis line.

الخطوة التالية: Call 998 at any hour, or email 998@social.gov.bh. In an emergency, call 999.
