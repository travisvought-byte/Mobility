Disability help in Trinidad and Tobago

Emergency in Trinidad and Tobago: call 999 (Police. Fire: 990. Ambulance: 811).

Disability payments and benefits

Disability Assistance Grant

Government of Trinidad and Tobago (social welfare)

A monthly grant for adults 18 and over who are certified as permanently disabled and unable to work, have an income of no more than TT$12,000 a year, and have lived in Trinidad and Tobago for at least 3 years. A separate grant pays TT$1,500 a month for children under 18 with a disability.

Next step: Get the forms at your Local Board (Social Welfare) Office. Adults need a medical report from a Government Medical Officer, national ID, birth certificate and a bank statement. For general help, call ttconnect on weekdays, 8am to 4pm.

Call 800-8826ttconnect.gov.tt

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Equal Opportunity Commission

Takes complaints of discrimination because of disability, among other grounds, in jobs, education, goods and services, and housing. Complain in writing within six months of what happened.

Next step: Use the online complaint form, email the form to complaints@eoc.gov.tt, or drop it at the office at 55-57 Manic Street, Chaguanas.

Call 672-0928equalopportunity.gov.ttcomplaints@eoc.gov.tt

Crisis and someone to talk to

Lifeline Trinidad and Tobago

A free listening line, open 24 hours, for anyone in distress or thinking about suicide. The Ministry of Health lists it among its suicide help numbers.

Next step: Call 800-5588 or 866-5433, both toll-free.
