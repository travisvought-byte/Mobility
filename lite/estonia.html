Disability help in Estonia

Emergency in Estonia: call 112 (Ambulance, police and rescue, always available and free of charge).

Disability payments and benefits

Allowance for a person of working age with disabilities

Social Insurance Board (Sotsiaalkindlustusamet)

A monthly allowance of 29 to 100 euros for people aged 16 to old-age pension age whose severity of disability has been determined. The amount depends on the severity and on which function is affected (for example 42 euros for a moderate motor disability, 50 euros for severe and 100 euros for profound).

Next step: Apply to the Social Insurance Board through its self-service portal, by digitally signed email, by post to Paldiski mnt 80, 15092 Tallinn, or at a customer service office. Information line: +372 612 1360.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Gender Equality and Equal Treatment Commissioner

Soolise võrdõiguslikkuse ja võrdse kohtlemise volinik

An independent commissioner who advises and helps people who feel discriminated against. For disability, the Equal Treatment Act protects you in work, in related education and in membership of employee or employer associations.

Next step: Call +372 626 9059, use the contact form on the commissioner's website, or write to Kiriku 4, Tallinn 15071.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Emotional support helpline 116 123

Social Insurance Board (Sotsiaalkindlustusamet)

Free emotional support by phone, in Estonian, Russian and English. It is not open around the clock: counsellors answer every day from 10:00 to midnight. The state health portal adds that spiritual counsellors answer from 16:00 to 02:00.

Next step: Call 116 123, every day 10:00 to 24:00. Outside those hours, call the victim support crisis line 116 006, which answers 24 hours a day. In an emergency, call 112.

Victim support crisis helpline 116 006

Social Insurance Board (Sotsiaalkindlustusamet)

Crisis counselling 24 hours a day, every day, in Estonian, Russian and English. The state health portal also lists it for chat and email.

Next step: Call 116 006 at any hour. In an emergency, call 112.
