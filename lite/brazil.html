Disability help in Brazil

Português

Emergency in Brazil: call 192 (Ambulance (SAMU), free from any phone at any hour).

Disability payments and benefits

BPC: continuous cash benefit for disabled people (BPC/LOAS)

INSS

Pays one minimum wage a month to disabled people whose family income is no more than a quarter of the minimum wage per person. You do not need to have paid into INSS. It is for Brazilians and Portuguese nationals, and your CadÚnico registration must have been updated in the last two years. There is no 13th payment.

Next step: Apply in the Meu INSS app or at gov.br/meuinss. If the system is down, or to book an in-person visit, call 135. Applying is free. In Portuguese.

Equipment, home changes and getting around

Digital parking credential for disabled people

Senatran (National Traffic Secretariat)

Lets you use parking spaces reserved for disabled people anywhere in Brazil. The credential is now issued digitally in the Carteira Digital de Trânsito (CDT) app, and can be linked to one vehicle at a time, yours or someone else’s.

Next step: In the CDT app, go to Condutor, then Credencial de Estacionamento, then Credencial PCD, and tap Emitir Credencial. Your disability is checked against Ministry of Human Rights and INSS records. In Portuguese.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Disque 100 (Human Rights Line)

Ministry of Human Rights and Citizenship

A free line, open 24 hours every day, for reporting human rights violations, including against disabled people.

Next step: Dial 100 free from any phone, or send a WhatsApp message to +55 61 99611-0100. There is also a video service in Libras (Brazilian Sign Language). In Portuguese.

Crisis and someone to talk to

CVV (Centro de Valorização da Vida)

Centro de Valorização da Vida

Emotional support and suicide prevention, 24 hours a day, from trained volunteers. Calls are free under an agreement with the Ministry of Health. You can also chat online.

Next step: Call 188. In Portuguese.
