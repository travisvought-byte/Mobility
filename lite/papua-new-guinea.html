Disability help in Papua New Guinea

Emergency in Papua New Guinea: call 112 (Police. Ambulance: 111; fire: 110).

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Disability Services Bill 2025 and National Policy on Disability

Department for Community Development and Religion

In July 2025 Parliament unanimously passed the Disability Services Bill 2025, a legal framework to protect the rights of persons with disabilities. The Department for Community Development and Religion led the groundwork and is putting it into practice with the United Nations. It follows the National Policy on Disability 2015–2025, the national plan for the disability sector. The official pages do not yet say how to register or apply for services.

Crisis and someone to talk to

1-Tok Kaunselin Helpim Lain

Listed by UNFPA and the United Nations in Papua New Guinea

Free phone counselling and referrals for anyone who is struggling and needs support, including people in a violent or abusive relationship. UNFPA says it is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and free to call from Digicel.

Next step: Call 7150 8000, free from Digicel, any hour. In an emergency, call 112 (police) or 111 (ambulance).
