Disability help in Dominica

Emergency in Dominica: call 999 (Ambulance, fire and police).

Disability payments and benefits

Invalidity Benefit

Dominica Social Security

One of Dominica Social Security’s long-term benefits, paid over an extended period to insured people with an invalidity; Social Security may ask its medical referee to assess the claimant’s condition. How much you get depends on the contributions you have paid and your average earnings, so it is not for people who have never paid in.

Next step: Ask Dominica Social Security: call the Roseau head office on 767-255-8311 or the Portsmouth office on 767-255-8350.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Bureau of Gender Affairs

Government of Dominica

The government office for gender affairs, which gives psychosocial support and works on the physical and emotional harm of gender-based violence, including abuse within the family. Its hours are not stated, so it may answer only in working hours. It is a government office, not a crisis line.

Next step: Call 767-266-3344 or 767-448-7855. The office is at the corner of Church Street and Victoria Street, Roseau. In an emergency, call 999.
