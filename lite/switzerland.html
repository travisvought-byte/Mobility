Disability help in Switzerland

Français

Emergency in Switzerland: call 112 (European emergency number, answered by the police control centre. Ambulance: 144. Fire: 118. Police: 117).

Where to start

Pro Infirmis social counselling

Pro Infirmis

Free counselling for disabled people and their relatives across Switzerland, covering housing, work, money and daily life, with a centre in every canton.

Next step: Choose your canton on the Pro Infirmis website to find the advice centre near you. In German, French or Italian, and in easy language.

Disability payments and benefits

Disability insurance (Invalidenversicherung IV / assurance-invalidité AI)

The IV office of your canton

Compulsory insurance for everyone living or working in Switzerland. It pays first for help to stay in or return to work, medical measures and aids such as a wheelchair or hearing aid; a pension if you are on average at least 40% unable to work for a year; a helplessness allowance if you need help with daily tasks; and an assistance contribution to help you keep living at home.

Next step: Register with the IV office of the canton where you live (cross-border workers: where you work) on the registration form. Do not wait: a late registration can cost you benefits. In German, French or Italian.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Procap legal advice on social insurance

Procap

Advice and legal support on disability insurance (IV), accident insurance, occupational pensions and supplementary benefits. It is free for Procap members.

Next step: Call the legal service, or find a regional advice centre on procap.ch. Non-members can ask about the terms. In German.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Die Dargebotene Hand / La Main Tendue (Tel 143)

Die Dargebotene Hand

An anonymous listening line open 24 hours a day for anyone who needs to talk. An English-language crisis line runs every evening.

Next step: Call 143, any hour; a call costs 20 to 70 centimes. In German, French or Italian. In English, call 0800 143 000, daily from 18:00 to 23:00.
