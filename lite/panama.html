Disability help in Panama

Español

Emergency in Panama: call 911 (Ambulance, fire and police. Ambulance and fire also 103; police also 104).

Where to start

Disability certification

National Secretariat for Disability (SENADIS)

The official certification of disability from the National Secretariat for Disability. You need the completed application form, a copy of your identity card or passport (a birth certificate and a parent's or guardian's identity card for a child) and a signed summary of your medical history on the form for your type of disability.

Next step: Download the forms from the Panamá Digital page and apply to SENADIS.

Disability payments and benefits

Ángel Guardián

Ministry of Social Development (MIDES)

A monthly cash payment from the Ministry of Social Development for people with severe disabilities who depend on others and live in extreme poverty. It was B/.80 a month in 2016; the current amount was not published on the pages checked. In February 2026, 136 more people joined it. MIDES adds people through its household surveys.

Next step: Ask your nearest MIDES office how to be considered.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Línea 147

Ministry of Social Development (MIDES)

A free emotional support line run by the Ministry of Social Development, with psychologists and social workers available 24 hours a day where anyone can talk about how they feel and ask for help.

Next step: Call 147 at any hour. In an emergency, call the emergency number at the top of this page.
