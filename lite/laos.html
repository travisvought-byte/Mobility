Disability help in Laos

Emergency in Laos: call 1195 (Ambulance (also 030 525 7239). Police: 1191; fire: 1190).

Equipment, home changes and getting around

Medical rehabilitation centres

Ministry of Health

The government told the UN disability committee in 2022 that it has set up five medical rehabilitation centres, with mobile clinics for remote areas, and works with international organisations to give assistive devices free of charge to persons with disabilities. It said persons with disabilities can use state health insurance, social security and social welfare, and that the Ministry of Labour and Social Welfare leads on disability.

Next step: Ask your local health service about the nearest medical rehabilitation centre.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Counselling and Protection Centre 1362

Lao Women's Union

The Lao Women's Union's Counselling and Protection Centre for women and children, which UNDP points people to if they have been subject to violence. Its hours and cost are not stated. It is a violence line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 1362. In an emergency, call the emergency number at the top of this page.
