Disability help in Iraq

العربية

Emergency in Iraq: call 911 (Police. Ambulance: 122; fire: 115).

Disability payments and benefits

Social Safety Net cash assistance

Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs

Cash assistance from the Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs’ Social Safety Programme for vulnerable groups in federal Iraq, including people with disabilities or special needs, who must show proof of their condition. UNHCR explains it for displaced people, returnees and refugees. Registering is free: never pay anyone to register.

Next step: Register on the ministry’s online survey (spa.gov.iq/umbrella) with your identity and residency papers and proof of disability. If you think you were wrongly left out, call the ministry’s line 1018.

Call 1018help.unhcr.org

Crisis and someone to talk to

Violence helpline 119 (Kurdistan Region)

General Directorate for Combating Violence Against Women, Kurdistan Region, listed by UNHCR

A line in the Kurdistan Region for anyone facing violence: women, girls, boys and men. It opened with UNFPA in 2018 as a 24-hour call centre where trained social workers and legal and psychosocial counsellors give support and counselling and refer callers to services near them. In the south and centre of Iraq, UNHCR says to call 911 for violence, child protection and mental health support. It is a violence line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: In the Kurdistan Region, call 119. Elsewhere in Iraq, use the emergency number at the top of this page.

Child protection line 116 (Kurdistan Region)

Listed by UNHCR

A line in the Kurdistan Region for child protection and for family and child-related support, including psychosocial help. Its hours are not stated. It is a children’s and family line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: In the Kurdistan Region, call 116.
