Disability help in Grenada

Emergency in Grenada: call 911 (Police and fire. Ambulance: 434 (St George's), 724 (Grenville), 774 (Carriacou)).

Disability payments and benefits

Invalidity benefit

National Insurance Scheme (NIS)

A National Insurance benefit for insured people under pensionable age (now 63) who are permanently unable to work because of a disease, illness or disablement that a doctor certifies. With at least 150 paid contribution weeks it is a monthly pension of 27% to 60% of average insurable earnings; with at least 50 weeks it is a one-off grant. It depends on contributions, so it is not for people who have never paid in.

Next step: Apply on the NIS web portal within three months of the doctor's diagnosis, or call NIS on 473-440-3309.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Domestic violence line 411

Royal Grenada Police Force, listed by the Government of Grenada

A short number, listed on the Government of Grenada's emergency services page, for reporting domestic violence to the police. Its hours are not stated. It is a violence line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 411. In an emergency, call the emergency number at the top of this page.
