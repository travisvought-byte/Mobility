Disability help in the Netherlands

Emergency in the Netherlands: call 112 (Police, fire brigade and ambulance).

Calling without your voice? Deaf, hard of hearing or speech-impaired: text 112 at any hour through the relay service, use the Tolkcontact app, or chat with the control room in the 112NL app. To text 112 directly, register once by texting “aanmelden” to 112.

Where to start

Support from your municipality (Wmo)

Your gemeente (municipality)

Under the Social Support Act (Wmo 2015), your municipality must help if you cannot manage on your own or take part in society and the people around you cannot help. Support can include a wheelchair for long-term use, regional transport, home adaptations such as a stairlift, household help and respite care. Some support has a personal contribution.

Next step: Contact your municipality. It works out with you what you need, usually in a conversation. You can take the support as a personal budget (pgb) and arrange it yourself. In Dutch.

Disability payments and benefits

Wajong benefit for young disabled people

UWV

Income for people who can never work because of an illness or disability they had at 18, or that began between 18 and 30 after at least six months of study in the year before. You must live in the Netherlands. UWV assesses whether you can work.

Next step: Apply to UWV. You cannot apply while living abroad. In Dutch.

Equipment, home changes and getting around

Disabled parking card (gehandicaptenparkeerkaart)

For people who have not been able to walk more than 100 metres on their own, even with a cane or rollator, for at least six months. There are cards for drivers and for passengers who need a driver’s help door to door. It is a European card, valid in all EU countries, for up to five years.

Next step: Apply to your municipality; a medical examiner decides. If you are not registered with a Dutch municipality, apply to the CBR. Each municipality sets its own fee. In Dutch.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Netherlands Institute for Human Rights (College voor de Rechten van de Mens)

College voor de Rechten van de Mens

The national human rights institute rules on discrimination complaints, including under the law on equal treatment on grounds of disability or chronic illness, for example when a needed adjustment is refused.

Next step: File a complaint on its complaint form, or call Monday to Friday, 10:00 to 16:00. In Dutch.

Crisis and someone to talk to

113 Suicide Prevention (113 Zelfmoordpreventie)

Stichting 113 Zelfmoordpreventie

Free, anonymous help around the clock for anyone thinking about suicide. It also offers help online.

Next step: Call 113. The service is in Dutch.
