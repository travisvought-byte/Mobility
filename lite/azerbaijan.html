Disability help in Azerbaijan

Emergency in Azerbaijan: call 103 (Ambulance. Police: 102; fire: 101).

Disability payments and benefits

Allowance for persons with disabilities

Ministry of Labour and Social Protection of Population

A monthly allowance for people with a disability determined by the state who are not entitled to a labour pension: 300 manat for an 81 to 100 percent loss of bodily functions, 250 manat for 61 to 80 percent and 180 manat for 31 to 60 percent. It is now mostly assigned automatically.

Next step: If it was not assigned automatically, apply at your local State Social Protection Fund office or a DOST centre with an identity document. Call centre: 142.

Equipment, home changes and getting around

Free wheelchairs, prostheses and rehabilitation aids

DOST Agency

People with disabilities get prosthetic and orthopaedic products, wheelchairs and other rehabilitation aids free of charge, paid for by the state, along with medicines, dentures and sanatorium treatment under Cabinet of Ministers rules.

Next step: Ask at a DOST centre, through Mobile DOST or on e-social.gov.az. Call centre: 142.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Children's Hotline and Women's Helpline 116111

State Committee for Family, Women and Children Affairs

A short number answered 24 hours a day for the Children's Hotline and the Women's Helpline, set up with the State Committee for Family, Women and Children Affairs. It gives psychological, emotional and social support to children and to women in crisis or facing violence, and refers callers to legal, medical and shelter services. It is not a general adult mental health line.

Next step: Call 116111 at any hour. In an emergency, call 102 for the police or 103 for an ambulance.
