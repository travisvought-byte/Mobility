Disability help in Cambodia

Emergency in Cambodia: call 119 (Ambulance. Police: 117; fire: 118).

Where to start

Disability Identification Card

Ministry of Social Affairs, Veterans and Youth Rehabilitation (MoSVY)

The ministry's Disability Identification Programme registers people with disabilities in a national database and gives them a card that opens access to health care, assistive devices and Family Package cash benefits. More than 360,000 people had been identified by January 2026.

Next step: MoSVY officials carry out disability screening and registration, often together with IDPoor checks; applications are checked and approved before a card is issued.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Cambodian Disabled People's Organisation (CDPO)

Cambodian Disabled People's Organisation

The national membership network of disabled people's organizations (56 member DPOs and federations, including Women with Disabilities Forums) that advocates for disability rights and keeps a directory of DPOs.

Next step: Call or email the Phnom Penh office (Mon-Fri 8:00-12:00, 13:30-17:00).

Crisis and someone to talk to

Child Helpline Cambodia 1280

Child Helpline Cambodia

UNICEF Cambodia lists this helpline among the non-governmental organisations that offer mental health support. It is a line for children; no general adult crisis line could be confirmed on an official page.

Next step: Call 1280. UNICEF's page does not give its hours or cost. In an emergency, call 119 for an ambulance or 117 for the police.
