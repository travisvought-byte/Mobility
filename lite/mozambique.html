Disability payments and benefits

Programa Subsídio Social Básico (Basic Social Subsidy)

Instituto Nacional de Acção Social (INAS)

Monthly cash transfers and psychosocial support from INAS for people with disabilities who cannot work, are poor and have no means of support. A health professional must certify the incapacity, and households are assessed with a poverty test. People with disabilities stay in the programme with periodic reassessment. A missing ID document does not stop you getting it.

Next step: Ask your community leader or the district INAS delegation to be put on the list of possible beneficiaries; INAS staff then visit your home to check eligibility.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Linha Fala Criança 116

Linha Fala Criança

Mozambique's child helpline, which UNICEF and the Ombudsman support and which opened a new office in Cabo Delgado in 2025. Children, and adults worried about a child, can call it. Its hours and cost are not stated. It is not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 116. In an emergency, call 112.
