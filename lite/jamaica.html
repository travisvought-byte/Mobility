Disability help in Jamaica

Emergency in Jamaica: call 119 (Police. For an ambulance or the fire service, dial 110).

Where to start

Register with the Jamaica Council for Persons with Disabilities (JCPD)

Jamaica Council for Persons with Disabilities

The government agency for people with disabilities. Registering is what opens its grants and services, and only registered members get benefits such as disability parking stickers and access to the share of National Housing Trust homes set aside for people with disabilities.

Next step: Register through the I Am Able: My JCPD app (Android), at the JCPD head office at 1C-1F Pawsey Road, Kingston 5, or with a social worker at a Ministry of Labour and Social Security parish office. Call 876-515-9069 or 876-447-0444, or email jcpd@jcpd.gov.jm.

Equipment, home changes and getting around

JCPD Assistive Aid and Economic Empowerment grants

Jamaica Council for Persons with Disabilities

Registered members, or their caregivers, can apply for help buying assistive aids such as wheelchairs, hearing aids, white canes and prostheses, and for grants to start a small business. In June 2026 the JCPD said assistive aid help runs up to J$300,000 and business grants from J$50,000 to J$250,000, depending on the need.

Next step: Register with the JCPD first, then apply in the I Am Able: My JCPD app or at a JCPD or parish office.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Discrimination complaints and the Disabilities Rights Tribunal

Jamaica Council for Persons with Disabilities

Under the Disabilities Act, 2014 (in force since 2022), you can complain to the JCPD about disability discrimination. The JCPD investigates and tries mediation; if that does not settle it, the case goes to the Disabilities Rights Tribunal, which decides it.

Next step: Make a written complaint to the JCPD on its complaint form, in person, by email to jcpd@jcpd.gov.jm, or in the I Am Able: My JCPD app. There is a time limit, so complain soon after it happens.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Mental Health and Suicide Prevention Helpline (888-NEW-LIFE)

Ministry of Health and Wellness

The Ministry of Health's free helpline for anyone in Jamaica who is struggling, including anyone thinking about suicide.

Next step: Call 888-NEW-LIFE (888-639-5433). It is toll-free.
