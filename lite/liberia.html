Disability help in Liberia

Emergency in Liberia: call 911 (Emergency. Calls may not be answered, so know the way to your nearest police station).

Where to start

National Commission on Disabilities (NCD)

Government of Liberia

The government body for disability, funded from the national budget, which advocates for persons with disabilities and steered the national action plan on disability. In 2019 the government reported three rehabilitation centres with trained technicians, and said a national register of persons with disabilities was still needed.

Next step: Ask the NCD about services and rehabilitation in your county.

Crisis and someone to talk to

National GBV 116 Call Center

Ministry of Gender, Children and Social Protection

A toll-free national line, opened in December 2024 with World Bank support, for survivors of gender-based violence and anyone reporting abuse of women and girls. Trained responders offer counselling in confidence and refer callers to the police, health facilities and social services. Its hours are not stated. It is a violence line, not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 116. In an emergency, call the emergency number at the top of this page.
