Disability help in Mexico

Emergency in Mexico: call 911 (Police, fire and medical emergencies across the country).

Disability payments and benefits

Pensión para el Bienestar de las Personas con Discapacidad Permanente

Secretaría de Bienestar

A federal pension paid every two months to people with a permanent disability. In states that signed agreements with the federal government it covers ages 0 to 64; elsewhere the age range is narrower. At 65, recipients move to the older adults' pension.

Next step: Registration opens at set times at Módulos del Bienestar (find yours at ubicatumodulo.bienestar.gob.mx). Bring a birth certificate, official ID, CURP, recent proof of address and, where your state asks for it, a disability certificate. For questions, call the Línea del Bienestar.

Call 800 639 4264gob.mx

Línea del Bienestar: weekdays 8:00 to 21:00, weekends and holidays 9:00 to 13:00

Equipment, home changes and getting around

DIF rehabilitation centres (CRI, CREE and UBR)

Sistema Nacional DIF and state and municipal DIF systems

The DIF family-services system runs rehabilitation centres across the country: Centros de Rehabilitación Integral (CRI), Centros de Rehabilitación y Educación Especial (CREE) and local Unidades Básicas de Rehabilitación (UBR). They offer therapy and rehabilitation for people with temporary or permanent disabilities, and many help with functional aids.

Next step: Ask your municipal or state DIF office for the nearest CRI, CREE or UBR. Services and costs are set by each state.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

CONAPRED: discrimination complaints

Consejo Nacional para Prevenir la Discriminación

The national council against discrimination. It takes complaints when a person, business or public office treats you unfairly because of a disability, and disability is one of the most common reasons people complain to it.

Next step: Call toll-free from outside Mexico City (in the city, 55 5262 1490), Monday to Thursday 9:00 to 17:30 and Friday 9:00 to 15:00, or file a complaint online.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Línea de la Vida

Comisión Nacional de Salud Mental y Adicciones (CONASAMA)

Mexico's free national line for anyone in emotional crisis, thinking about suicide, or dealing with addiction, open any hour.

Next step: Call 800 911 2000 from any phone in Mexico. The line is in Spanish.
