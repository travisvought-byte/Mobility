Disability help in Indonesia

Emergency in Indonesia: call 112 (The single emergency number joining police, ambulance and fire, brought in across districts and cities in stages).

Where to start

Ministry of Social Affairs command centre 171

Kementerian Sosial (Ministry of Social Affairs)

The ministry’s command centre number. Disabled people can call it to ask for help: in one case the ministry describes, a blind caller was assessed by a regional centre and given an adaptive cane and business capital under its ATENSI programme.

Next step: Call 171. The ministry’s page does not give hours or call costs.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Komnas HAM (Komisi Nasional Hak Asasi Manusia, National Human Rights Commission)

Komnas HAM

Indonesia's national human rights institution; it monitors and investigates human rights and has a complaints and mediation function.

Next step: File a complaint through the Komnas HAM online complaint portal (pengaduan.komnasham.go.id) or write to the commission.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Healing119 mental health line

Ministry of Health (Kementerian Kesehatan)

Free emotional support and basic consultation from licensed health workers, 24 hours a day, for Indonesian citizens. If you cannot get through, the lines may be busy.

Next step: Call 119 and choose extension 8, or use the call or WhatsApp button on the healing119.id website. No registration is needed.

Call 119

Choose extension 8.
