Disability help in Ukraine

Emergency in Ukraine: call 112 (The single emergency number, free of charge. Deaf people and people with speech impairments can text 112. You can also call 101 for the rescue service, 102 for the police and 103 for an ambulance).

Disability payments and benefits

State assistance for people disabled from childhood and children with disabilities

Pension Fund of Ukraine

A monthly payment for adults disabled since childhood, for as long as the disability lasts, and for disabled children under 18. It is set as a share of the subsistence minimum: 100% for group I, 80% for group II and 60% for group III, and 70% for a child. Refugees in Ukraine can apply too. The service is free and a decision takes 10 days.

Next step: Apply at a Pension Fund office, by registered post, or at your local administrative services centre (TsNAP). Diia's online version of the service showed as temporarily unavailable when checked. The Pension Fund's contact centre is 0 800 503 753. In Ukrainian.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights (Ombudsman)

Ombudsman of Ukraine

Takes appeals about human rights, including those of disabled people; its published cases include one where a disabled person's stopped assistance was recalculated and more than 50,000 hryvnias were paid after its check.

Next step: Call the free hotline 1678, Monday to Thursday 9:00 to 18:00 and Friday 9:00 to 16:45 (from abroad, +380 44 299 7408), or email hotline@ombudsman.gov.ua. The office warns that power cuts can briefly interrupt the line. In Ukrainian.

Crisis and someone to talk to

National hotline 116 123

La Strada-Ukraine

A free national hotline with psychological and legal consultations, for domestic violence, human trafficking and gender discrimination. La Strada-Ukraine also runs a free psychological support line for men, 2345, from 14:00 to 06:00, and a children and youth line, 0 800 500 225 or 116 111.

Next step: Call 0 800 500 335 from a landline or mobile, or 116 123 from a mobile. You can also write on Telegram. The organisation's page does not give the hours. In Ukrainian.

Call 0 800 500 335la-strada.org.ua
