Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Human Rights Ombudsman of Bosnia and Herzegovina

Institution of Human Rights Ombudsman of Bosnia and Herzegovina

Its Department for the Protection of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities takes complaints when a person's dignity or autonomy is at risk, when they are discriminated against or denied equal opportunities, or when they are kept from taking part in society. Filing a complaint cannot lead to any sanction against you.

Next step: Call the Sarajevo office on +387 33 666 006 or the Banja Luka head office on +387 51 303 992, or email sa.ombudsmen@ombudsmen.gov.ba. Offices: Dubrovačka 6, Sarajevo, and Akademika Jovana Surutke 13, Banja Luka.

Crisis and someone to talk to

SOS line 1265

Federal Ministry of Labour and Social Policy (Federation of BiH)

A free SOS line for victims of violence in the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, available 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Shelters and civil society organisations answer it, now with Federation funding. It is not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 1265 free at any hour. In an emergency, call 122 for the police or 124 for an ambulance.
