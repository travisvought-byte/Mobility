Disability help in Côte d'Ivoire

Emergency in Côte d'Ivoire: call 185 (Ambulance (SAMU). Police: 110, 111 or 170; fire: 180).

Disability payments and benefits

Pension d'invalidité (disability pension)

Caisse Nationale de Prévoyance Sociale (CNPS)

A monthly pension from the CNPS for salaried workers who have lost at least two-thirds of their capacity to work through illness or an accident that is not work-related, after at least 15 years of insured work. It is worked out like the retirement pension and paid at any age, without the early-retirement reduction.

Next step: File a claim with the CNPS as for a retirement pension (request form, work certificates, payslips, ID, bank details) and add a medical report confirming the disability. A CNPS medical adviser confirms the degree of disability.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Conseil National des Droits de l'Homme (CNDH) (National Human Rights Council)

Conseil National des Droits de l'Homme de Côte d'Ivoire

Receives complaints and reports of human rights violations and carries out non-judicial investigations into them; it has a complaints department (Département des requêtes) and a green line for reporting.

Next step: Call the Council or visit its office at Cocody 2 Plateaux Vallon, Rue J95, Abidjan.

Crisis and someone to talk to

143, Ministry of Health line

Ministère de la Santé, de l'Hygiène Publique et de la Couverture Maladie Universelle

The Ministry of Health's free, anonymous call centre (ligne verte). Callers in distress are put through to psychologists of the National Mental Health Programme (PNSM). The WHO reported in 2023 that it answers five days a week; daily hours are not stated. The state news agency reported it still running in August 2025.

Next step: Call 143. In an emergency, call 185 for an ambulance or 110, 111 or 170 for the police.
