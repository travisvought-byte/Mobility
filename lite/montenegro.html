Disability help in Montenegro

Emergency in Montenegro: call 112 (Emergency).

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Protector of Human Rights and Freedoms

Zaštitnik ljudskih prava i sloboda Crne Gore

Montenegro's ombudsman takes complaints when a state or other body has violated your rights, handles discrimination, and is the independent mechanism for the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities. The procedure is free.

Next step: Call +382 20 241 642, use the complaint form on the website, or visit Svetlane Kane Radević 3, Podgorica (reception Monday to Friday, 10:00 to 13:00). Complain within six months of learning of the violation, and within a year of it.

Crisis and someone to talk to

National SOS line for children and young people

Mladost Children's Home, Bijela

A free national SOS line for children and young people, answered by licensed professionals 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

Next step: Call 116 111 at any hour. Parents can call the National SOS Parent Helpline on 080 888 888. In an emergency, call 112.
