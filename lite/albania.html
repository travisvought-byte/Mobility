Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Commissioner for Protection from Discrimination

Komisioneri për Mbrojtjen nga Diskriminimi

Albania's equality body takes complaints about discrimination, including on grounds of disability, and keeps the information confidential.

Next step: Call the free green line 0800 0606, use the complaint form on its website, email info@kmd.al, or visit Rruga "Durrësit" Nr. 27, Tirana (offices also in Shkodër, Korçë and Fier).

Call 0800 0606kmd.alinfo@kmd.al

Crisis and someone to talk to

National Counselling Line 116 117

National Counselling Line for Victims of Domestic Violence

A 24-hour national line for women and girls facing domestic violence, running since February 2017. It is not a general mental health line.

Next step: Call 116 117 at any hour. In an emergency, call 112.

Call 116 117

ALO 116 111 child helpline

Albanian National Child Helpline

The national child helpline, supported by UNICEF since 2010, for children and young people. UNICEF does not give its hours.

Next step: Call 116 111. In an emergency, call 112.
