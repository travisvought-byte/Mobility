Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Human Rights Commission of Sri Lanka (HRCSL)

Human Rights Commission of Sri Lanka

Takes complaints from a victim or group of victims of a violation, or threatened violation, of fundamental rights, or from someone representing them.

Next step: Call the hotline 1996, or complain in person, by post, by email to secretary@hrcsl.lk, by fax or with the online form. Complaint forms are free from the head office (14 R. A. De Mel Mawatha, Colombo 4; 011 2505580), regional offices or the website. In Sinhala, Tamil or English.
