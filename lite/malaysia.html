Disability help in Malaysia

Emergency in Malaysia: call 999 (Police, fire and rescue, health and civil defence. Free).

Where to start

Register as a person with disabilities (OKU)

Social Welfare Department (JKM)

Malaysian citizens with a permanent physical, mental, learning, hearing, speech, vision or multiple disability, confirmed by a medical specialist, can register as OKU with the Social Welfare Department (JKM).

Next step: Get a report from a medical specialist at a government or private hospital or clinic. Then apply online through MyDaftar OKU, or take the BPPOKU form, the report and a copy of your MyKad (or a child's birth certificate) to a JKM counter.

Disability payments and benefits

PERKESO Invalidity Scheme

Social Security Organisation (PERKESO / SOCSO)

For insured employees who become permanently unable to earn a living because of an illness or injury from any cause, not only at work, as decided by a PERKESO Medical Board. It pays a monthly pension of 50% to 65% of average wages (at least RM475), RM500 a month more if you need someone with you at all times, and covers rehabilitation and aids such as wheelchairs, crutches, hearing aids and artificial limbs.

Next step: Ask PERKESO about making an invalidity claim.

Rights, discrimination and advocacy

Human Rights Commission of Malaysia (SUHAKAM)

SUHAKAM

Malaysia's national human rights commission. It takes complaints about human rights violations through its online complaint system.

Next step: Complain online, call the Kuala Lumpur office on weekdays, or email humanrights@suhakam.org.my.

Crisis and someone to talk to

Befrienders Kuala Lumpur

Befrienders KL

A free, private listening line, open 24 hours a day, every day, for anyone who feels lonely, in distress or is thinking about suicide.

Next step: Call 03-7627 2929.

Talian HEAL 15555

Ministry of Health Malaysia

The Ministry of Health's counselling line for emotional support and early crisis help, including for suicidal thoughts, depression, anxiety and stress. It is open every day from 8am to midnight.

Next step: Dial 15555. Your mobile carrier may charge for the call.
