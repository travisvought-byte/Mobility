Light Upon Light

Light Upon Light is a Washington nonprofit based in Everett. It works for dignity, respect, accessibility and equality for differently abled people through educational programs, advocacy, mentorship and community engagement.

If you live in Washington, or you want to bring disability-inclusion education to your own community, start with their website.
