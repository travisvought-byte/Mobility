The item

An original poster from The Power to Move: The Long Overdue Fight for Mobility Independence, the award-winning documentary about the Mobility Independence Foundation. It is signed by Thomas Quiter, the man on the poster and MIF’s founder, and by members of the film team.

Thomas was born with osteogenesis imperfecta (brittle bone disease). He spent five years unable to leave his home after insurance denied the wheelchair he needed. He raised the money himself, and that fight became MIF: a nonprofit that designs open-source wheelchairs and mobility equipment, shares the plans freely, and stands up for people’s right to repair what they depend on. You can watch the film on Prime Video.

How it works

  1. Bid by December 1. Bids must arrive by 11:59 p.m. Eastern on Giving Tuesday. Each bid must beat the current high bid by at least $10. If two bids are the same, the earlier one stands.
  2. The winner hears from us by December 3.
  3. The winner pays MIF directly. You give the amount of your winning bid straight to the Mobility Independence Foundation through MIF’s donation page and forward the receipt. Veteran Home Guardians never holds the money.
  4. We send you the poster. Pick it up near Marengo, Ohio, or we ship it to you in the U.S.

Because the winner receives a poster in return, the part of the gift that is tax deductible may be smaller than the bid. Ask a tax advisor. Veteran Home Guardians may decline a bid it cannot verify.

Not bidding? Give on Giving Tuesday anyway.

Whether or not you bid on the poster, please consider a Giving Tuesday gift to the Mobility Independence Foundation. A wheelchair design paid for once can be built, repaired and improved by anyone, anywhere. Gifts of any size help.

MIF takes card, bank, PayPal, Venmo, crypto, stock and donor-advised fund gifts. If your employer matches gifts, look for MIF on your company’s giving portal and ask whether your gift can be matched.
