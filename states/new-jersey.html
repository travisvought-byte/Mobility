Ramps & home modification

Programs that build, lend or pay for ramps, bathroom changes and other home access work.

Before you call: what to have ready and what to ask

Have ready

  • Whether you own or rent. If you rent, ask the landlord in writing before any work.
  • Photos of the entrance and a count of the steps, or the height from the ground to the door.
  • What the person uses to get around: wheelchair, scooter, walker or cane.
  • Household size and income, if the program is based on income.

Ask

  • Does the work have to be approved before it starts?
  • Is this a grant, a loan or a free build by volunteers?
  • Who handles permits and inspections?
  • Is there a waiting list, and how long is it?

These are general reminders. Each program sets its own rules, so ask what it needs.

NJ TRANSIT Access Link: ADA paratransit

NJ TRANSIT

Access Link is NJ TRANSIT’s shared-ride paratransit for people with disabilities who cannot use NJ TRANSIT buses or light rail. It picks up and drops off within three-quarters of a mile of an NJ TRANSIT bus or light rail route, during hours like those of the bus and light rail, for a fare similar to a regular bus fare.

Disability parking placard in New Jersey: how to apply

For: Mobility or physical disability

New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission (MVC)

In New Jersey, disability parking placards are issued by the New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission (MVC). Apply with the Application for Vehicle License Plates and/or Placard for Persons with a Disability (SP-41). Who can certify the disability: a medical practitioner, who also attaches a prescription or letterhead statement; a U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs representative for veterans rated 100% permanently disabled.

Next step: Open the agency’s page for the current form and have the medical part completed. Bring the form to a motor vehicle agency for same-day processing, or mail it to NJ Motor Vehicle Commission, Special Plate Unit, PO Box 015, Trenton, NJ 08666-0015. The medical certification must be dated within 60 days.

Good to know
  • Temporary placards are issued by your municipal chief of police, not the MVC: apply in person (form SP-68). They last six months, with one six-month renewal.
  • There is no fee for plates or a placard. Permanent placards and plates need a new medical certification every three years.
  • Forms, fees and rules change. Check the agency’s page before you apply.

Before you call: what to have ready and what to ask

Have ready

  • Your Social Security number and a photo ID.
  • Names, addresses and dates for your doctors, clinics and hospitals, and a list of your medicines.
  • Recent pay stubs, benefit letters and bank statements.

Ask

  • Which program fits my situation, and can I apply for more than one?
  • What is the deadline to appeal, and where do I send the appeal?
  • Will working or saving money change my benefits?
  • Who can help me fill out the forms at no cost?

USDA Rural Development, Mount Laurel state office

The USDA Section 504 Home Repair program gives loans to low-income and very-low-income homeowners in eligible rural areas to repair, improve or modernize their homes, and grants to very-low-income homeowners age 62 and older to remove health and safety hazards. New Jersey Housing and Mortgage Finance Agency, with the Department of Human Services

For: Children, youth and families

Early Intervention System; Division of Family Health Services; Department of Health

The early intervention program in New Jersey serves children from birth to age three who have a developmental delay or disability. Parents do not have to wait for a referral, and the evaluation is free.
