Choose a state or U.S. territory, then print it (front and back) or save it as a PDF. Hand it to a client, a neighbor, a patient or a family member. Phone numbers come first because many people do not have internet at home.

Free or low-cost help for people with mobility and other disabilities. Call first and ask what you need to bring.

Anywhere in the United States

Need help now? Thinking about suicide, or in any crisis: call or text 988, any time. Veterans: dial 988, then press 1. No safe place to sleep tonight: dial 211 and press 6 for homeless services, or use HUD’s Find Shelter tool. Veterans: 1-877-424-3838, 24 hours a day.
