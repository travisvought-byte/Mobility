MOBILITY TALK · PARTNER WITH US

Help more people find the help they need.

Mobility Talk is free for everyone. If you have an audience, a clinic, a congregation, a podcast, a business, or just a few people who could use this, here are five simple ways to partner with us.

  • Free for visitors
  • No paid placement in results
  • Nothing you type is saved or sent

1. Share it (free, for anyone)

Send someone the page for their state. They see programs, rules and a message they can edit, with no sign-in.

Know a business or organization that would sponsor? Introduce them and you receive a share of what they pay. You do not have to be an employee, and anyone can apply. The terms are in writing before you start, and the share and terms will be posted on this page before the first partner begins.
