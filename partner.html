Advertising

Advertising is separate from the nonprofit sponsorships above. An ad is a paid message from a business or organization, shown on the site and labeled as an ad. It is not a gift and it is not a thank-you.

Who can advertise

Businesses and organizations whose work helps the people who use this site: mobility equipment and repair, accessible travel, home modification, accessible vehicles, disability services and the like.

Ground rules

  • Every ad is labeled “Advertisement.”
  • Ads are kept apart from program listings and resource lists, never placed inside them.
  • Ads never change what is listed or the order it is listed in, and an ad never gets anyone listed.
  • No ads on crisis or emergency help.
  • Every ad is reviewed before it runs.
  • We turn down ads that are misleading and ads aimed at people in crisis.

How ads affect privacy is on the privacy page.

How we handle this

  • The directory is free to use. No paywalls and no sign-in.
  • No paid placement. Sponsors are thanked here, never mixed into program results.
  • Ads are labeled “Advertisement,” kept apart from the directory and never change what is listed.
  • No commissions. Nobody is paid a percentage of gifts or sponsorships, and partners are thanked in public instead.
  • Gifts and sponsorships go to a 501(c)(3) nonprofit.
