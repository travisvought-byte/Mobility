Help more people find the help they need.

Mobility Talk is free for everyone. If you have an audience, a clinic, a congregation, a podcast, a business, or just a few people who could use this, here are simple ways to help.

  • Free for visitors
  • No paid placement in results
  • No commissions
  • Nothing you type is saved or sent

1. Share it or print it (free, for anyone)

Send someone the page for their state. They see programs, rules and a message they can edit, with no sign-in. Or print a help list with phone numbers to hand to a client, patient, neighbor or family member.

Caregivers, peer mentors, clinic and agency staff, and anyone who walks a neighbor through the guide are the reason it works. Tell us what you are doing. With your permission we thank you here and send you new state updates first.
